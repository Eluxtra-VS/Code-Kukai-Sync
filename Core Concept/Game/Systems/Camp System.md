- By using a proximity prompt near a designated camp spot a player can spawn in a camp site that serves as a home and it would be portable with a 1 minute pack-away timer
- your house could have storage in which you could place items that would stay there when you leave and join the game
- You can then upgrade your home for extra necessities such as crafting, storage, refinery, etc. 
- You can rest at your camp to heal
  - **Rest limits (locked 2026-09-28):** resting heals only **Healthy-State** damage (the "recover in 60 seconds" tier). It does nothing in Knocked/Critical/Lucid states — those follow the [[Health System]] timers and revival rules. Rest cannot substitute for revival; it only speeds the walk home after one.
