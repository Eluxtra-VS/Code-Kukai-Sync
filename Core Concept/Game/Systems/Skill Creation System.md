# Mutation

## How it works
When you create a move, it wont come out how you envisioned it / exactly how you inputted your points

this is called Mutation and it allows for the next process called Adaptation

# Adaptation

## How it works

When you move is mutated and changed, some things simply cannot be changed like the color and the properties, but most of the attributes might be off

and so the way this would work is the more you use your ability, the more MASTERY it gets.
the more mastery, the closer it gets to the move you originally wanted.
During this process, the enemies you go against also allow your move to change if you commonly go against them - so if you constantly go against enemies with Armor, your ability would gain the property **Piercing**

these properties that can only be obtained through adaptation are called "Unique Properties" and they save for your next move, so when you want to create another move, you would be able to select Piercing as a property.

You can then further enhance Adaptation through your Reiva and the Rei-Buds - As if you choose to train with your Reiva - it can give you pointers depending on your move, so if it is very Rei inefficient then your Reiva will notify you, maybe subtly but it will

# Upgrades

## How it works
You will notice that after complete mastery of your move, you gain Upgrade Points.
These points allow you to apply them to your move after they reach a certain point - and would allow you to upgrade some of the attributes, and would naturally upgrade the **Level** of your ability.

The Level of your ability matters a lot and ill explain why.

The way Levels in abilities work is like this:

If you make 2 exact moves (nearly statistically impossible)
at different points (One at Level 1 and another at level 10)

The Level 10 ability will NATURALLY be stronger and therefore would deal more damage at full Output
and the Level 1 Ability would then cost less Rei for more Output, meaning using it at 100% wouldn't cost as much Rei

BUT if you upgrade the Ability made at Level 1 twenty times, it would be considered much stronger then the ability made at Level 10
