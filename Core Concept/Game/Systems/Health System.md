# Stages

**1**: Healthy State:
- your health isn't zero and you aren't in any immediate danger
**2**: Knocked State:
- Occurs when Health reaches zero in Healthy State
- Limited mobility and incapable of fighting back
- People are able to steal from you during this state(they are restrained during)
- Able to be restrained
- You may be carried
- You will recover in this state in 60 seconds
**3**: Critical State:
- Occurs when continuous damage is taken in Knocked State
- Complete inability to move
- You may be carried
- You will eventually go to the Lucid State with no medical attention
- You can be gripped in this state
- Speech is inhibited
4: Lucid State:
- Last chance of possibility of revival 
- You can choose to give up and die instantly by holding the button for 3 seconds
- A final maximum of 20 seconds to be revived 
- Being revived in this state will take you to critical state, but your assuming that someone will have healing
- Chat gets disabled
5: Death:
- You can no longer be revived
- Any items carried are deleted upon death
  - **Relic exception (locked 2026-09-28):** [[Relic|Relics]] are indestructible — they are never "deleted." A Relic on a dead player drops at the death location as a world object, lootable by anyone (it outlives the wipe). This preserves both rules: inventory deletion, Relic permanence. Relic-theft on death is therefore possible — and is intended as a high-risk/high-reward driver of the Calamity economy.
- You CANNOT steal from their body
- You are wiped
