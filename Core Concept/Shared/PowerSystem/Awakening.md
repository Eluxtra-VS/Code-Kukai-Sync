## Short Definition

**Awakening** is the process by which a dormant [[Reiva]] becomes active and the human host begins to access supernatural ability. It is as much a psychological event as a supernatural one.

---

## Typical Steps

### 1 — Catalyst

Usually one of two things:

- **An emotionally extreme event** — trauma, loss, intense resolve, a moment that forces confrontation.

### 2 — Pressure Event

An intense emotional or existential moment that demands a response. The host cannot avoid it. Something must give.

### 3 — Reiva Activation

The Reiva _chooses_ to awaken. It has been watching. When it judges the host is ready — or when Pressure is so high that waiting becomes dangerous — it begins sharing control and [[Rei]].

### 4 — First Resolution

The host performs the first meaningful action to channel or release their Pressure. This moment determines path:

|Response|Path|
|---|---|
|Externalise — release outward|[[Akarui]]|
|Internalise — self-correct, modify|[[Weaver]]|
|Displace — redirect into objects|[[Reiki]]|

 Your desires when pressure builds up determines your **Affinity** — the optimal [[Classes|Class]] expression that suits the host's psychological and physical makeup.

---

## Notes

- Early Awakening is volatile. First uses are almost always [[Bond Stages|Unstable]] until the bond begins to strengthen.
- The Reiva often waits for emotional maturity. A host with raw power but no internal groundwork may carry their Reiva for years before it stirs. (See: [[Kaito Amori]].)
- Rejecting the resolution — refusing to commit to any path — risks immediate [[Kukai]] transformation.

## In-Game Version

In the game version of Code:Kukai, the awakening process will be a bit different.
To begin, you must complete a few tasks to become a [[Kyumin]], which has the potential to awaken.

1. You must begin by getting all negative emotions to 100%.
This is soft capped at 90% in majority of cases. To gain further negative emotions, you must go to a location which invokes one emotion further.

- Rage - Defaced Statue.
- Sorrow - Graveyard.
- Fear - Haunted House.
Upon reaching 100% in one of these emotions, a small indicator will pop up and, while vague, encourage the player to keep seeking out these places.

2. You must then get 100% for each of the positive emotions.
This again is soft capped at 90% but can gain more if in the correct location.

- Bravery - Statue of a Hero.
- Joy - Polished Shrine.
- Tranquillity - Garden of flowers.
Once you reach 100%, there will be more small indicators.

3. You must reach lucid state while still surviving.
Once you complete this final task, it will mention at the top of your screen the following.

> _***"You feel like you've experienced a lot of emotional pressure lately"***_

This indicates you are now a [[Kyumin]] and have the potential to awaken.
There is now one final part to awaken to your [[Reiva]].

4. You must experience the final **[[Pressure]] Event**.
This is a random event that can occur to any [[Kyumin]] that allows them to awaken.
The event occurs as such.

- Your [[Reiva]] will be visible in front of you, by getting close to it, you trigger the rest of the event.
You will then be told the following.

>_***"You feel immensely in danger coming from the being in front of you, going to it could mean death."***

You will then have 2-3 options to pick from.

1. Approach the being. ([[Akarui]])
2. Flee to prepare. ([[Weaver]])
If the player has a bond with an item over a certain amount, you will unlock a third choice.
3. Prepare your {item name}. ([[Reiki]])

If you pick any of the options, you will awaken and become a different type based on the option you picked.

Failure to choose in a time limit of 15 seconds will end the event and you will not awaken.
