# Summoning
## Explanation

**Summoning** is a high level technique that any awakened person can learn, regardless of type. It allows a person's Reiva to transfer its consciousness into another body for a time, letting it and its host fight side by side. It relies on two primary components for a successful summoning.

1. A Vessel for the Reiva to inhabit.
2. The [[Transference Technique]]
   
The **Vessel** the Reiva can inhabit must be created thorugh its own technique as well, by combining both techniques, a person could fight alongside their Reiva as two separate beings.

Reiva do not know how to fight or move around in the beginning and it can take a while to train them for combat. However, there can be a lot of benefits to teaching them combat.

### Teaching Reiva

Reiva are very impressionable naturally, so when teaching them, they learn by example in a lot of cases, this means you can make your Reiva be more biased towards different actions, such as special attacks, physical attacks or blocking.
