# Pressure

## Short Definition

**Pressure** is the internal emotional tension a person accumulates over time — the sum of unprocessed feelings, obligations, guilt, desire, fear, longing, and habits of suppression. In Code: Kukai, Pressure is the seed that drives [[Reiva]] activity, shapes how [[Rei]] behaves, and determines the psychological path a human takes after [[Awakening]].

> _The [[Code]] was supposed to protect people from this. Most people are following it wrong._

---

## How Pressure Accumulates

Pressure builds through lived experience — it is not measured in numbers in-story, but can be thought of as **low / rising / critical** for scene design purposes.

Sources of Pressure include:

- Trauma and loss.
- Repeated suppression of genuine emotion.
- Social expectations of silence and endurance.
- Unresolved conflict and guilt.
- Grief that was never allowed to be grief.

Pressure does not announce itself. It is the thing people carry without naming.

---

## Pressure Tendency

Every person develops a **tendency** — the psychological pattern they fall into under stress:

| Tendency        | Expression                                   | Path       |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| **Externalise** | Release outward, act, project                | [[Akarui]] |
| **Internalise** | Self-correct, self-blame, modify             | [[Weaver]] |
| **Displace**    | Redirect into objects, environment, distance | [[Reiki]]  |

The first meaningful response after Awakening reveals which tendency is dominant. It is not a choice — it is a reflex.

---

## Pressure and Luz

- Pressure is the emotional source that converts into [[Rei]] when a [[Reiva]] bonds and the host acts.
- **Calm, acknowledged feelings → clean, efficient Luz.**
- **Suppressed, mislabelled feelings → chaotic Luz** — wastes energy, causes unpredictable backlash, reduces precision.

> _Kaito Amori mistakes jealousy for anger, longing for irritation. Every mislabelled emotion makes his Rei flicker and waste. He has the raw output. He doesn't have the clarity yet._

---

## Pressure States (Gameplay / Design)

|State|Mechanical Effect|
|---|---|
|**Low**|Luz is stable; bond functions cleanly|
|**Rising**|Luz becomes slightly volatile; abilities cost more|
|**Critical**|High damage variance; bond instability risk; [[Kukai]] susceptibility increases significantly|

## Recovery

Pressure reduces through:

- **Rest and honest emotional processing** — the fastest recovery.
- **Confession scenes and successful small resolutions** — meaningful beats that clear accumulated load.
- **[[Bond Stages|Bonding]] exercises and rituals** — reduce Pressure permanently over time.
- **[[Awakening]]** — the first resolution event is itself a massive Pressure discharge, which is why it's so volatile.

Pressure does **not** reduce through:

- Suppression — this masks it without resolving it.
- Distraction — the load stays.
- Winning a fight — violence clears nothing unless paired with genuine emotional acknowledgment.

---

## The Cultural Trap

The world Kaito lives in prizes silent endurance. "Don't show it. Push through." This is treated as strength.

The [[Code]] — misread by most people — reinforces this. People believe suppression equals discipline. What they are actually doing is **compressing Pressure inward** until it has nowhere to go.

The real Code says the opposite: suppressing is the weakness. Processing is the discipline. Kaito's discovery of this is a major turning point — not just philosophically, but mechanically, because it directly affects how cleanly his [[Rei]] runs.

---

## Narrative Uses

- **Inciting scene:** Kaito witnesses Yoshi's collapse and misreads the lesson. He turns fear of becoming unacceptable inward, and the resulting self-correction triggers his partial, misdirected Awakening. This is the locked Arc 1 application of Pressure.
- **Slow-burn arc:** A character with chronic suppressed Pressure (Hitori Tanaka's baseline resilience, Aoki Sora's containment) slowly accumulates [[Kukai]] risk without dramatic warning signs.
- **World-building:** A culture that praises silent endurance is a culture manufacturing Kukai. The social world is quietly, systemically dangerous.
