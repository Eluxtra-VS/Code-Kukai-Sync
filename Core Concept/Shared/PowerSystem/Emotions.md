## Short Definition

**Emotions** are the six core emotional states that power [[Rei]]. Positive Emotions fuel Luz. Negative Emotions fuel Kuro. Both sides affect combat, bond stability, and the risk of [[Kukai]] transformation.

> _"Your power is not separate from what you feel. It is what you feel."_

---

## The Six Emotions

### Luz Emotions (Positive)

|Emotion|Nature|Combat Role|
|---|---|---|
|**Tranquility**|Calm, settled clarity|Powers sustained Luz defenses; improves Luz efficiency|
|**Joy**|Warmth, connection, hope|Powers cooperative and restorative abilities; strengthens [[Bond Stages|bond]]|
|**Bravery**|Resolve, courage, will|Powers Luz offensive bursts and high-risk techniques|

### Kuro Emotions (Negative)

|Emotion|Nature|Combat Role|
|---|---|---|
|**Rage**|Heat, aggression, drive|Powers strongest Kuro offensive output|
|**Sorrow**|Grief, loss, heaviness|Powers sustained Kuro drain and emotional debuffs|
|**Fear**|Dread, avoidance, paralysis|Powers Kuro destabilisation — reduces enemy Luz control|

---

## How Emotions Work in Combat

Each ability is linked to:

1. **An Emotion** (one of the six above).
2. **An Affinity** ([[Classes]]: Vigor, Pulse, Forge).

Mastery of an ability determines how deeply it manipulates the target's emotional state.

---

### Getting Hit by a Kuro Ability (Fear-Correlated)

|Mastery Level|Effect on Target|
|---|---|
|**Low**|Target's **Bravery is reduced**|
|**Mid**|Target's **Bravery is reduced** + **Fear is increased**|
|**High**|All positive emotions reduced (Bravery most severely) + **Fear is significantly increased**|

### Getting Hit by a Luz Ability (Bravery-Correlated)

|Mastery Level|Effect on Target|
|---|---|
|**Low**|Target's **Bravery is reduced**|
|**Mid**|Target's **Bravery is reduced even more significantly**|
|**High**|All positive emotions reduced, Bravery most severely|

> **Note:** Even Luz attacks reduce Bravery in the target — being overwhelmed by power, even positive power, erodes confidence. The difference is that Kuro attacks _also install_ the corresponding negative emotion, creating a double effect.

---

## Emotion Levels & Their Effects

### High Positive Emotions → Luz Benefits

- Higher Tranquility → Cleaner, more efficient Luz (less waste, better control).
- Higher Joy → Bond strengthens faster; cooperative techniques cost less.
- Higher Bravery → Unlocks high-risk Luz techniques; increases burst output.

### High Negative Emotions → Kuro Consequences

When a character's **negative emotions** (Rage, Sorrow, Fear) are elevated:

- Their Kuro output **increases in potency**.
- **Enemy [[Luz]] control is reduced** — their defenses weaken.
- **Enemy [[Pressure]] is increased** — they become more susceptible to bond instability.
- The user themselves becomes more vulnerable to Pressure accumulation and corruption.

> **The trap:** High negative emotions make you hit harder, but you are also consuming yourself. This is the mechanical expression of the Kukai risk.

## Emotion Interactions: Full Table

|Emotion Triggered|Opposed Emotion|Cascade at High Mastery|
|---|---|---|
|Fear (Kuro)|Bravery (Luz)|All positive reduced; Fear installed|
|Rage (Kuro)|Tranquility (Luz)|Tranquility collapsed; Rage spreads|
|Sorrow (Kuro)|Joy (Luz)|Joy drained; Sorrow lingers|
|Bravery (Luz)|Fear (Kuro)|Bravery reduced (overwhelm); no negative installed|
|Tranquility (Luz)|Rage (Kuro)|Rage suppressed (temporarily)|
|Joy (Luz)|Sorrow (Kuro)|Sorrow lightened; bond reinforced|

## Emotion Recovery

- **Rest and honest emotional processing** restore positive emotions faster than time alone.
- **[[Bonding]] exercises and rituals** can stabilise emotion levels permanently.
- **Suppression** (see [[Code]] and [[Pressure]]) actively prevents recovery — it masks emotion levels without resolving them.
- **Forced Kuro use** without processing leaves residual negative emotion that compounds.

---

## Narrative Use

- Emotion levels are never shown as numbers in-story — they manifest physically and behaviourally.
    - A character with low Bravery hesitates, second-guesses, moves slower.
    - A character with high Rage hits harder but loses precision.
    - A character with high Tranquility is eerily calm and efficient in combat.
- Kaito Amori is an example of someone who **misidentifies** his emotions — mistaking Sorrow for Rage, Longing for Anger — which produces chaotic, wasteful Luz/Kuro output.
