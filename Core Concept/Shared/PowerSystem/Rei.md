# Rei

## Short Definition

**Rei** is the fundamental spiritual lifeforce of a Reiva — and by extension, the shared lifeforce of a bonded host. It is not an ability or a combat resource on its own. It is the raw essence that sustains both entities and makes everything else possible.

Rei splits into two expressions depending on how it is channelled:

| Expression | Alignment | Fuel |
|---|---|---|
| **Luz** | Positive | Tranquility, Joy, Bravery |
| **Kuro** | Negative | Rage, Sorrow, Fear |

> _Think of Rei as the river. Luz and Kuro are the two directions it can flow._

---

## Reserves

The reserve system is two pools. Every ability draws from Reserve. Pushing past empty Reserve into Core Reserve is the catastrophic choice.

| Reserve          | Function                                                                                                            |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Core Reserve** | The minimum Rei required to sustain the Reiva bond. Drawing from this is dangerous. Reaching zero is fatal to both. |
| **Reserve**      | Expendable Rei for all ability use. Standard combat resource. Regenerates through rest and emotional processing.    |

There is no third pool. The distinction is simple: Reserve is what you spend. Core Reserve is what you are. Spending what you are has consequences that do not regenerate.


---

## Rei and Emotional State

Because Rei channels into Luz or Kuro based on emotional state, a person's internal condition directly determines what kind of energy they produce:

- Calm, resolved emotional clarity → clean, efficient Luz.
- Rage, grief, or fear → precise, immediate Kuro.
- Suppressed, mislabelled, or conflicting emotions → unstable, wasteful output from either channel.

This is why Pressure management is not philosophy. It is the literal condition under which Rei functions correctly.

---

## Rei and the Bond

- If the Reiva's Rei is completely drained, the host dies.
- Destroying the host places the Reiva in immediate danger.
- A strong bond improves Rei efficiency — less wasted, both channels run cleaner.
