## Short Definition

**Progression** describes how a host's relationship with their [[Reiva]] evolves, and how their resulting path — [[Akarui]], [[Weaver]], or [[Reiki]] — deepens in power, control, and psychological cost.

> Progression is not time-based. It is earned through bond strength, emotional clarity, lived experience, and narrative milestones.

---

## The Decision Moment

After [[Awakening]], the **first meaningful way the host resolves [[Pressure]]** defines their path:

|Response to Pressure|Path|
|---|---|
|**Externalise** — release outward, project, defend|[[Akarui]]|
|**Internalise** — self-correct, modify the body|[[Weaver]]|
|**Displace** — redirect into objects, environment|[[Reiki]]|

This is not a conscious choice. It is a psychological reflex revealed under pressure.

## Akarui Path — External

> **Naming note (locked 2026-09-28):** Akarui progression uses the Bond Stage names (Unstable → Zanshin → Union) *by design, not by omission*. The External path has no separate stage vocabulary because an Akarui's growth IS their bond maturing — there is no internal Thread architecture (Weaver's Loom/Seam/Unravel) or Anchor discipline (Reiki's Connection/Hinan/Blame) developing on a second axis. One axis, one set of names. See [[Bond Stages]].

> _"Their power faces outward. So do they."_

## Unstable:
### Stage 1 
- Random headaches that don't apply in combat that have a custom animation and usually last 30 seconds with the first one being an exception that last 1 minute and has camera/lighting effects
### Stage 2 
- Basic Rei Control with an Unstable Output Percentage
- This is when the black box test is usually possible for people

## Zanshin:
### Stage 1

- Able to control your Rei
- Able to control your Output Percentage
- Able to build your own skills 
### Stage 2 

- Signature expression becomes legible through the host's affinity, Pressure pattern, and bond development; the host may later name or refine it, but does not simply choose an unrelated signature from a menu
- Flicker is able to be learned
- Perception 
### Stage 3

- You gain access to the Oscuro
- You have the capability to learn Summoning and Fusion

## Union:
### Stage 1 

- You've learned your Reiva's Mark
- That then allows for use of the Transference technique
- Possible to Summon 
### Stage 2

- Fusion
### Stage 3

- Kaiju 
- This is when you start to gain mastery of the Transference Technique

## Reiki Path — Displacement

> _"Their power doesn't live in them. It lives in what they carry."_

### Connection

First contact. Objects barely respond. Every use is a deliberate investment.

- Single object, requires sustained touch.
- High feedback burn on unfamiliar items.
- Basic intent only: move, hold, resist.

### Hinan

_Refuge._ The user retreats into their Anchors — objects become the emotional shelter they struggle to find in people.

- Multiple Anchors (3–5) controlled simultaneously.
- Range opens up — touch no longer required for seeded objects.
- Affinity begins bleeding through Anchors weakly.
- Power grows alongside emotional dependency. Both are real.

### Blame

_Peak mastery. Peak cost._ The user has projected their emotional weight onto their Anchors. The objects carry what the user cannot — grief, guilt, longing.

- Anchors respond to emotional state alone; explicit control is no longer needed.
- Full affinity channeling through Anchors (weaker than direct Akarui use, but precise).
- Destroying a Blame-stage Reiki's Anchors is psychologically devastating — the objects _hold_ something that has nowhere else to go.
- Highest [[Drift]] saturation risk.

_(See [[Reiki]] for full mechanical detail.)_

## Weaver Path — Internal

> _"Their power faces inward. So does everything else."_

### Loom

The Thread is new. Every use tears something. The cost is immediate and brutal.

- Harsh physical debuffs after every use: numbness, motor loss, blurred vision, cognitive strain.
- Power is real but punishing — each use is a sacrifice.
- The Weaver is learning which body part they've chosen to break and rebuild.

### Seam

The Thread is familiar. The cost is managed, not eliminated.

- Debuffs reduced but persistent — cannot be ignored.
- The Weaver has learned to pace themselves.
- Emotional arcs here: beginning to question _why_ they chose the body part they did.

### Unravel

Full Thread power. Full psychological reckoning.

- The Thread operates at maximum efficiency.
- Pushing past Unravel's limits risks the Thread consuming the body part entirely — permanent, catastrophic damage.
- The Weaver's arc lands here: they either learn to stop fuelling their power with self-blame, or they lose themselves to it.

_(See [[Weaver]] for full mechanical detail.)_

## Cross-Path Failure

Any path, at any stage, can collapse into [[Kukai]] transformation if:

- [[Pressure]] is never resolved and accumulates past a critical threshold.
- The [[Bond Stages|bond]] breaks catastrophically without recovery.
- The user draws on [[Kuro]] without the emotional groundwork to contain it.

Progression forward is never guaranteed. Regression is always possible.
