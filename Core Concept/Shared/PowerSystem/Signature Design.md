# Signature Design — Pressure → Ability Framework

## Short Definition

**Signature Design** is the method for creating or revising any named ability in Code: Kukai. Its one rule: **class comes from desire, not function.** A power's surface — what it does — can be anything. Its class must come from the wound underneath — what the character actually wants and cannot admit.

Every signature is a pressure profile expressed as power. It must contain the coping (the way the character avoids naming the wound) **and** the seed of resolution (what honest processing would unlock). If a signature only mirrors the coping, it describes the character's flaw without offering them a way out — it is a description, not a story.

> _The power system IS the metaphor. A signature that does not contain both the trap and the way out is not finished._

---

## The Worksheet — 8 Steps

Use this for every new or revised ability. Do not skip steps. Do not reverse their order.

### 1. Core Wound
The belief they suppress, stated as a first-person claim:
> "I am the problem." / "I am not enough." / "If I had been better, this would not have happened." / "Being seen incomplete is failure."

### 2. Desire
What they actually want underneath the coping — the thing they cannot let themselves want:
> Mel wants to be seen. Sora wants her hands to be enough to help. Kaito wants to be seen as complete. Jakar wants genuine contact.

### 3. Coping Behavior
What they do instead of naming the desire:
> Mel redirects care outward. Sora takes ownership of every break. Kaito revises himself before anyone can judge. Jakar manufactures suffering to strip masks.

### 4. Class — the desire, mapped
The class is the desire given shape by Rei. **Ask: what does this character want?** Not: what does their power do?

| Class     | The Desire Underneath                                                      | Question to Ask                                   |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Vigor** | To **be enough** — the self is insufficient and must be made more          | "Am I enough?"                                    |
| **Pulse** | To **reach** — a gap must be closed; something of the self must go outward | "How do I close the gap?"                         |
| **Forge** | To **build** — make something real that cannot be dismissed                | "What can I make that is undeniable?"             |
| **Veil**  | To **control** — cannot tolerate entering situations unmanaged             | "Can I make the situation mine before it begins?" |

### 5. Path — the reflex, not the choice
Determined by the first resolution of Pressure after Awakening: **Externalise** → Akarui, **Internalise** → Weaver, **Displace** → Reiki. This is a psychological reflex, not a class choice. Class and Path are independent axes: a Weaver can be Vigor, Pulse, Forge, or Veil.

### 6. Signature — the class at its deepest personal expression
The signature is what the class looks like when it belongs to exactly one person. For a Weaver, the Thread is the body part where the wound lives. For a Reiki, the Anchor is the object that carries the displacement. For an Akarui, the expression goes outward.

### 7. Fix — what honest processing unlocks
What the ability becomes when the character stops suppressing. This is the arc's resolution written into the mechanic. If you cannot name this, the character has no arc.

### 8. Worse — what suppression deepens
What the ability costs more of the longer the wound stays unprocessed. Every signature needs a Worse column. **A signature without a Worse column is overpowered.**

## The Condition Layer (universal)

Conditions are **not** Veil's property. Any signature may carry a condition. The condition is a **perk, amplifier, or stabilizer**: the ability works without it; the condition makes it *better*, *cleaner*, or *more stable*.

| Condition Role | What It Does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Amplifier** | Condition met → the ceiling rises | Beat-Break: honest rhythm → stacks build |
| **Stabilizer** | Condition met → output stays clean | Shackles: sincerity → constructs hold |
| **Mode Gate** | Condition met → a specific mode unlocks | Mend → Unmake: the destructive mode is gated behind self-blame |

A condition on a non-Veil signature should never be the *entire* mechanism. If removing the condition makes the ability cease to exist, it is a Veil — classify it as such.

---

## Veil — The Pure Form

**Veil is the only place where the condition is the mechanism.** The effect **does not exist until the condition is met**, and once met it is **absolute within its defined scope** — unblockable, because you already walked into it.

> You can shield against a beam. You cannot shield against eye contact you already made, smoke you already inhaled, or a guilt you already carry.

Good Veils: Daiki's eye contact (nothing happens until eye contact; then control), Dante's Judgement (nothing happens until intentional harm + present guilt; then consequence), Kashi's Fracture (nothing happens until active suppression; then the suppression is the opening), Niko's Rapt (nothing happens until the name is spoken; then permanent extraction).

**The balance rule:** the easier the condition is to satisfy, the narrower the effect must be. A Veil whose condition is trivially met is a Veil violating its own class. (This was the Faultline problem — fixed in [[Faultline]].)

## The Fit Tests

Before accepting any signature, run three tests:

1. **The Swap Test** — could this ability belong to another character without changing its meaning? If yes, it is not personal enough. Rewrite it.
2. **The Class Test** — does the class come from the desire (Step 4), not the function? Sora's Mending *functions* as Forge but her desire is Vigor. Function follows class, never the reverse.
3. **The Resolution Test** — does the signature contain both Fix and Worse? If it only contains one, it describes a character instead of telling one.

## Worked Example — Sora (2026-08-20 revision)

| Step              | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Core wound** | "Everything I touch gets ruined."                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **2. Desire**     | To get close — to help instead of ruin.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **3. Coping**     | Repairs before being asked; takes ownership of every break; apologises for existing.                                                                                                              |
| **4. Class**      | **Vigor** — "am I enough?" Her hands must become enough to not ruin. (Was misclassified as Forge: Forge fits the *function* of repair; Vigor fits the *desire*.)                                  |
| **5. Path**       | Weaver — internalise; the fault is located in herself first. Thread: Hands.                                                                                                                       |
| **6. Signature**  | **Mend** — hands amplify toward wholeness on touch. Destructive mode: **Corrupt** — the same hands unravel structure, gated behind self-blame (the inverted mirror of Beat-Break's honesty gate). |
| **7. Fix**        | Mend cleans when she asks "what does this need?" instead of "how is this my fault?" Corrupt weakens when she stops believing she ruins things.                                                    |
| **8. Worse**      | Corrupt is *fueled* by self-blame — the more she believes she ruins everything, the stronger and easier it becomes. Her worst belief is literal power. The trap is refusing to spend it.          |
