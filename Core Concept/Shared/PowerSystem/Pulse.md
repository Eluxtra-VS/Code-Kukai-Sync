# Pulse

## Short Definition

**Pulse** is the Class of reaching — the desire to close a gap, to send something of the self outward because where the person currently is is not where the thing they need is. Where Vigor presses into what already exists and Forge builds something new, Pulse fires outward. Energy leaves the origin point and travels.

The desire underneath Pulse is directional. It is always toward something. A Pulse user is never content with the range they currently have. The ability is the expression of someone who needs to reach further than their body alone allows.

> _"The energy left. That's the whole thing. It left."_

---

## The Core Principle

Pulse does not stay. Whatever Pulse produces — projectile, beam, wave, burst — exits the origin point and travels through physical space. It obeys physical rules. It can be dodged, blocked, absorbed, redirected. This is the trade Pulse makes in exchange for range: it becomes something that can be countered physically once it is in the air.

The skill of a Pulse user is not just output volume. It is making the physical counter insufficient — too many angles, too fast, too precisely aimed, or the environment too controlled for the block to land before the Pulse does.

Pulse is also the most emotionally readable Class. The direction tells you where the desire is pointing. A Pulse technique aimed at a specific target tells you something about what that target means to the person firing. The Class that always points at something is the Class most easily read by someone paying attention to what it points at.

## Pulse — Akarui Expression

**Direction: External. Energy fired outward from the body into the world.**

Akarui Pulse shapes Rei and fires it outward as projectiles, beams, or sustained field effects. The energy leaves the body entirely — the Akarui is not enhanced by this technique and not protected by it. It simply travels toward the target.

**Early stage:** Single projectiles or short beams. High energy cost per use. The gap between firing and impact is the window of vulnerability — the Akarui has committed the Rei and it is no longer available for anything else while in flight.

**Mid stage:** Multiple simultaneous projectiles. Directional control improves — beams can be curved within limits, projectiles can be split mid-flight. Sustained fields become possible: an area of active Pulse output that costs ongoing Rei to maintain but covers range no single projectile addresses.

**High mastery:** Multi-directional volleys that eliminate the dodging window entirely. Shaped energy constructs that function as barriers — not Forge constructs, which are solid matter, but Pulse fields dense enough to deflect incoming force. The distinction matters: a Pulse barrier is active and costs continuous Rei. A Forge construct is passive once built.

**The Seong Ki expression:** Pure Extraction — specialised Luz and Kuro bullets fired through an Extractor Anchor. The Pulse exits through the object rather than the body directly, which is simultaneously Akarui and Reiki expression. The bullets function as condensed Pulse output that carries the specific emotional signature of what produced them — a Kuro bullet fired through grief does not just damage. It carries that grief to wherever it lands.

**Combat identity:** Akarui Pulse is a range and suppression class. The threat exists at every distance simultaneously. Closing range does not solve the problem — at close range the Akarui simply fires from a different angle.

## Pulse — Weaver Expression

**Direction: Internal. Energy channelled through the body and released at points of contact or from body surfaces.**

Weaver Pulse never leaves the body as an external projectile. The energy moves through internal Rei channels and releases at the point the Weaver chooses — any part of the body, in any of three distinct modes. The Weaver does not aim outward. They emit.

**Three modes — usable from any body point:**

**Resonance:** Luz vibrates internally at controlled frequency. When the Weaver touches or strikes something, the vibration transfers — shockwaves travel through the target from the point of contact outward. The damage is internal to whatever is hit, not surface damage. A punch with Resonance active does not bruise the surface — it disrupts whatever is inside.

**Burst:** Luz pressurises inside a chosen body part and releases entirely on contact. The energy is not sustained — it fires once, at the moment of impact, at maximum intensity. The window is catastrophic and brief. Missing a Burst means the Rei is spent with nothing to show for it.

**Redirection:** Incoming energy — a strike, a Pulse technique, a force of any kind — is absorbed into the body's Rei network on contact and rerouted. It exits through the next strike the Weaver makes, carrying the original force plus whatever Rei the Weaver adds to it. The Weaver does not block. They receive and return.

**Thread activation (amplified):** All three modes operate simultaneously through the Thread body part, at maximum intensity for each. At Unravel stage: the Thread's resonance grows strong enough to affect the immediate surrounding space without contact — a pressure wave extending slightly outward from the Thread part. This is the only moment a Weaver releases something outward. It is Thread-exclusive, expensive, and brief.

**Debuff cost:** Applies on Thread activation. Individual mode use from non-Thread body parts has no debuff. The Weaver pays only for the exceptional moment.

**Combat identity:** Weaver Pulse is an unpredictability class. The emission point can be any part of the body. An opponent preparing to block a right hook may receive Burst from the left shoulder on contact. Resonance through a foot on the ground transfers to the floor and up through whoever is standing on it. The Weaver who has full command of all three modes from all body points simultaneously is a person who is dangerous to touch from any angle.

## Pulse — Reiki Expression

**Direction: Displaced. Energy channelled through Anchors and emitted or redirected through them.**

Reiki Pulse seeds the capacity for energy emission or redirection into objects. A seeded Anchor becomes a fixed point of Pulse output — a turret, a deflection surface, or a focal point for directed fire.

**Connection stage:** Single seeded Anchor emits basic energy on command. The output is weak and requires sustained proximity to maintain.

**Hinan stage:** Multiple Anchors emit or redirect simultaneously. Anchors within range respond without requiring the Reiki to hold them. Energy can be bounced between Anchors — redirected from one to another before reaching the target, eliminating clear lines of sight as a reliable counter.

**Blame stage:** Full Pulse output through Anchors across significant distance. The Reiki can construct a network of emission and redirection points using every seeded Anchor in their environment — a room full of Blame-stage Reiki Pulse Anchors is a room where energy is coming from every surface and every bounce point simultaneously.

**Combat identity:** Like Vigor, Reiki Pulse is environmental. The longer the Reiki has seeded an area, the more comprehensive the network. The specific advantage over Akarui Pulse is the elimination of a single firing origin — there is no point to aim at to prevent the next shot. It is coming from the wall. It is coming from the floor. It is coming from the object you are standing next to and the one behind you.

## Pulse and Desire

Every Pulse user carries the desire to reach something at range. The specific target of that desire shapes how the ability expresses.

Seong Ki's desire is contact — the rush of making something land, the charge of an exchange, the specific aliveness of a fight where something is actually happening. Pure Extraction fires outward because every technique is an attempt to make contact with something real and feel it. The Extractor is his Anchor and his mouthpiece simultaneously. He is not attacking. He is reaching.

The direction a Pulse technique points tells you what that person is always trying to get to. A Pulse user who fires at distance is someone most comfortable affecting things from where they cannot be touched in return. A Pulse user who fires at close range is someone who needs the thing to be close to feel like they reached it. Neither is wrong. Both are information.

## Pulse and the Horror Register

Kuro Pulse is the Class most immediately effective in combat — it does not build up, it does not require sustained presence, it arrives precisely and the emotional effect arrives with it.

A Kuro Pulse technique carries the negative emotion it was fuelled by directly to wherever it lands. Fear fired as a Pulse beam delivers Fear to the target at the point of impact — not as a side effect of the damage, but as the primary payload. The damage and the Fear arrive simultaneously. A target hit by sustained Kuro Pulse in rapid succession is not just injured — they are accumulating the emotional weight of every shot while trying to respond to the physical consequences of each one.

A Weaver Pulse user with Redirection in a Kuro emotional state absorbs an incoming strike and returns it carrying the negative Rei that was already running through their system. The opponent's own force returns to them with Kuro attached. Their own hit becomes the delivery mechanism.

Seong Ki forcing someone into Overdrive through Pure Extraction: the specific horror is not the Overdrive itself but the experience of your own Rei being hijacked from outside. The ratio shifts against your will. The Reiva responds to a state the host did not choose and cannot control. The body does something without the person.
