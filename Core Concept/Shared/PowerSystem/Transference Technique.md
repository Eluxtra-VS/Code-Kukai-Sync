# **Transference Technique**
A learned technique that allows the control of your own Reiva's consciousness. This means you are able to put it into objects and sharing the total Rei between you both. 

## **Obtaining**

To learn this technique, your Reiva must allow you passage into the Oscuro, this can take a while to convince the Reiva to let you do this, as the Oscuro is a harsh and dangerous place and it ends in death for most people. 

Once in the Oscuro, you must survive there until you can find the place of your Reiva's birth, which means surviving the Kukai shades and likely taking them down in a small group.

While you can leave the Oscuro at any time, progress you make in the Oscuro is not saved and you will reset back to the start. 

The place of your Reiva's birth is marked by a glowing white symbol on the ground, by interacting with it, you can learn the symbol's pattern and by extension, learn the **Transference Technique**.
 
Using this technique won't do much yet, it will imprint the ground with Rei in the shape of the Reiva's symbol but won't have any other function. To get further use out of this, you will need a **Vessel**.
