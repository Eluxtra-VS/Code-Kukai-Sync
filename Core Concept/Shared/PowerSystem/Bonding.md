# Bonding

## Short Definition

**Bonding** is the long-term connection process between a host and their [[Reiva]]. It determines control balance, power quality, and survival risk across the entire story.

---

## Phases

### Initial Entry

The Reiva enters the vessel and is mostly dormant. It observes the host's life — learning patterns, emotional tendencies, relationships — and waits.

### Trust Building

Through shared experiences and deliberate choices, host and Reiva learn to synchronise. This is not automatic. It requires the host to _yield_ — small things first, then larger ones.

### Control Balance

Over time the host shifts from [[Bond Stages|Unstable → Zanshin → Union]]. The cleaner the trust, the more fluid the control split becomes.

---

## Bond Strengthening Factors

- Trust — consistent, small yields of control.
- Shared risk — facing danger together rather than one protecting the other.
- Clear communication — mental and empathic, not just action.
- Non-destructive cooperation — using the bond in low-stakes contexts as much as in combat.

---

## Consequences

| Bond Quality | Effect                                                                          |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Strong**   | Increased [[Rei]] efficiency; hybrid abilities unlock; reduced harmful backlash |
| **Weak**     | Reiva takeover risk; memory gaps; Luz waste                                     |
| **Broken**   | [[Kukai]] transformation risk; possible permanent damage                        |

---

## Narrative Role

Bonding is a central emotional arc. Mechanical bond improvement should **always parallel** growth in trust, friendship, and emotional honesty between host and Reiva. The two are not separate tracks — they are the same track expressed in two registers.
