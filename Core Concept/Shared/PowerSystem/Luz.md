## Short Definition

**Luz** is the positive expression of [[Rei]], channelled through positive emotional states. It is the energy the main cast understands first, so early training treats Luz as if it is the whole power system.

> What they do not know yet: [[Kuro]] is the other expression of the same lifeforce.

---

## Rei and Luz

Rei is the raw spiritual lifeforce. Luz is what Rei becomes when it flows through:

- **Tranquility** - calm, settled clarity.
- **Joy** - warmth, connection, hope.
- **Bravery** - resolve, courage, will.

Luz is not a separate substance. It is Rei in its positive state. This is why forcing Rei through suppressed or mislabelled emotions produces unstable, wasteful output: the energy is real, but the channel is distorted.

---

## Two Roles

### Expression of Lifeforce

Because Luz is the positive expression of Rei, healthy Luz flow is a sign of a stable bond and a resolved emotional state. The bond is a bond of shared Rei. Host and Reiva do not have separate reserves; their lifeforce is intertwined.

### Energy in Combat

Luz powers early [[Manifestation|Manifestations]], [[Weaver|Threads]], [[Reiki]] techniques, and most supernatural acts the cast first learns. At story start, the main cast uses Luz for everything: offense, defense, perception, channeling. This is suboptimal for offense. Luz is a long-game energy, and the cast discovers that when they later learn [[Kuro]] exists.

## Reserves

There are only two reserves:

| Reserve | Function |
|---|---|
| **Core Reserve** | Essential Rei sustaining the Reiva bond. Drawing from this is dangerous and must never reach zero. |
| **Reserve** | Expendable pool for normal ability use. Standard combat resource. |

There is no Store pool in current canon. See [[Rei]] for the full reserve system.

## Offensive Use - The Long Game

Luz offense operates on **accumulation and saturation**. It does not end fights immediately. It makes the opponent unable to continue.

### Stage 1 - Disable

Foreign Luz enters the opponent's system and begins limiting their [[Kuro]] access. The more foreign Luz is present, the harder precise Kuro techniques become. Their own Rei output becomes less reliable and less controlled.

### Stage 2 - Saturation

As Luz continues building in the opponent's system, it causes **Luz poisoning**: a draining effect that depletes reserves and stamina over time. The target's body spends Rei fighting the foreign Luz rather than using its own.

### Stage 3 - Overflow

When the target reaches capacity, excess foreign Luz causes direct damage. The body cannot process it, so the overflow burns from the inside.

> A person's own Rei expressed as Luz never poisons them. Only Luz forcibly introduced by an opponent triggers saturation and overflow.

---

## Defensive Use

Luz defense works by **dampening and absorbing** incoming force rather than blocking it cleanly.

- Luz barriers spread and reduce impact rather than stopping it outright.
- A Luz defender is hard to damage quickly; they endure.
- Sustained pressure eventually gets through, but a single overwhelming strike is harder to land cleanly.

---

## Emotion Effect

In addition to disable and saturation, Luz attacks can erode positive [[Emotions]]:

- Primary: **Bravery** - being overwhelmed by something you cannot immediately counter erodes resolve.
- Secondary: Tranquility and Joy diminish as the target realises they are losing ground.

This is separate from the mechanical disable.

---

## Pacing Tools

A Luz fighter needs to prevent a Kuro opponent from ending the fight before saturation builds:

- **Vigor Luz:** Suppressive physical force; hits that pin, slow, and accumulate.
- **Pulse Luz:** Area dampening; projected Luz that makes movement energetically costly.
- **Forge Luz:** Constructs that wall off, create barriers, and impose weight.

---

## Luz Quality and Emotional State

| Emotional State | Luz Quality |
|---|---|
| Calm, acknowledged, resolved emotions | Stable, efficient, precise output |
| Suppressed, mislabelled, chaotic emotions | Unstable; prone to waste, backlash, unpredictable output |

A person who names their feelings produces cleaner Luz. [[Pressure]] left unresolved makes it volatile.
