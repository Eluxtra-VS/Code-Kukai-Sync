## Short Definition

**Vigor** is the Class of force — the desire to make the self, or what the self touches, into something that is enough. Where Pulse reaches outward and Forge builds outward, Vigor presses into what already exists and makes it more than it was.

The desire underneath Vigor is never abstract. It is personal. It is the specific, located belief that the body — present, embodied, here — is not sufficient, and that power must correct this. Not to be more than others. To finally be enough.

> _"The hit doesn't stop at the surface. It never did."_

---

## The Core Principle

Vigor does not create new force from nothing. It amplifies what already exists and extends it past the point it would naturally stop.

A Vigor technique does not end at the point of contact. Impact reverberates — through the target, through the object, through whatever is receiving the force — continuing to propagate past the moment it should have finished. The damage is not only what the strike does. It is what the strike continues doing after it lands.

This is why Vigor users are frequently underestimated in the opening moments of an encounter. The initial impact does not represent the total effect. What follows does.

---

## Vigor — Akarui Expression

**Direction: External. Force projected outward from the body into the world.**

Akarui Vigor channels Luz or Kuro into physical output and the force continues reverberating after impact. The energy does not stop at the point of contact — it propagates through the target, through surfaces, through chains of connected matter, continuing to disrupt structure and function past the initial hit.

**Early stage:** Reverberation is raw and uncontrolled. The force continues but the Akarui cannot direct where it goes after contact. Significant but unpredictable.

**Mid stage:** The Akarui learns to direct reverberation — shaping the propagation along a specific path, through a specific limb, across a specific surface. Force becomes surgical rather than blunt.

**High mastery:** Reverberation can be chained — a single strike propagates through multiple targets in sequence, each one receiving the continuation of the force from the last. A wall can be hit and the force directed through it into whoever is standing on the other side. Distance between the initial impact and the final effect becomes significant.

**Combat identity:** Akarui Vigor is a long-consequence class. The opening strike is the beginning of the technique, not the technique itself. Opponents who focus on surviving the initial hit are focused on the wrong thing.

---

## Vigor — Weaver Expression

**Direction: Internal. Force channelled through the body itself.**

Weaver Vigor does not project outward. It enhances. The Weaver's physical capacity is permanently elevated across the entire body at all times — speed, strength, and durability operating above baseline without Luz cost and without conscious activation. This passive state is always on.

**Passive (whole body):** Baseline enhancement that does not diminish between uses. A Weaver with Vigor affinity at full progression is physically superior to a non-awakened person in every measurable dimension, without spending anything to be so.

**Thread activation (amplified):** When the Thread body part activates, that specific part reaches a ceiling that makes everything else — including the Weaver's own passive baseline — look slow.

- A leg Thread: passive speed is already significant. Thread activation makes the leg operate at a level where the rest of the Weaver's body cannot keep up with it. The leg arrives before the body is ready.
- An arm Thread: passive strength is already above human limit. Thread activation produces impact that reverberates the way Akarui Vigor does — the force continues past contact, through the target, without the Akarui doing anything to direct it.
- Reverberation through the Thread: uniquely, Weaver Vigor at Thread activation does not stop at the surface either. The force travels through the target from the Thread part outward. The difference from Akarui Vigor: it originates inside the body rather than outside it.

**Debuff cost:** Applies on Thread activation only. Passive enhancement has no cost. The Weaver pays only when pushing past the ceiling.

**Combat identity:** Weaver Vigor is constant pressure with escalating stakes. The passive baseline means there is no moment of vulnerability between technique uses. The Thread is the exceptional moment, not the only moment.

## Vigor — Reiki Expression

**Direction: Displaced. Force channelled into Anchors and through them.**

Reiki Vigor seeds physical force into objects — weight, impact momentum, structural resilience that the object does not naturally possess. A weapon seeded with Vigor carries force that extends past the point of contact in the same way Akarui Vigor does, but the origin point is the Anchor rather than the body.

**Connection stage:** Anchors gain basic force enhancement — they are heavier, hit harder, resist damage better. The reverberation is not yet present. The object simply has more impact than its mass suggests.

**Hinan stage:** Seeded Anchors begin producing reverberation on impact. The force continues past contact. The Reiki does not need to be holding the Anchor for this to apply — seeded objects within range deliver the effect independently.

**Blame stage:** Full reverberation through Anchors indistinguishable from Akarui Vigor output in terms of effect, routed through the object rather than the body. A Blame-stage Reiki Vigor user surrounded by decades of seeded Anchors produces a combat environment where every surface, every object, every piece of the space they have claimed is delivering continuing force on contact.

**Combat identity:** Reiki Vigor is an environmental class. The longer the Reiki has inhabited a space and seeded it, the more comprehensive the force network becomes. Fighting a Blame-stage Reiki Vigor user on their home ground is fighting every object in the room simultaneously.

## Vigor and Desire

Every Vigor user carries the same root desire expressed differently:

Ku Mochizuki believed he was a mistake and wanted to be worth something. His Thread is his heart. Beat-Break makes him stronger with every strike landed in sync with his heartbeat — the body becoming more through emotional honesty, the thing he was told was defective becoming the source of the power. The desire to be enough expressed as the heart proving it is.

The specific body part a Weaver Vigor user Threads tells you exactly where they believe the insufficiency is located. The hands: guilt about what they have done or failed to do. The legs: the need to have been faster, to have gotten there in time. The back: the weight carried that was never put down. The Thread is always an answer to the question of what specifically needed to be more.

---

## Vigor and the Horror Register

At extreme use — Thread pushed past Unravel for Weavers, Core Reserve drawn for any type — Vigor becomes the body consuming itself.

A Weaver Vigor user who pushes the Thread past its limit does not simply lose the body part to damage. The Thread consumes it — the amplification turns on its host. The part becomes more than it was until there is nothing left of it that recognisably belongs to the person it was part of. What remains is not injury. It is absence with edges.

A Vigor user who has drawn from the Core Reserve in a combat situation produces force that does not feel like their own anymore. It is more than it should be, more than they understand, and the extra comes from somewhere that is dissolving as they use it.

The body becoming enough. And then becoming too much. And then not being there anymore.
