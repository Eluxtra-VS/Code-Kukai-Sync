# Common Techniques

## Short Definition

**Common Techniques** are Rei applications any awakened person can learn. They are not signatures: they draw on the user's general Rei output rather than their claimed Class affinity, they are trainable like any craft, and mastering one says nothing about who you are. Most awakened people learn one or two, badly, if at all. There are many more than listed here.

Relationship to signatures: a signature is the claimed self (see [[Signature Design]] and [[Signatures]]); a common technique is a tool. Techniques have no claiming condition and produce no Rei configuration shaped like a person's self.

Learning: any awakened person can attempt one. Training is slow, mastery is rare, and K.E.F. operatives are the largest trained population of technique users — which is where civilian-replicated knowledge of most techniques circulates from. Awakened status is required; [[Kyumin]] cannot use techniques (Relics channel power through grafted human chips instead — see [[Relic]]).

## Confirmed Techniques

### Imprint

See the history of where a specific Reiva has been: a trail, readable like tracks. Trainable in tiers. At mastery, the trail stops mattering — the user can sense a Reiva anywhere and see the person glowing through obstacles. The glow's brightness is tied to emotional state: a person facing negative emotions glows far dimmer, sometimes barely at all. Tracking a frightened person is tracking a dying light.

- **Cost:** sustained Imprint use fatigues the eyes' Rei focus; long reads leave after-images of other people's emotional residue.
- **Counter:** genuine calm; suppressing or processing emotion makes a person hard to see. Suppression, for once, hides — and costs what it always costs.
- **Notes:** Ku Mochizuki replicates Imprint from knowledge of K.E.F.-trained users (see [[Ku Mochizuki]]). Used in the Arc 2 search for Kaito — the trail is faint because Kaito is terrified, which is the search's cruel design.

### Blackout

Pair your Rei pulse with your physical fighting style. Every strike pulls the enemy toward the impact point; dodging becomes enormously harder because dodging feeds the pull. The technique converts a fight into a conversation about distance, and the user owns distance.

- **Cost:** the pulse must stay synced with real intent to strike — feints under Blackout drag the user off-balance as hard as the enemy.
- **Counter:** do not dodge. Take the hit on your own terms, break the sync, reset.
- **Notes:** natural fit for Pulse-path fighters; Vigor users adopt it as a brawling discipline.

### Flicker

The physical form "flickers": brief phases of intangibility paired with near-invisibility, usable for movement and combat. A flicker-phase through an opponent, followed by re-solidifying inside their space, is a full-penetration strike that damages seriously.

- **Cost:** each flicker spends Rei heavily; the intangible window is short enough that mistiming means rematerializing inside something solid. Training is as much about courage as control.
- **Counter:** area effects that do not care about solidity; forcing the user to flicker on defense until the cost stacks.
- **Notes:** feared precisely because it punishes the moment of contact — the one time most fighters commit.

### Bulb

Let Rei leak out of the body in huge amounts through points across the skin. The leak weakens the user significantly, but anyone who looks at them finds their gaze redirected away uncontrollably — the eye slides off, attention refuses to land. A dying-bulb glow that cannot be looked at.

- **Cost:** massive Rei drain while active; the user is burning their own Reserve to be unseeable, and the weakening is real and immediate.
- **Counter:** look away on purpose and act on memory; strike the point-sources; wait it out.
- **Notes:** escape and stealth technique. The ugliness is the point: you survive by becoming harder to witness.

### Rattle

Accepted into canon 2026-08-29 (user ruling from agent proposals).

Vent Rei through a struck surface — a wall, a floor, a bell. Everyone touching that surface feels your emotion for one breath, at full strength, as yours.

- **Cost:** the broadcast is honest or it is nothing. Strike the surface with fear and fear arrives; strike it while performing fear and the surface returns silence. You cannot fake a Rattle.
- **Counter:** break contact with the surface; outwait the breath.
- **Notes:** the Code as mechanic: Rei cannot lie. Combat use is terror-breaking and calm-sharing alike; a Rattled calm is one of the rarest things in the setting.
