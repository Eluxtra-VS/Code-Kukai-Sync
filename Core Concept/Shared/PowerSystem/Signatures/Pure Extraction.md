# Pure Extraction

## Short Definition

**Pure Extraction** is Seong Ki's Extractor-based fighting style. It opens his body and movement into a freer combat state so he can fight the way he actually wants to fight: playful, loose, chaotic, and alive.

This is not self-harm and should not be written as self-harm.

## Pressure Logic

Seong's Pressure is expressive and fast-vented: he refuses to let emotion sit still long enough to rot, and his deepest desire is the feeling of being fully alive in motion. That desire makes him Pulse — the affinity of exchange and rhythm — and Pure Extraction is that desire made weapon: the Extractor Anchor is not a wound but a key, opening more ways to move, more speed, more play at a higher ceiling. Even his favourite application, forcing opponents toward Overdrive, serves the same answer to the wound — a bigger, faster, more alive fight is the closest thing he knows to freedom. ## Core Idea

Seong resolves Pressure by having fun.

He does not become stronger through pain, punishment, or self-destruction. His Extractor gives him more freedom to move, improvise, and enjoy the fight without being locked into normal limits.

The point is not "hurt yourself to win."

The point is "stop fighting like the world is a cage."

---

## Effect

Pure Extraction lets Seong widen his combat options through an Extractor.

It can:

- Increase mobility and improvisational movement.
- Let him redirect momentum in strange, playful ways.
- Expand his access to Rei output during combat.
- Turn the battlefield into something closer to a game space.
- Make his fighting style harder to predict because he is genuinely enjoying himself.

The emotional engine is freedom and fun.

---

## Limits

Because this is Extractor-based, it depends on compatibility and control.

Not everyone can use an Extractor effectively, and Seong's success with Pure Extraction should not imply Extractors are easy or universally safe.

If Seong loses the ability to enjoy the fight, the style should become less fluid. Forced fun does not work. The freedom has to be real.
