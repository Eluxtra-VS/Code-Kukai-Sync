# Fracture

## Short Definition

**Fracture** is Kashi Kizu's signature: an Akarui Veil ability that attacks active emotional suppression as a structural weakness in Rei.

Where Jakar's Faultline adjusts emotional states through attention, Fracture targets what a person is currently forcing down.

## Pressure Logic

Kashi's ideology is suppression-as-method: watching the unnamed girl transform taught him that Pressure held past every threshold builds into something resolution could never produce, so his deepest desire is a world of complete expressions — what humans become when they stop resolving. That desire makes him Veil, and the condition is the ideology weaponised: Fracture fires only on someone actively suppressing an emotion in that moment, turning their refusal to process into a literal structural weakness he can press. Emotional honesty and present-moment resolution starve the ability — which is exactly what Kashi refuses to conclude about himself. Full character context: [[Kashi Kizu]]

## Condition

The target must be actively suppressing an emotion in the moment.

This cannot be any painful memory from the past. The suppression has to be present in their Rei architecture now: anger being swallowed, fear being denied, grief being locked down, shame being hidden behind control.

If the target is honest with themselves, Fracture has less to hold onto.

---

## Effect

Fracture finds the compressed point in the target's Rei and weakens the structure around it.

It can:

- Destabilise Rei flow.
- Cause hesitation, misfires, or loss of technique control.
- Make suppressed emotion erupt at the worst possible moment.
- Create a temporary opening in Veil, Forge, or Buki structure.
- Push a target closer to Pressure overload if they keep suppressing.

Fracture does not simply make someone sad or angry. It turns their refusal to process emotion into a literal weak point.

---

## Limits

Fracture is less effective against someone who is emotionally honest in the present moment, even if they are hurt.

It also requires Kashi to correctly read the suppressed emotion. If Kashi misidentifies what the target is holding down, the effect weakens or lands in the wrong place.

---

## Story Use

Fracture is strongest against controlled characters, perfectionists, liars, and people trying to stay useful by hiding what they feel.

It should make Kashi frightening because he does not need to invent a weakness. He only needs to find the one the target is already protecting.
