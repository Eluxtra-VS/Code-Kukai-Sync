# Monarch

## Short Definition

**Monarch** is the signature ability of Mai Takeda — Akarui Forge expressed at the level of high mastery, producing autonomous mechanical ant constructs seeded with Rei that interface with the emotional architecture of whoever they contact. The constructs do not need her active control to function. They exist independently, they spread, and when they have reached sufficient saturation, the people inside the affected area bow.

Not because they were forced. Because their own Rei is telling them it is right.

> _"She doesn't make you bow. She adjusts the conditions until bowing is what naturally happens."_

## Pressure Logic

Mai's Pride completed its arc before the story meets her: recognition arrived for everything she built and calcified instead of satisfying, leaving no hunger except the engineering of deference itself. Her deepest desire is no longer to be recognised but to make hierarchy feel inevitable — conditions under which bowing is what naturally happens — which is Forge at high mastery: solid autonomous constructs seeding rooms until the Rei running through targets produces the genuine felt sense that bowing is right. The ants require nothing from her — they persist, spread, and work whether she watches or not, which is Pride calcified past needing confirmation, expressed as architecture. ## The Constructs

Each ant is a Forge construct — solid, real, made of densified Rei given mechanical form at a scale small enough to operate invisibly on most surfaces. They have a faint metallic appearance at close range, a dark sheen that distinguishes them from background material if specifically examined. They are not difficult to destroy individually. They are difficult to destroy comprehensively.

**Internal structure:** The ants carry Rei in a specific emotional configuration — a sustained output of Rei shaped to correspond to awe, deference, and the specific felt sense that the person one is with is above the category of individuals who require assessment. This is not an inserted emotion. It is Rei adjacent to the target's own Rei architecture, producing a resonance that the target's emotional channels respond to as if the feeling originated internally.

**Movement:** The ants operate autonomously under Mai's general intent. She does not direct individual units — she directs the swarm as an environmental system. They spread to coverage positions, embed in clothing and surfaces, maintain formation without active management.

**Production rate:** Mai produces ants continuously at high mastery. Individual losses within a deployed swarm are replaced faster than targeted destruction can eliminate them. Comprehensive removal of a full deployment requires either removing Mai from the engagement or addressing the swarm at a speed and scale that has other tactical costs.

## How the Emotional Effect Works

The effect is not Veil. The ants are solid Forge constructs — they can be touched, seen, destroyed. What they deliver through contact is Rei in a specific configuration, which produces emotional resonance in the target through sustained proximity. This is the Forge Class being used to deliver something that operates through the Rei medium — the construct is the delivery mechanism, not the ability itself.

**Low saturation** — one or several ants in contact with the target: Subtle adjustment. Confidence becomes slightly less accessible. Anxiety becomes slightly easier to manage, in the direction of deferential calm rather than resolute calm. The target may not notice anything beyond a vague sense of the situation having weight.

**Medium saturation** — significant ant coverage on the target's person or immediate environment: Meaningful emotional reshaping. Positive emotional states that conflict with deference become harder to sustain. The specific configuration the ants carry — awe and the appropriateness of lower ground — becomes the emotional baseline. The target's Rei quality degrades as their emotional architecture is adjusted away from what powers clean ability use.

**Full saturation** — comprehensive coverage across the target and their immediate environment: The target bows. This is not compelled motion. Their body follows from a genuine felt sense that bowing is correct — because the Rei running through them has been adjusted to produce exactly that sense. They know they are bowing. They cannot access the emotional architecture that would give them reason not to. They are doing it themselves, with everything they have, which is the specific design.

## Deployment

Mai does not typically deploy Monarch mid-combat. She deploys it before contact — during approach, during conversation, in the period before an engagement where the ants have been seeded into the environment at scale. By the time direct engagement begins, saturation is already underway.

This is the expression of her Class through her sin: she does not want to fight people. She wants the conditions under which fighting is unnecessary because the outcome is already determined. Monarch is not a combat technique. It is an environmental technique that produces the conditions where combat is redundant.

When used in direct combat — when circumstances require deployment during an engagement rather than before it — the ants operate on a slower saturation curve. Mai's tactical advantage in this mode is maintaining the combat while saturation builds, which means every second of direct engagement is simultaneously a second of progress toward the point where the engagement becomes one-sided in a direction that cannot be reversed by technique.

## The God Complex in the Ability

Monarch is Pride expressed as Forge at its most complete development.

She does not demand that people be below her. She creates the conditions under which being below her is what they genuinely feel is correct, from inside their own emotional architecture, using their own Rei to produce the assessment. The demand would require her to need something from them. The ability requires nothing from her but the constructs — which persist, which spread, which do not require her to be invested in whether they work.

She is not in the room making people bow. She was in the room earlier, and the room is now doing it on its own.

This is the difference between arrogance and Pride calcified past the point of requiring confirmation. Arrogance wants the bow and waits for it. Mai makes the bow inevitable and then loses interest in whether she sees it.

## The Counter

The ants are physical. They can be removed from the body, burned, swept away. Comprehensive physical removal before saturation is complete is the most direct counter.

The secondary counter is the same as the counter to all Veil-adjacent effects: Rei that flows cleanly. The ants' resonance effect works most efficiently against targets with significant unresolved Pressure — emotional channels that are already compromised make the adjustment easier to produce. A target whose Rei is clean and whose emotional architecture is honestly maintained requires more ant coverage for the same saturation level. Not immune. More resistant.

The specific difficulty: removing ants during direct engagement, while maintaining combat performance, while Mai continues producing new units, is a problem of resource allocation that quickly becomes impossible to solve at every level simultaneously. The ants require attention. So does Mai. The division produces openings in whichever is being attended to less.

## Aiden and Monarch

Aiden Takeda has grown up adjacent to the person who built Monarch. He has seen the effect from the observer side — rooms of people orienting toward Mai, the specific quality of the attention she receives, the way no one quite seems to be making the choice to defer and yet defer. He absorbed this as evidence of what capability looks like when it is handled correctly.

He has never understood the mechanism. He has understood only the outcome — recognition arriving without being pursued — and has been trying to reproduce the outcome without understanding what produced it.

When he eventually sees Monarch operating against people he cares about, the understanding will arrive. What it does to his relationship with what he has spent years trying to become is one of the critical beats of his arc.
