# Rapt

## Short Definition

**Rapt** is the signature ability of Niko Juul — Weaver Veil expressed through the Thread ears. When someone speaks the name of their signature ability within his perceptual range, the Rei configuration of that ability at its most clearly expressed — the moment of full claiming — passes through his hearing and is permanently extracted. Per person, per signature heard, Niko gains access to what that person is distinctly capable of at their highest level.

The stacking has no confirmed upper limit.

> _"You named it. You made it real in language. That's when he had it."_

## Pressure Logic

Niko's Envy is addiction-shaped: a gap that moves, aimed not at things but at the existence of someone else having them, so attainment never touches it. His deepest desire is the erasure of other people's having, which produces Weaver Veil — the ears as Thread catch the Rei event of claiming itself, extracting a permanent fragment of another person's ceiling whenever they name their signature within his hearing. The Worse column states the price: fragments never cohere into anything, the library grows while his sense of self atrophies, and each extraction opens awareness of the next person ahead — the ability meant to close the gap is the thing that keeps it open. ## The Thread — Ears

The Weaver Veil condition is always bodily. The Thread body part is the mechanism and the condition simultaneously — inseparable from the physical self.

For Niko, the Thread is his ears. The condition is hearing — specifically, hearing a signature name spoken aloud within his perceptual range by the person whose signature it is.

This is not passive. The ears do not absorb everything that passes through them. Rapt fires specifically on the Rei event that occurs when a person names their signature: the moment the Rei briefly configures in the precise shape of the fully claimed ability. That configuration is what moves through sound, through the air, through the physical medium of speech — and when it reaches Niko's ears, the Thread catches it.

The Thread is his. The sensitivity is in his body. He was a Weaver before he understood what Weavers were, and the ears were always the Thread, because hearing — knowing what is around him, being told things, the specific deficit of being in a place where the information that mattered always seemed to be somewhere else — was always where the insufficiency was located.

---

## What Happens When the Condition Is Met

The signature name is spoken. The Rei configuration passes through Niko's hearing. The Thread extracts the configuration permanently.

**What is extracted:** Not the technique itself. Not a copy of the ability. The affinity — the deepest expression of that person's Class as it manifests in their specific signature. The ceiling they can reach, the precision they can achieve, the specific character of their Rei output at its most developed level.

**What Niko gains:** Per person whose signature he has heard, he gains one specific buff — access to what that person is distinctly good at at their personal ceiling. A Forge specialist's signature heard means Niko can build in Forge at the level that person builds. A Vigor specialist's signature heard means Niko's physical output can reach that person's physical ceiling.

This does not stack as a general power increase. It stacks as a library. Each absorbed capacity is distinct, separately accessible, available to Niko as an additional mode of operation. He is still himself, with his own abilities and his own ceiling. Each absorbed capacity is additive — a new thing he can also do, at the level the original person could do it.

**The integration:** Absorbed capacities do not require training to access. They are available immediately upon extraction. This does not mean they are free — using a capacity draws on Niko's own Rei at the cost appropriate to that level of output. He has the access. He provides the fuel.

## The Envy Expressed in the Mechanic

The sin of Envy is not about wanting things. It is about the specific intolerance of other people having them.

Rapt does not remove the ability from the original. They retain it fully. Niko knows this. He does not take their ability. He makes it also his. This is, in the structural logic of Envy, the most accurate possible expression — not theft, because theft would be resolution. What Rapt produces is the permanent condition of: whatever you have, I have too now. Whatever makes you distinct, I have made myself capable of as well.

The gap closes. Then the original person still exists with their ability and Niko has moved on to the next person who has something he does not.

## Scaling and Limits

**Early:** One or two absorbed capacities. Devastating in the specific encounter where those capacities were relevant. The person who taught Niko what they were capable of by naming it now faces an opponent who can meet them in their own strongest territory.

**Mid:** Multiple absorbed capacities from multiple encounters. Niko's effective ceiling is no longer fixed — it shifts depending on what the current engagement requires. He draws from the library.

**Late:** A Niko who has been operational for months or years is not the same person who began. The library is extensive. The tactical problem becomes selection — which capacity is most useful in this moment — rather than availability. This requires the kind of combat intelligence that knowing many tools requires.

**No confirmed upper limit:** No one has established how many signatures Niko can absorb or whether the integration has a capacity ceiling. He has not found one. Whether this is because there is none or because he has not yet reached it is not established.

## The Cost — What Envy Pays

Rapt has no upper limit, but it has a price, and the price is the sin.

**The fragments never cohere.** Every absorbed capacity is a foreign configuration living in his Rei — someone else's ceiling, someone else's signature shape, someone else's most personally owned expression. They do not merge. They stack as a library. And a library is not a self.

The more Niko absorbs, the less he can feel his own desire underneath the borrowed ones. His own capacity does not grow; it is furnished. At high counts, the capacities begin to argue — a Forge's construction instinct against a Vigor's need to be enough against a Veil's need to control conditions — and Niko experiences his own interior as a crowded room where the furniture has opinions and the owner cannot remember which pieces were his.

**The gap moves anyway.** Every absorbed capacity closes one gap and opens the awareness of the next person who has something he does not. The library never feels complete, because the sin is not about having — it is about the existence of someone else having. Rapt gives him everything and never gives him enough.

**The Rei cost compounds.** Using a foreign capacity at its original ceiling draws Rei at that level of output, and the foreign configurations are inefficient in his body — they were built for someone else's architecture. Sustained reliance on the library degrades his own Rei coherence and raises his Pressure, because unintegrated fragments are unresolved emotion given mechanical form.

**Worse — what suppression deepens:** every extraction is an act of refusing to sit with his own insufficiency. The more he fills himself with others, the more hollow his own sense of self becomes — and the more hollow he feels, the more urgently he needs the next name. Rapt is an addiction with an infinite tolerance and a shrinking user. His arc's trap: the ability that was supposed to close the gap permanently is the thing that keeps the gap open, because the gap is the point.

## The Counter

The counter is silence.

Never speaking your signature name within Niko's perceptual range. Fighting without naming. Never claiming your ability in language while Niko is present.

This is straightforward in principle and psychologically brutal in practice. Signature names are the thing people reach for at their moment of maximum engagement — when they are most fully present in their own capability, most committed to the technique, most invested in what they are doing. The moment of naming is often the moment of maximum power because the naming is the moment of maximum claiming.

Fighting Niko means denying yourself that moment, in every encounter, indefinitely. Using your signature without naming it. Expressing your highest capability without the language that makes it most completely yours.

For some people this is not very difficult. For people whose identity is closely tied to what they can do — whose relationship with their ability is a relationship with who they are — this counter requires something psychologically costly.

**The Kaito question:** Kaito's ability has no name yet. Niko cannot take what has not been claimed. This is not strategic on Kaito's part — the ability is unnamed because Kaito has not yet arrived at the moment of claiming, the moment of looking at what he can do and deciding it is his. 

When that moment arrives, with Niko present, the tactical question of whether to speak it aloud is real. Speaking it names the ability and completes the claiming — and hands Niko the most personally significant capability in the encounter. Not speaking it withholds the capacity from Niko — and also withholds the claiming from Kaito.

What Kaito does in that moment, and why, is one of the most significant decisions in his arc.

## Rapt and the Weaver Path

Niko is a Weaver. His Thread is his ears. The ability lives in his body, requires his body, fires through his body.

The psychological architecture of the Weaver is inward-facing — the belief that the problem is located in the self and must be corrected. For Niko the inadequacy is in hearing — in being the person who is not told things, not included in things, not given the information that would let him be at the level of the people around him. The ear as the Thread is the Weaver expression of Envy: the insufficiency located in reception, in not having heard the right things, in being the person who finds out second.

Rapt is the correction. He will never again fail to hear what matters. Everything that passes through the air within his range, everything that is named in his vicinity, becomes his. The Thread catches it. He keeps it.

The correction does not resolve the insufficiency. It never does, for a Weaver. The Thread is not the solution to the Pressure — it is the Pressure expressed as ability. Niko's ears absorb everything worth absorbing and the gap still moves. Someone always has something he has not heard yet.
