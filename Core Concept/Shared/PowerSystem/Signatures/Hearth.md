# Hearth → Flare (RENAMED)

> **This signature has been renamed to [[Flare]] as of 2026-08-20.**

> Hearth was the working name for Mel Silva's Reiki ability, built around care being *accepted*. It mirrored her coping — care outward, self-erasure — but not her desire. Mel wants to be **seen**, to mean something, to have care land on *her*. Hearth was support; Flare is presence. The core Anchor (the woven bracelet) is unchanged; the class moved from Veil to Pulse and the mechanism from shelter to signal.

> See [[Flare]] for the current signature.

---

## Historical Note

Hearth's mechanics — stabilizing channels, redirecting force through the Anchor, protective warmth built from genuinely accepted care — were retired with the rename. The emotional purpose survives in Flare's Amplifier condition: someone genuinely seeing Mel is the new form of "care landing inward." Her breakthrough beat (the bracelet finally receiving what it was made for) is preserved in the new signature.
