# Signatures

Index of named signature abilities. Each signature is a character's Class and path expressed at its deepest personal level.

> **Class diversity (revised 2026-08-20):** following the [[Signature Design]] framework, several signatures were reclassified so class comes from desire, not function. Veil is now reserved for abilities whose condition is the entire mechanism. Conditions on other classes are universal perks/amplifiers, not Veil gates.

> **Class spread after revision (updated 2026-08-24):** Veil 6 (All Seeing Eye, Calming Fog, Faultline, Fracture, Judgement, Rapt) · Vigor 4 (Beat-Break, Divergence, Flex, Mend) · Forge 3 (Chimera, Monarch, Shackles) · Pulse 2 (Flare, Pure Extraction).

> **Note on Chimera (2026-08-24):** Ren Adeyemi's Weaver Forge signature — the body becomes rather than builds. Mythical forms are composite Rei-assemblies with duration limits. The automatic danger-adaptation is his Pressure made autonomous; see [[Ren Adeyemi]].

> **Note on Flex (2026-08-24):** Gabriel Ferreiro's Weaver Vigor signature — durability as embodied sufficiency. Scar-synchrony is the visual register of escalation. See [[Gabriel Ferreiro]].

> **Note on Faultline vs Fracture:** Jakar and Kashi both exploit emotional distortion, but the abilities are not the same. Kashi's Fracture attacks active suppression as a structural weakness — a pure Veil gate. Jakar's Faultline requires the mask to be *down* — realness as the condition — with Warden as the permanent bond formed at the Kukai threshold.

> **Note on Divergence:** Canonical name for design purposes. In-story, Kaito has not yet named it. The claiming scene in front of Niko is a major narrative beat.

> **Note on Flare:** Mel Silva's signature, renamed from Hearth (2026-08-20). Use [[Flare]] for the mechanic and [[Mel Silva]] for her full emotional arc. See [[Harbor]] and [[Hearth]] for rename history.

> **Note on Sora's Mend:** Reclassified from Forge (Mending) to Vigor (Mend) — class follows desire, not function. Unmake is the destructive mode, the inverted mirror of Beat-Break.
