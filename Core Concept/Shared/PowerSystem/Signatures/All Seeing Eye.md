# All Seeing Eye

[[Daiki Amori|Daiki]]

---

## Short Definition

**All Seeing Eye** is Daiki Amori's Akarui Veil signature. It converts sustained eye contact into progressively stronger control over a target's immediate decision-making.

The ability is not possession. The target remains aware that their actions are not entirely their own. That awareness — watching yourself choose something you would not choose — is the signature's central horror.

## Pressure Logic

Daiki's core wound is invisibility: born noticed, then systematically made irrelevant, until he learned that attention is power and goes wherever the world chooses to look. His deepest desire is to be seen fully and completely — as the central character of his own story — and that desire produces Veil: control of conditions, exercised through the gaze. The condition is the desire made exact — the target must give him their eyes, turning every act of looking into an exchange of power as sustained eye contact converts into progressively total command of another person's attention and choice. Full character context: [[Daiki Amori]]

## Condition

The target must look directly into Daiki's eyes.

The condition is genuine eye contact, not simple visibility. The target must give him their gaze for the ability to take hold. Looking at his face without meeting his eyes is insufficient.

A target can resist or interrupt the effect by:

- closing their eyes;
- looking away;
- using suitable eyewear or another visual barrier;
- using a reflective surface rather than meeting Daiki's eyes directly; or
- breaking the line of eye contact before the effect deepens.

The ability needs the target's actual gaze to meet his. Seeing Daiki through a reflection is not the same condition.

---

## Duration and Control

The longer the eye contact remains unbroken, the deeper the control becomes.

| Contact | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Brief glance** | A mild suggestion-level influence that fades within minutes. The target may mistake it for their own impulse. |
| **Several seconds** | Significant influence over an immediate decision. Daiki can steer what the target chooses while the contact holds. |
| **Prolonged contact** | Full control of immediate action for the duration of the eye contact. The target understands what is happening but cannot override the command while still looking at him. |

All Seeing Eye controls immediate decisions and actions; it does not rewrite a person's history, permanently replace their personality, or create genuine loyalty after the contact ends.

The target's awareness is part of the effect. They are not unconscious, hypnotised, or absent. They are present and unable to stop themselves.

---

## Rei Expression

The ability does not create a physical construct. Daiki's Rei enters the target through the visual condition and interferes with the target's decision-making architecture.

At low intensity, the influence feels like an unusually persuasive impulse. At high intensity, the target's own intention is displaced by Daiki's immediate direction. The target can still perceive the room, remember what they believe, and understand that the action is wrong for them. Their ability to act against the imposed direction is what has been taken.

The effect is strongest when Daiki can hold the gaze without interruption. Crowds, rapidly changing sightlines, smoke, darkness, and targets trained to break eye contact all make sustained control harder to establish. These are practical constraints of maintaining the condition, not separate immunity rules.

---

## Daiki's Self-Limitation

Daiki wears tinted eyewear around groups and avoids sustained eye contact in ordinary conversation. He positions himself slightly off-axis and looks beside a person's face rather than into their eyes unless he has consciously chosen to risk the condition.

This is not a cosmetic habit. It is the practical architecture of living with an ability that can make intimacy indistinguishable from control.

His relationship with Alice contains a rare exception: she knows what his eyes do and chooses when to meet them. Their trust does not remove the danger. It gives the danger a boundary.

---

## Psychological Expression

All Seeing Eye is Daiki's hunger to be seen turned into the power to make himself the centre of another person's attention and choice.

He learned early that attention determines who receives power. The ability is his attempted correction of that lesson: if the world will not reliably see him, he will look into the world until it cannot look anywhere else.

That makes the signature an expression of his former Tenebri identity, not merely a useful combat technique. It offers control where recognition once failed him, while preserving the same unresolved need underneath.

---

## Limits and Counters

- No direct eye contact means no activation.
- Breaking eye contact interrupts the active control.
- Brief contact produces influence, not full command.
- Multiple targets are difficult because Daiki must establish and maintain each gaze separately; treat this as a practical deployment constraint rather than a new immunity rule.
- Eyewear, closed eyes, reflective surfaces, visual obstruction, and disciplined gaze-breaking reduce the ability's reliability.
- The ability controls immediate action; it does not permanently rewrite personality or manufacture lasting devotion.
- Sustained use demands concentration and leaves Daiki vulnerable to anyone he is not actively watching.

The most dependable defence is refusing to give Daiki the gaze he needs.
