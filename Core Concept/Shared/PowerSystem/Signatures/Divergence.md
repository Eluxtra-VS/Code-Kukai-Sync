[[Kaito Amori|Kaito]]

## Short Definition

**Divergence** is Kaito Amori's Weaver Vigor signature. It uses his two-eye Thread to alter the rate at which Rei flows through a target and the state toward which that Rei is currently resolving.

The ability does not manipulate time. It manipulates Rei, the medium through which a thing's physical processes and configurations are expressed.

## Pressure Logic

Kaito's Inadequacy — editing himself into an acceptable version before anyone can see the first draft — produces Vigor directly: the desire beneath the shame is that the self be made enough, and the eyes are where being-seen lives. Divergence expresses that pressure as its honesty gate: Rate and State reach their ceiling only when he holds something in honest focus, and degrade exactly when he tries to correct mid-perception. Full character context: [[Kaito Amori]]

---

## Why Vigor

Vigor's desire is to **be enough** — the belief that the self is insufficient and must be made more. Kaito's Inadequacy is this desire at its most inverted: he believes the version of himself people might see is not enough to survive being seen, so he must correct it before anyone looks.

His Thread is both eyes because the eyes are where being-seen and seeing-truly live. He has spent his life wanting to correct the blue right eye — the mark of the bond he thought was a defect. The eyes are the body part he believes most fundamentally needs to be made enough. That is why they are the Thread.

Vigor for a Weaver means passive whole-body capacity with a Thread ceiling. Kaito's passive capacity is perception. When the Thread activates — when he holds something in honest focus — the eyes reach a ceiling no one else's perception reaches: they do not just see, they read and alter the Rate and State of Rei itself.

## Thread and Functions

Kaito's Thread is both eyes, with each eye carrying a different function:

| Eye | Function | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Left eye** | **Rate** | Changes how quickly Rei flows through a connected target. |
| **Right eye** | **State** | Moves Rei toward a previous or future configuration within the target's own history. |

The two functions are related but not interchangeable. Rate changes how quickly a process occurs. State changes which configuration the Rei is expressing.

---

## Left Eye - Rate

Rate manipulates the speed of Rei flow through whatever visual condition the left eye connects to.

Slowing Rei flow can make a living target sluggish: movement drags behind intention, reactions arrive late, and processes operate behind the moment they should occupy. Accelerating Rei flow can produce the opposite problem, with speed arriving before the body can fully control it.

Rate is not time manipulation. Kaito does not pause, rewind, or accelerate time. He changes the Rei flow on which a physical process depends.

---

## Right Eye - State

State manipulates where in its own history a thing's Rei currently sits.

Rei carries the record of configurations it has passed through — damage and wholeness, before and after change, intact and degraded states. State can return Rei toward a previous configuration or advance it toward a future configuration the thing would naturally reach.

Examples:

- A wound can be returned toward the state before it was made.
- A damaged structure can be restored toward the Rei configuration of its intact version.
- A structure can be advanced toward a degraded state it would naturally reach.

State cannot create a configuration that never existed, cannot return something to a state Kaito has no basis to perceive, and cannot advance something past its natural endpoint.

It is not healing in the ordinary sense. It is not time travel. It is a controlled movement through the target's own Rei history.

---

## The Honesty Gate and Stages

Both functions require visual contact through the relevant eye.

- **Stage One:** Rate works on anything Kaito can touch. State works only on Kaito himself.
- **Stage Two:** Clear visual contact through the relevant eye replaces touch.
- **Stage Three:** Anything within Kaito's perceptual field meets the condition if he is consciously aware of it.

The right eye's requirement is stricter than simply seeing. Kaito must consciously focus on the target. If his attention slips, if he edits himself in the middle of an action, or if he refuses to hold the object or wound clearly in awareness, State fails or returns imperfectly.

This is not a Veil gate — State still functions without perfect focus; it just returns imperfectly. It is the **amplifier gate** of Vigor, the same shape as Beat-Break's honest rhythm: the ceiling only holds while the internal state is honest. Beat-Break requires honest *rhythm*; Divergence requires honest *focus*. Ku's power judges whether he can feel cleanly. Kaito's power judges whether he can see cleanly.

This is a combat rule and a psychological rule at once: Divergence punishes the difference between looking at something and honestly seeing it.

---

## Pressure and Failure

Kaito's output becomes unstable when he tries to correct himself while using the ability. His attention splits between the target and the internal revision that tells him how he should be acting.

The established failure rule is that State can fail or return imperfectly when Kaito does not consciously focus on what he is trying to affect. The exact visual symptoms — such as a delayed Rate change, an incomplete State return, or a pause while he revises himself — are proposed design expressions rather than locked canon.

Whatever form the failure takes, it should remain the mechanical expression of his Inadequacy: the more urgently he tries to erase imperfection, the less honestly he can perceive what is in front of him.

---

## Fix and Worse

**Fix — what honest processing unlocks:** when Kaito stops trying to edit what he sees and simply holds it in awareness, both eyes reach their ceiling. Rate runs clean, State returns true. His strongest use of Divergence comes not from perfect control, but from finally seeing what is present before deciding what it needs to become. That is the Code: correction without honest recognition is another form of suppression.

**Worse — what suppression deepens:** every use while revising himself costs more focus and returns less. The harder he tries to erase imperfection, the dimmer his perception gets — the Inadequacy loop made mechanical. Unresolved, the eyes become the opposite of a Thread: they keep showing him only what he has already decided he is.

## The Naming Lock

Divergence is named for design and documentation purposes only. In-story, Kaito has not claimed the name yet.

[[Niko Juul|Niko]] cannot take or absorb Divergence until Kaito claims and names it in language within Niko's hearing. That makes the naming scene both a personal threshold and a tactical event. The exact timing, witnesses, cost, and immediate consequence remain open in the roadmap and must not be invented by this reference file.

---

## Thematic Function

Divergence makes Kaito's central wound visible as a power system.

He believes the unacceptable parts of himself must be corrected before anyone can see them. Rate lets him change the process. State lets him revise the configuration. Both functions tempt him to treat reality as an error that can be edited if he focuses hard enough.

The later meaning of the Code is not that correction is always wrong. It is that correction without honest recognition is another form of suppression.
