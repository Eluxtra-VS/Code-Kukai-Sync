# Flex

[[Gabriel Ferreiro|Gabriel]]

> **Status:** New signature (2026-08-24). Canon pending first story appearance — currently scheduled for Arc 4 aftermath entry.

---

## Short Definition

**Flex** is Gabriel Ferreiro's Weaver Vigor signature. The harder he flexes, the harder he *gets* — muscle and bone densifying into a visible, stone-like hardness that belongs to no specific material. It is pure durability: a wall that walks.

## Pressure Logic

Gabriel wants to be seen as strong — because somewhere underneath is the belief that only the strong deserve to stay. That is Vigor's desire (*the self must be made more*) in its most literal form: the body made demonstrably, visibly enough. Weaver because nothing leaves him — no constructs, no projectiles, no anchors; the amplification lives entirely inside his own frame. His scars are the pressure made visible history: every one taken for someone else. When he flexes, they light up — his pain history synchronising into proof. Full character context: [[Gabriel Ferreiro]].

## Why Vigor

Vigor's desire is for the self — physical, present, embodied — to finally be enough. Gabriel answered that desire with mass and permanence: be hit, remain, be seen remaining. Weaver Vigor means passive whole-body enhancement always on, reaching its ceiling at Thread activation — which for Gabriel is total-body hardening under maximum voluntary flex.

## Functions

### Passive Baseline

Durability beyond human norms at all times, no Luz cost — bruises shallow, fractures rare. He reads as deceptively heavy on his feet.

### Escalation Tiers

| Tier | State | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| **One** | Voluntary partial flex | Scars glow faintly; skin takes on a dense matte texture at contact points |
| **Two** | Sustained flex | Hardness spreads across the visible musculature — stone-like but material-less, neither rock nor metal, just *density* |
| **Three** | Maximum flex / Thread activation | Whole-body hardening at ceiling; scars blaze, pulsing **in unison** with his heartbeat |

The scar-synchrony is the signature's tell and its poetry: the harder Gabriel becomes, the brighter his history shines. He does not hide his scars. He proves them.

### The Resolve Gate (Amplifier Condition)

Hardness holds its ceiling only while his will holds. Gabriel's resolve is extreme — it takes enormous sustained pressure to break — but the gate is real: fight from hatred, panic, or despair and the hardening runs at reduced capacity; cracks propagate through the dense tissue like stress-fractures in poured concrete. Steady resolve — standing firm *for* someone rather than proving something — raises the ceiling further.

This is an amplifier gate, not a Veil gate: Flex functions regardless; resolve sets how far it climbs.

## Fix and Worse

**Fix:** Strength that stops needing witnesses. When Gabriel lets himself be soft — seen tired, seen hurt, seen needing help — without losing standing in his own eyes, the gate widens permanently. His best fights come after he stops performing invulnerability.

**Worse:** Numbness. The harder he gets, the less he feels — first physically, then everything. Each escalation toward Tier Three as a *default* rather than a choice deadens tactile warmth, then emotional warmth. The end-state Worse is the living statue: untouchable, unbreakable, unfelt — alone behind his own armour, protecting people he can no longer feel protecting. The Code inverted into mineral.

## Limits

- **Mobility tradeoff:** each tier costs speed and flexibility; Tier Three can hold ground but barely chase.
- **Sustain cost:** maximum flex drains Rei heavily; minutes, not hours.
- **Progressive protection:** internal organs shield progressively, not absolutely — Tier One takes a kidney shot seriously.
- **No transfer:** he cannot harden anyone else. A wall, not armour.
