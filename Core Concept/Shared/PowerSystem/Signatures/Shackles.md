# Shackles / Stagecraft

[[Aiden Takeda|Aiden]]

---

## Short Definition

**Shackles** is Aiden Takeda's working Akarui Forge signature. It externalises his Vainglory: the need to make the encounter stay with him because he does not trust himself to matter if attention moves elsewhere.

The ability may later evolve or be renamed **Stagecraft** if the story wants his Forge expression to broaden from bindings into coordinated combat architecture.

## Pressure Logic

Aiden's Vainglory covers the fear of being ignorable: performed pride standing over absent self-belief, so his deepest desire is to build something real enough that it cannot be dismissed — visible proof that he exists correctly, which is Forge's desire. The signature expresses the pressure through its sincerity stabilizer: shackles formed while auditioning for attention come out large, decorative, and hollow; shackles formed while solving the actual problem come out small, clean, and holding. His power is not unreliable — his relationship with recognition is, and the mechanic measures that gap every time he builds. Full character context: [[Aiden Takeda]]

---

## The Mechanic

Aiden creates solid Forge constructs. When one of those constructs makes direct contact with a target, he can form a shackle at the point of contact.

A shackle immobilises the contacted point. A shackle on a wrist locks the wrist. A shackle on an ankle locks the ankle. Multiple shackles compound until the target's movement is reduced to whatever parts remain free.

---

## Vainglory Limitation

Aiden's constructs become worse when he is showing off.

When he is conscious of being watched and trying to impress, the shackles are larger, louder, more decorative, and less stable. They look stronger than they are. They break under pressure because the first design priority was being seen.

When he is not performing, the shackles become smaller, cleaner, and harder to break. His Forge output coordinates better because he is solving the problem instead of auditioning for belief.

The ability is not unreliable. Aiden is.

---

## Possible Evolution — Stagecraft

If Aiden's mature signature expands beyond binding, **Stagecraft** is the stronger final concept.

Early Stagecraft: flashy platforms, barriers, and bindings that announce themselves before they function.

Mature Stagecraft: coordinated Forge architecture that controls the fight space with minimal waste. Platforms appear where allies need footing. Barriers land where they protect rather than where they impress. Shackles lock the exact joint that matters. The stage no longer exists to prove Aiden is important. It exists so everyone can move correctly.

This preserves Forge while making the arc sharper: Aiden stops building monuments to his insecurity and starts building structures that hold.
