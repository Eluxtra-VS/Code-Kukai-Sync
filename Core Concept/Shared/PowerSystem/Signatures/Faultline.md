# Faultline

[[Jakar Offei|Jakar]]

> **Signature unification (2026-08-20):** Jakar previously had two signatures — Faultline (combat emotional control) and Warden (permanent Kukai bond). They are now **one signature: Faultline.** Warden survives as the name of Faultline's most extreme application — the permanent bond formed when the ability is used at the moment of a Kukai's transformation. One ability, one condition, one character. See [[Signature Design]].

---

## Short Definition

**Faultline** is Jakar Offei's signature: an Akarui Veil ability that manipulates a target's emotional pressure lines **once the target is real** — once their emotional mask is genuinely down and they are feeling something they cannot perform, manage, or manufacture.

It is not mind control. It is emotional control operating on a person's actual interior, and it can only reach that interior when the performance has fallen away.

> _He doesn't break the mask. He waits for the mask to drop, then works on what was behind it._

## Pressure Logic

Jakar's core wound is being seen only as surface — looked through, projected onto — fused with Ama's death: the attention that finally landed on him killed her, so genuine contact became something he could only manufacture. His deepest desire is contact with what is actually behind people's masks, and that desire produces Veil — the condition is the entire mechanism, gripping a target's pressure lines only when their mask is genuinely down. This is why the sadism exists: pain is the most reliable mask-remover he knows, so suffering became both the only door into the realness his ability requires and the only human contact he has had since. Warden, the permanent bond at maximum realness, is the same hunger taken to its extreme — keeping, permanently, the people whose realness he caused. Full character context: [[Jakar Offei]]

---

## The Condition

**Condition: the target is genuinely real in this moment** — experiencing an emotion they cannot perform away: fear stripped of management, grief stripped of composure, rage stripped of control, love stripped of posture. The mask must be actually down, not lowered theatrically.

This condition is the entire mechanism (Veil). If the target is performing, managing, or deflecting — if their emotional output is manufactured in any degree — Faultline finds nothing to grip. The ability is inert against a composed person.

This is why Jakar manufactures suffering. Pain is the most reliable mask-remover in existence. Fear and grief strip people to what they actually are faster than anything else he has found — which is why the sadism is not separate from the power: **his ability requires realness, and suffering is the only door he has ever found into it.** The condition explains the character.

It also means the ability is a mirror of his own wound. He has spent his life being seen as a surface — projected onto, looked through. Faultline can only touch people when the surface is gone. It is the only contact he has ever been able to make, and it is the only contact he has ever had to manufacture.

---

## Effect

Once the condition is met, Faultline adjusts the emotional pressure lines inside the target's Rei — the actual interior, not the surface:

- Increase fear, anger, grief, fixation, trust, or doubt.
- Lower emotional resistance by making a feeling seem more natural than it is.
- Amplify unresolved Pressure toward a dangerous threshold.
- Destabilise Rei quality by forcing emotional states out of alignment.
- Create openings in combat by making the target react from a feeling they did not choose.

The manipulation operates on the real interior, so it is more precise and more intimate than surface-level influence. But it is limited by the condition's duration: **the grip only holds while the realness holds.** The moment the target re-composes, regains performance, or processes the emotion into a managed state, the grip weakens and the condition must be re-entered.

This is the nerf. Jakar cannot hold permanent emotional control over anyone. He must keep making people real — which is exactly his addiction.

---

## The Kukai Threshold — Warden

Faultline's darkest application is **Warden**: the permanent bond formed when the ability is used at the moment of a Kukai's transformation.

A person crossing the Kukai threshold is at maximum realness — masks destroyed, Pressure critical, the surface gone entirely. Faultline at full focus in that moment creates a permanent link: awareness of the Kukai's location, a channel of communication, and directional influence that cannot be fully refused. Not puppetry — the will remains; the direction it moves in is not entirely their own.

Warden is not a separate ability. It is what Faultline becomes when Jakar pushes a real moment past the threshold. It requires deliberate causation — he must be the direct cause of the transformation — which is why Ori exists: he chose to be the cause rather than the bystandard, to keep the one person who had ever genuinely seen him.

Warden's permanence is not a balance oversight. It is the point: the ability that can only touch real people is the ability that permanently binds him to the people he made real by destroying them. He cannot turn it off. He cannot undo it. Every Kukai he has ever deliberately created is a permanent extension of his presence — and his permanent accountability.

---

## Fix and Worse

**Fix — what honest processing unlocks:** Faultline's condition does not actually require suffering. It requires realness. A person who is *voluntarily* honest — who drops the mask because they choose to, who is real with Jakar without being broken down — meets the condition too. The ability was never the problem. His belief that realness can only be manufactured is the problem.

The story's seed: Jakar encounters an honest connection that does not require pain — genuine contact, mask willingly down, the thing behind the window looking back at him — and rejects it, because he cannot believe it is real. The condition works. He refuses to accept it.

**Worse — what suppression deepens:** every use reinforces the equation "suffering = realness." Each manufactured emotion is furniture he arranges in someone else's room, and each one deepens his isolation — the more he makes people real by breaking them, the less he believes anyone could be real with him any other way. The loop tightens until Ori's coherent windows — the one real thing he did not manufacture — become the only thing left he cannot bring himself to exploit.

---

## Limits

- Faultline is inert against the composed, the performed, and the genuinely at-peace.
- Emotional honesty is the universal resistance: a person who processes openly has fewer exploitable distortions — and a person who is voluntarily real with him gives him the thing he wants without him having to take it.
- The manipulation holds only while the realness holds.
- Distance, sensory shielding, and refusing to engage reduce or end the effect.
