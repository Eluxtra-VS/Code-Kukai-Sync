# Warden — the Extreme Application of Faultline

[[Jakar Offei|Jakar]]

> **Unified with Faultline (2026-08-20):** Warden is no longer a separate signature. It is the name for **Faultline's most extreme application** — the permanent bond formed when the ability is used at the moment of a Kukai's transformation. One ability, one condition, one character. See [[Faultline]] for the unified signature.

---

## Short Definition

**Warden** is the permanent Kukai bond. When Jakar is the **direct cause** of a person's Kukai transformation — when their Pressure crosses the threshold through him, deliberately and with full knowledge — Faultline's condition is at its maximum (masks destroyed, Pressure critical, realness absolute) and the link becomes permanent.

Every Kukai whose transformation Jakar directly caused falls under Warden. Not influenced. Not monitored. Under it.

> _"He didn't just make them. He kept them."_

## Pressure Logic

Warden is not a separate desire — it is Jakar's single pressure at its most extreme expression through Faultline: the need for real contact fused with the terror of losing the one person who ever saw him clearly. Where Faultline waits for realness and grips it while it lasts, Warden makes the realness permanent by causing it, converting a moment of absolute masklessness into a bond that cannot be refused or undone. The permanence is the desire stated plainly — he keeps them, because keeping was the only form of contact he ever managed to secure. Full character context: [[Jakar Offei]]
---

## The Condition — Inherited from Faultline

The condition is Faultline's, met at its absolute extreme: the target is more real than they have ever been, at the precise moment their humanity is being destroyed. The deliberate causation is what makes the bond permanent — not the transformation itself, but the fact that Jakar was the cause.

The condition is met once and cannot be unmet. There is no counter, no reversal, no process by which the transformed person exits Warden's scope. It is a permanent one-way gate — but it is a gate of Faultline, not a separate ability. Jakar could no more sever a Warden bond than he could stop being the cause.

---

## What It Does

- **Awareness:** Jakar knows where each Warden-bound Kukai is, with the same quality of knowledge as knowing where his own hand is.
- **Communication:** he can speak to them and hear them regardless of distance, through the bond rather than any medium.
- **Influence:** he can push — not puppet. Directional pressure on behaviour and attention. The will remains; the direction it moves in is not entirely their own.
- **What it cannot do:** it cannot make a Kukai want something they do not want. It can make the path toward what Jakar wants feel clearer. Ori's coherent windows exist in part because Warden preserves continuous interior connection — and in part because Warden cannot rewrite what Ori is.

---

## Why Jakar Made Ori

Warden's design intent was never tactical. The first person he transformed deliberately was someone he was about to lose — someone who had seen him clearly enough that losing them was a different kind of absence than any he had known. He could not keep them as they were. Warden means he can keep them at all.

Whether this was care or possession is the question the story does not answer for a long time.

---

## Scope and the Horror Register

Jakar has created more Kukai than Ori. Most were incidental. He is aware of all of them; he does not maintain active communication with most. Ori is the exception — the one for whom Warden was the point, not the consequence.

The horror from the Kukai's perspective: transformation did not end their connection to the person who transformed them. They cannot outrun the relationship. They cannot close the channel. The bond persists not because they want it to but because it was built into the transformation itself.

For Ori, who has coherent windows and something like interiority, this is either the only remaining reason to have windows at all, or the most precise definition of what was taken.

---

## Story Use — Unchanged by the Unification

Warden's permanence is locked canon and unchanged: Ori refuses a Warden-directed action without breaking the bond, isolates after Jakar's defeat or containment, and eventually dies quietly from the consequences of the manufactured Kukai existence. Jakar stops active pressure but cannot sever the bond and is not redeemed by regret. The endgame staging remains open (custody, record medium, observer, death signs) and must not contradict Warden's permanence.

The unification only changes the *classification* — Warden is Faultline at its extreme, not a second ability. All endgame scene cards remain valid.
