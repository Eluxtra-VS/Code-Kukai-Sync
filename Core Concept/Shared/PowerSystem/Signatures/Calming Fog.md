# Calming Fog

[[Yeshi Tanaka|Yeshi]]

## Short Definition

**Calming Fog** is the signature ability of Yeshi Tanaka — Reiki Veil expressed through seeded cigar Anchors that produce smoke with properties determined by each cigar's seeding configuration.

## Pressure Logic

Yeshi's Sloth is exhaustion finished as philosophy: a lifetime of Gear District noise capped by his wife's death left him withdrawn into the conviction that nothing is worth the energy caring costs. His deepest desire is rest — a moment of quiet he never once found while anyone was asking something of him — and rest is Veil's shape: control of conditions, imposed softly, displaced outward into the cigar Anchors he already carried for decades as portable silence. Calming Fog expresses that desire literally: seeded smoke that softens the world past the point of demanding anything, whether structural calm or sensory fold-back where urgency stops reaching anyone inside it. Full character context: [[Yeshi Tanaka]]

---

## The Anchor System

Each cigar is a distinct, pre-seeded Anchor. Yeshi swaps them constantly — not out of habit, but operationally. Different cigars carry different smoke configurations. Each is disposable after use. He does not reuse them.

**The Veil condition:** skin contact with the smoke, or inhalation of smoke from a seeded cigar. The condition is always the Anchor. Without the cigar, the ability cannot fire.

---

## What the Smoke Does

The smoke does two things depending on seeding configuration:

**Structural:** The smoke condenses into solid forms — barriers, weapon shapes, constructs with enough structural integrity to function in combat. These last as long as Yeshi's attention holds on them and dissolve when his focus shifts or the cigar burns out.

**Sensory:** The smoke diffuses into wide clouds carrying full sensory distortions. Not simple visual tricks. Fold-back illusions — the target's perception is turned back on itself, repeating what they expect to see rather than what is actually there. The distortion holds until the smoke clears or the condition (contact) is broken.

---

## Yeshi's Relationship with the Ability

The thing that kept him functional for decades was already part of him. He found out late that the smoke was never just a habit. The Anchors were always there, in his hands, carrying more than he knew he was putting in them.

He does not find this interesting. He finds it agreeable.
