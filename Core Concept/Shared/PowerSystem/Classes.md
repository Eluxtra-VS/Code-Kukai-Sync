# Classes

## Short Definition

**Classes** are the four core affinity types that define the style of a character's power expression. They are not factions — they apply across Akarui, Weaver, and Reiki, but how they manifest is fundamentally different depending on the path.

> Akarui expression is External. Weaver expression is Internal. Reiki expression is Displaced.

---

## How Class is Determined

Class is not tested or chosen. It is expressed — the deepest functional desire underneath the Pressure, given shape by Rei when it finally has somewhere to go.

The first uncontrolled Rei output after Awakening almost always reflects Class naturally — before training, before control, Rei defaults to affinity. An Akarui with Forge affinity will instinctively build rather than fire. A Weaver with Veil affinity will find their Thread produces perception before it produces force. The Reiva, when asked directly, can name the affinity with certainty — they have known since before the host could speak.

The signature ability, when it forms, is always the affinity Class at its deepest expression. Affinity was always pointing toward the signature. The signature confirms what was already true.

## The Four Classes

---

### Vigor — The Desire to Be Enough

The desire underneath Vigor is the belief that the self — physical, present, embodied — is insufficient and must be made more. Not more than others. More than it currently is.

**Akarui Vigor:** Luz channels into physical output and reverberates after impact. The force continues propagating through the target past the point of contact — structural damage, internal disruption, cascading force that does not stop at the first thing it hits.

**Weaver Vigor:** Passive whole-body physical enhancement at all times without Luz cost. When the Thread activates, that specific part reaches a ceiling that makes everything else — including the Weaver's own passive baseline — look slow.

**Reiki Vigor:** Anchors gain physical force, weight, and structural resilience. At high mastery: reverberation-style impact through the object, echoing the Akarui expression but routed through the Anchor rather than the body.

---

### Pulse — The Desire to Reach

The desire underneath Pulse is the need to close a gap. To send something of the self outward because where the person is is not where they need to be.

**Akarui Pulse:** Luz or Kuro shaped and fired outward as projectiles, beams, or sustained fields. The energy leaves the body entirely.

**Weaver Pulse:** Never external. Three interlocking internal modes usable from any body point: Resonance (vibration transferred through contact), Burst (compression released on contact), Redirection (incoming energy absorbed and discharged through the next strike). At Thread activation: all three simultaneously at maximum intensity. At Unravel: the Thread's resonance extends slightly into surrounding space — the only moment a Weaver releases anything outward.

**Reiki Pulse:** Anchors emit or redirect energy. A seeded object becomes a turret, a deflection surface, or a focal point.

---

### Forge — The Desire to Build

The desire underneath Forge is constructive — to make something real, something whole, something that has presence and cannot be dismissed.

**Akarui Forge:** Luz externalised and given solid form — constructs exist independently of the Akarui's body. Weapons, barriers, platforms, traps. At high mastery: complex, persistent, self-modifying constructs.

**Weaver Forge:** Passive whole-body malleability — surface hardening, density shifts, minor structural reshaping at any point. Thread activation: the Thread part undergoes complete transformation. It does not build something. It becomes something. A hand becomes a blade. A leg becomes a piston. At Unravel: transformations sustained and layered, at risk of the Thread consuming the part entirely.

**Reiki Forge:** Seeded Anchors temporarily reshaped. At Blame stage: an Anchor dissolved and reformed mid-combat, carrying the emotional weight of its history into the new form.

---

### Veil — The Desire to Control Conditions

The desire underneath Veil is situational — not to be stronger or faster or more constructed, but to make things become what they need to be. Not to defeat someone. To make defeat inevitable before the fight is fought.

**Veil is the only class where the condition is the entire mechanism.** Without the condition, a Veil ability does nothing. When the condition is met, the effect is absolute within its scope. The condition is not a weakness. It is what makes Veil unblockable once entered.

Veils should be rare. If a character can do *something* without the condition, the condition is a perk — and the ability belongs to another class with a condition layer, not to Veil.

---

## The Universal Condition Layer

Conditions are a signature-level mechanic available to **every** class, not a Veil monopoly. Any signature may carry a condition as a **perk, amplifier, or stabilizer** — the ability works without it; the condition makes it better, cleaner, or more stable.

| Condition Role | What It Does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Amplifier** | Condition met → the ceiling rises | Beat-Break: honest rhythm → stacks build |
| **Stabilizer** | Condition met → output stays clean | Shackles: sincerity → constructs hold |
| **Mode Gate** | Condition met → a specific mode unlocks | Mend → Unmake: destructive mode gated behind self-blame |

The distinction from Veil is the cut: **if removing the condition makes the ability cease to exist, it is Veil.** If the ability functions without it and the condition merely sharpens it, the class is whatever the desire says it is (Vigor/Pulse/Forge) and the condition is a layer on top. See [[Signature Design]] for the full framework and the class-by-desire table.

**Akarui Veil:** The condition is always external — something the target does, says, carries, or encounters. The Akarui engineers situations where the condition becomes inevitable.

**Weaver Veil:** The condition is always bodily — something the Weaver does with their own body, or something that happens to it. The Thread and the condition are inseparable.

**Reiki Veil:** The condition is always the Anchor — something that happens in relation to the seeded object. Without the Anchor, the ability cannot fire.

## Cross-Path Summary

| Class | Akarui | Weaver | Reiki |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vigor** | Reverberation after impact | Passive body-wide + Thread amplification | Anchors gain physical force |
| **Pulse** | Projectiles and beams | Body emission + Thread amplification | Anchors emit and redirect |
| **Forge** | Independent constructs | Body malleability + Thread transformation | Anchor reshaping |
| **Veil** | External condition trigger | Bodily condition trigger | Anchor condition trigger |

---

## Notes

- Class is not a ceiling. It is the natural shape of a character's power. Non-affinity Classes can be used at higher Rei cost and lower precision.
- Each ability links to both a Class and an Emotion — the combination defines the full character of the technique.
- The signature ability is always the affinity Class at its deepest expression.
