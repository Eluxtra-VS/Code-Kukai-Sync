## Short Definition

A **Manifestation** is the named, unique signature ability that an [[Akarui]] unlocks when they integrate their [[Reiva]] and resolve [[Pressure]] outward in their first meaningful action. It is not a general skill — it is _theirs_, tied to their psychology, their history, and the specific shape of theia Manifestation.

## Characteristics

- **Named** — every Manifestation has a specific name personal to the host.
- **Visually distinct** — its appearance reflects the host's emotional world, not just their combat style.
- **Personally tied** — it cannot be replicated by another person. It is the Akarui's fingerprint.

---

## Evolution

A Manifestation changes as the host changes:

| Trigger                | Effect                                                                    |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Deepening Pressure** | Escalation — the ability grows more powerful but more unstable and costly |
| **Resolving Pressure** | Reconfiguration — the ability refines, becomes more precise               |


These examples demonstrate the design rule: a Manifestation should be mechanically legible, visually distinct, and psychologically specific. If the ability could belong to another character without changing its meaning, it is not yet personal enough.
