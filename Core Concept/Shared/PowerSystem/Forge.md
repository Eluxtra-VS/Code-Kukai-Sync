# Forge

## Short Definition

**Forge** is the Class of making. The desire underneath it is always constructive at its root — the belief that the answer to what is wrong is something that needs to be built, shaped, or restored. Not fixing the self. Not reaching something at range. Making something that stays.

Where Vigor amplifies and Pulse projects, Forge creates. Something comes into being that was not there before — solid, present, occupying space. The form can be a weapon, a structure, a reshaping of the body itself, a restoration of what was broken. What it cannot be is insubstantial. Forge produces things that persist after the technique that made them has finished.

> _"It wasn't there. Now it is. That's the whole argument."_

---

## The Core Principle

Forge takes Rei and gives it form. The form is real — it can be touched, destroyed, built upon. This persistence is both the strength and the constraint. A Forge construct exists, which means it must be made with enough integrity to survive contact with whatever opposes it.

The craft of Forge is not just in the making. It is in making something worth making.

---

## Forge — Akarui Expression

**Direction: External. Rei externalised into independent constructs.**

Akarui Forge builds outside the body. Constructs exist independently — once made, they occupy space on their own. The Akarui does not need contact with them for them to persist.

**What can be constructed:** Weapons the Akarui wields or throws. Barriers between them and incoming force. Platforms that create positioning advantage. Traps built in advance and triggered later. Complex, self-modifying structures that adapt mid-combat. The limit is conceptual clarity and available Rei — a construct the Akarui cannot hold in complete mental detail costs more and produces less.

**Early stage:** Simple shapes. A wall is a wall. A weapon is a shape that hits hard. Significant Rei cost per build.

**Mid stage:** Complex geometry. Multiple simultaneous constructs. Reduced cost per unit as the Akarui develops architectural understanding — building something that does exactly what the situation requires rather than the closest available shape.

**High mastery:** Constructs that self-modify after creation. A barrier that redirects rather than absorbs. A weapon that changes form mid-fight. Multiple simultaneous constructs managed as a coordinated environment. Persistent constructs maintained at low ongoing Rei cost between encounters.

**Aiden Takeda's expression:** The Shackles. See [[Shackles]].

**Mai Takeda's expression:** Monarch. See [[Monarch]].

**Combat identity:** Architecture. The fight happens inside what the Forge user has built. Every new construct changes the conditions of the encounter before direct engagement.

## Forge — Weaver Expression

**Direction: Internal. The body itself is the construct.**

Weaver Forge does not build outside the body. It transforms it. The Thread body part does not produce something — it becomes something.

**Passive (whole body):** Continuous malleability from any point — surface hardening, density shifts, minor structural reshaping. Available at low cost without Thread activation. The body is always partially Forge-active.

**Thread activation (amplified):** Complete transformation of the Thread part. Not enhancement — transformation. A hand becomes a blade, a crushing mass, a lattice of hardened Rei-fused structure. A leg becomes a piston, a spike, an anchoring root. The transformation is total for the duration.

**At Unravel:** Transformations sustained and layered. The risk: the Thread consuming the part entirely. The amplification turns on its host. What remains is not injury — it is absence with edges.

**Aiden's expression:** Shackles — the desire to make something that holds, expressed as Forge. What he builds is visible proof. The constructs stabilise when he stops performing. *(Sora was formerly listed here as "Mending"; she has been reclassified to Weaver Vigor — see [[Mend]].)*

**Combat identity:** Adaptation. The Weaver becomes the right tool for what the fight requires. The body is the inventory.

## Forge — Reiki Expression

**Direction: Displaced. Rei seeded into Anchors; the Anchors reshaped.**

Reiki Forge seeds the capacity for reshaping into objects. A seeded Anchor can be altered — its form changed, its structure modified, its function shifted from what it is to what the moment requires.

**Connection stage:** Basic reshaping. Extensions, compressions, simple alterations. Reverts when active maintenance stops.

**Hinan stage:** Significant reshaping held for meaningful duration. A knife becomes a hook. A chain holds rigid. The shape stays.

**Blame stage:** Full dissolution and reformation mid-combat. An Anchor dissolved into its component Rei and rebuilt as something entirely different. The new form carries the emotional weight of the Anchor's entire history. A Blame-stage weapon that was once something beloved, reformed into something sharp, is not just dangerous. It is heavy in a way that has nothing to do with its mass.

**Combat identity:** History. Every reshaped Anchor carries what it was before. The longer the Reiki has seeded something, the more it carries into every new form.

## Forge and the Horror Register

A Weaver Forge user who pushes the Thread past Unravel's limit does not simply lose the body part to damage. The Thread consumes it. The amplification turns on its host. What remains is no longer recognisably part of the person it was attached to. Functional in the Forge sense. Gone in every other.

A Kukai with Forge Class builds in Kuro. What they construct carries the emotional weight of their collapsed state. A wall built by a Forge Kukai out of grief-fuelled Kuro does not just block — it makes everyone near it feel that specific grief. Not vaguely. Exactly. The construct holds the emotional content of what produced it and delivers it to whoever touches it.

## Forge and Desire

The specific thing a Forge user believes needs to exist tells you what is missing in them.

Aiden builds shackles — he wants opponents held in place, unable to leave the encounter. He is performing for an audience. When he stops performing, the shackles hold perfectly.

Mai builds ants — she wants the conditions under which everyone is already below her before direct engagement begins. The constructs are small, numerous, and distributed. Pride that does not need to announce itself.

Aoki mends — she wants things to be whole. *(Reclassified 2026-08-20: Weaver Vigor — the desire to make things enough is Vigor's desire; mending is her expression, not her Class. Kept here because it remains the clearest illustration of constructive desire.)*
