## Short Definition

**Reiki** are the rarest expression of [[Buki]] — a Sub-Type so specialised that most awakened people never encounter one. Where ordinary Buki users seed objects through contact and proximity, a Reiki is a full manifestation of the Buki path: someone whose [[Luz]] has become so deeply fused with their Anchors that the objects respond to them across any distance, without being touched.

> _Most people who use Buki never become Reiki. The community that already looks down on Buki looks down on Reiki even harder — until they see one fight on their home ground._

---

## What Makes a Reiki Different from a Buki User

||Buki User|Reiki|
|---|---|---|
|**Anchor control**|Requires proximity or contact with seeded objects|Can control seeded Anchors **remotely** — no contact required|
|**Black Box Test result**|Cube forms on held object|Cube forms on held object — and remains responsive even when released|
|**Community perception**|Seen as low-control, last resort|Seen as even more eccentric, but harder to dismiss once developed|
|**Power ceiling**|Blame stage — emotionally devastating dependency on Anchors|Blame stage + remote control = a fighter whose battlefield is wherever their Anchors are|

## Remote Anchor Control

Once an object has been seeded by a Reiki, it does not require physical contact to respond. The Reiki can:

- Move, activate, and direct Anchors from any distance.
- Channel their [[Classes|Class affinity]] through remote Anchors.
- Maintain awareness of multiple Anchors simultaneously across a wider area than a standard Buki user.

The **emotional investment** in each Anchor still applies — and at [[Buki|Blame]] stage, losing an Anchor is still psychologically devastating regardless of whether it was in hand or across the room.

---

## Reiva Communication — Anchor Method

Reiki have a unique communication advantage: they can **speak to their [[Reiva]] through any seeded Anchor at any time**. The Reiva may choose not to respond, but the channel is always open as long as the Anchor is intact.

This is distinct from the [[Reiva Communication|Earphone method]] (requires Luz input and has a time limit) and the post-[[Extractors|Overdrive]] window (situational). For a Reiki, every Anchor is a passive line to their Reiva.

## Black Box Test Result

> _A cube forms on the held object — and when the object is released, the cube stays._

This distinguishes Reiki from standard Buki users at the diagnostic stage. A Buki user's cube disappears when they put the object down. A Reiki's continues responding. The object remembers them even without contact.

---

## Rarity

Reiki are rare enough that many awakened people have never encountered one. Part of the reason Buki is dismissed as a technique is that most observers only ever see early-stage Buki users — people who need to hold their objects and whose control looks clumsy compared to an Akarui's direct projection or a Weaver's body-integrated power.

A Blame-stage Reiki surrounded by decades of seeded Anchors is a categorically different thing. The community just rarely lives long enough around one to update its assumptions.

---

## Progression

Reiki follow the same [[Buki]] progression as standard Buki users: **Connection → Hinan → Blame**. The difference is that at each stage, the remote control capability amplifies what is already there:

- **Connection:** Anchors respond while held; brief response window when just released.
- **Hinan:** Anchors respond within meaningful range without contact. The refuge dynamic applies — the Reiki retreats into their Anchor network the way others retreat into people.
- **Blame:** Full remote control across significant distance. The Anchors carry emotional weight that has nowhere else to go. Destroying them is the hardest counter.
