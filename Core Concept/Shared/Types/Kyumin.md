## Short Definition

A **Kyumin** is any human who has the will, desire, and potential to [[Awakening|Awaken]]. In practice, the term covers anyone who is part of the human–[[Reiva]] world — whether they know it or not — and has a dormant Reiva bond.

---

## What Being a Kyumin Means

Being a Kyumin is **not a power**. It is a state of potential.

- A Reiva is bound to you — dormant, observing, waiting.
- You have the will and desire to awaken, even if you don't know what that means yet.
- You may sense things others don't: low sounds, cold patches, a feeling that something is underneath the surface of daily life.

Not every human becomes a Kyumin. Some are naturally insensitive; others are sensitive but never encounter the conditions for Awakening. Potential is not destiny.

---

## Kyumin vs. Awakened Paths

**Kyumin** describes the _threshold state_ — dormancy and potential, before resolution.

After Awakening and first Pressure resolution, a Kyumin becomes:

- **[[Akarui]]** — if they externalise.
- **[[Weaver]]** — if they internalise.
- **[[Reiki]]** — if they displace.
- **[[Kukai]]** — if they fail to resolve entirely.

---

## Social Meaning

- **Cultural alienation:** Kyumin often feel that something is under the surface of daily life — they sense it without being able to name it.
- **Oral tradition:** Small Kyumin groups exist informally — elders, fishermen, people who pass along warnings. Not organisations. A loose cultural memory. The way the [[Code]] propagates without anyone organising it.
- **Traditions:** "Listen nights" on low tides. Avoiding certain rocks. Stories of "white hands in the fog." These are real-world traces of [[Kukai]] encounters, translated into folklore over generations.

---

## Kyumin and the Tenebri Bloodlines

Characters in the [[Tidal Plate]] have a **disproportionately high rate of Kyumin potential** due to their descent from the original [[World History|Seven]]. This is not universally known — most of them simply grow up feeling slightly different without understanding why.
