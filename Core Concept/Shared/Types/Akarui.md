> _"They wear their power on the outside."_

## Short Definition

**Akarui** are hosts who integrate their [[Reiva]] and channel [[Pressure]] **outward** as a [[Manifestation]]. Their power is _external_ — projected from the self into the world. The Akarui path values trust, discipline, and shared control.

> **Akarui are External. [[Weaver|Weavers]] are Internal.**

---

## How They Work

### Pressure Resolution

When a [[Kyumin]] first resolves Pressure and the Reiva awakens, those who **externalise** — who release pressure outward rather than turning it back on themselves — become Akarui.

This isn't just a combat style. It is a psychological orientation: Akarui face outward. They project. They defend others by putting their power between a threat and the people they protect.

### Luz Channeling

- Akarui channel [[Luz]] from **inside themselves → outward into the world**.
- This contrasts with Weavers (Luz into the body) and [[Buki]] users (Luz into objects).
- The cleaner and more resolved their Pressure, the more efficient and precise their Luz output.

---

## Manifestation

Every Akarui has a **[[Manifestation]]** — a named, unique signature ability tied to their psychology and Pressure history.

- Visually distinct and personally meaningful.
- **Evolves** as the host grows: resolving Pressure refines it; deepening Pressure escalates it dangerously.
- Stronger Manifestations require a stronger bond and greater emotional alignment.

## Classes

Akarui apply their [[Classes|affinity class]] directly through their Manifestation:

- **Vigor** — physical augmentation: speed, strength, durability.
- **Pulse** — energy shaping: beams, shields, projectiles.
- **Forge** — creating Luz constructs and solid manifestations.

---

## Drawbacks

- Overuse of [[Luz]] or an unstable bond can cause **berserk states** — the Reiva seizes control.
- Memory loss during Reiva-dominant episodes.
- Pushing the bond beyond its current stage risks [[Kukai]]-adjacent corruption.

---

## Narrative Role

Akarui represent **outward mastery and protective instinct**. They are the visible defenders — the ones whose power is legible to others, which comes with both strength and burden. They are expected to act. They are expected to be strong.

This expectation is, itself, a Pressure source.
