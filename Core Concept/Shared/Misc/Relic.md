# Relic

## Short Definition

A **Relic** is the indestructible, preserved white body part that remains after a [[Kukai]] is killed and its white part has been surrounded by [[Kyumin]] energy over decades. They are rare, unnaturally white, and always carry the emotional memory of the human who became the Kukai.

---

## Properties

- **Physically indestructible** — no known force can destroy a Relic. They outlast everything around them.
- **Unnaturally white** — they stand out visually against any environment, as if the corruption never reached them.
- **Holds residual Pressure memory** — a Relic carries an imprint of the original host's Pressure. It does not grant power immediately, but it acts as a catalyst.

---

## How a Relic Forms

1. A [[Kukai]] is killed.
2. The white body part — the one part that never corrupted — remains.
3. Over decades, as [[Kyumin]] live near it and their ambient [[Luz]] surrounds it, the part crystallises into a Relic.
4. The longer it sits, the stronger the imprint it carries.

---

## Usage of Relics

Each Relic contains one ability derived from the pressure imprint of the human before they became [[Kukai]] this ability can be activated by the chip contained in humans ([[Human Progression]])
While this does not use up the Relics Power, it does have a cooldown between usages



---

## Signature Part as Indicator

The body part that became the Relic often suggests what kind of Pressure the original human carried:

|Part|Pressure Suggestion|
|---|---|
|Hand|Action, guilt, something done or left undone|
|Eye|Seeing, knowledge, something witnessed and never processed|
|Wing|Freedom, escape, the desire to leave|
|Crown|Pride, expectation, the weight of being seen as more than human|
|Jaw|Words unsaid, refusal, denial of what was|
|Tongue|Offers made, bargains struck, promises traded away|
|Heart|Peace made too early, surrender, what was let go|

This is not deterministic — it is thematic. A new host touched by a hand Relic may trend toward action-based resolution, but they are not locked into it.

---

## Narrative Importance

- Relics explain why awakenings cluster near ruins, coastlines, and isolated areas — that's where old Kukai were killed and forgotten.
- They are rare and valuable: black markets, cults, and the [[Tenebri]] organisation all have interest in them.
- A Relic is the only visible, permanent mark a [[Kukai]] leaves on the world after death. It is grief made indestructible.
