# Misc

Index of miscellaneous mechanics that don't belong cleanly in the power system or entity categories.

## Files

- [[Bond Stages]] — the three milestones of host-Reiva cooperation: Unstable (all-or-nothing), Zanshin (~75/25 split), Union (~50/50 true partnership). Affects all combat and narrative progression.
- [[Drift]] — environmental Pressure imprinting; areas and objects absorb the emotional history of the people who lived there; affects Kukai attraction and Reiki power on home ground.
- [[Extractors]] — Gear District firearms that force Awakening (Ratio Bullet) or trigger Overdrive (7-minute full-power state followed by days of inactivity); five generations with varying stats.
- [[Relic]] — the indestructible white remnant left when a Kukai dies; carries Pressure memory; catalyses Awakening; usable by Chipped humans.
