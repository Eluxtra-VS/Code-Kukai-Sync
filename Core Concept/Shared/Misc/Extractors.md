# Extractors

## Short Definition

**Extractors** are manufactured firearms that interface directly with [[Rei]]. They can force [[Awakening]] through Ratio Bullets or trigger a temporary state called **Overdrive**.

They are manufactured in the [[Gear District]] and sold through controlled channels, but they are not easy tools. Owning one is not the same as using one effectively.

> In the narrative they are known as the **"3-E's"** because they **Extract Reiva, Extract Mind, Extract Life**.

---

## Access and Usability

Extractors are confusing in-world because they are both commercial technology and specialist technology.

- They can be manufactured and sold.
- Most people cannot use them well.
- They require enough Rei awareness to input the correct energy and survive the feedback.
- They require some understanding of [[Kuro]] for advanced use, which means many users can operate them only partially.
- Cheap or early-generation Extractors are dangerous enough that many people avoid them even when they can access one.
- High-end Extractors are controlled by wealth and politics, especially in the [[Sovereign's Crown]].

This is why Extractors can exist publicly or semi-publicly without making Kuro common knowledge. Many people know Extractors do something supernatural; far fewer understand the Luz/Kuro ratio behind them.

---

## Manufacture and Distribution

- Produced in the **[[Gear District]]**.
- Generations 1-3 are available at varying levels of access depending on plate, class, and institutional connection.
- Generation 4 is black market only.
- **Generation 5 is distributed exclusively in the [[Sovereign's Crown]]**.
- They are largely inaccessible to people in the **[[Tidal Plate]]**, which is part of why raiding a factory becomes a story arc.

---

## Function 1 - Forced Awakening

The **Ratio Bullet** is a specialised round containing a perfect equilibrium between [[Luz]] and [[Kuro]]: the ratio that manifests naturally at the moment of genuine Awakening.

Natural [[Awakening]] requires:

- A catalyst.
- A genuine [[Pressure]] event.
- The [[Reiva]] choosing to activate.
- A first resolution that reveals Type.

The Ratio Bullet bypasses this. It reads the person's psychological state at the moment of impact, selects Type based on what the person believes their resolution would be in that exact moment, and heals the wound if the process works.

**The risk:** If the person has no resolve - no answer to how they would face their Pressure - the bullet finds nothing to anchor to. The wound does not heal. It is, functionally, suicide.

| Generation | Awakening Capability |
|---|---|
| 1st | Cannot reliably awaken; not designed for it |
| 2nd | Possible but unlikely and dangerous |
| 3rd+ | Guaranteed with Ratio Bullets if the target has sufficient resolve |

---

## Function 2 - Overdrive

**Overdrive** is a temporary state where normal restrictions on [[Rei]] use are removed.

- **Duration:** 7 minutes.
- **Aftermath:** The user is rendered inactive for days to weeks in a coma-like recovery state.

During recovery, the Reiva often becomes conscious before the host is fully active. This creates a rare communication window where the host and Reiva can speak without combat, crisis, or daily distraction.

### Extractor Method

The user inputs a quarter of their own Luz and a quarter of their own Kuro into the Extractor. The gun converts this into the perfect ratio and triggers Overdrive. Only the user's own energy works; another person cannot trigger Overdrive through the gun on their behalf.

### Manual Method

The user manually achieves the perfect Luz/Kuro ratio themselves. This requires extreme mastery and usually only happens near death. It is rarer, harder, and more dangerous than the Extractor method.

## Story Role

- **Factory raid arc:** Kaito's group raids a Gear District facility to acquire an Extractor.
- **Ethical weight:** Awakening that can be forced, bought, and manufactured asks what Awakening means.
- **Access disparity:** The Crown has the best tools. The Tidal Plate, full of high-potential descendants, has almost nothing.
- **Kuro reveal:** Extractors become one route by which the cast realises there is another half of Rei they were never taught.
