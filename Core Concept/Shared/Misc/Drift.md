# Drift

## Short Definition

**Drift** is the environmental imprinting of local [[Pressure]] patterns onto places and objects over time through [[Kukai]] and [[Kyumin]].

---

## How It Works

- [[Kukai]] / [[Kyumin]] who had high contents of previous [[Pressure]] leave imprints and this influences Reiva who resonate in that general area

---

## Effects

### On Areas

- Neighbourhoods become known for particular types of awakenings — certain Pressure tendencies cluster by location.
- High Drift areas **lure [[Kukai]] away** from unawakened [[Kyumin]] elsewhere; Kukai are drawn to dense Luz concentrations.
- Long-settled Drift zones can make [[Reiki]] extraordinarily powerful on their home ground (see [[Reiki]] — Blame stage).

## Story / Gameplay Use

- Drift neighbourhoods function as **thematic districts** — the emotional landscape of a place is legible before anyone explains its history.
- Drift explains why certain areas have more awakenings, similar-looking Relics, or draw Kukai at unusual rates.
