# Kukai

> _"Worse than death. They know exactly what they're doing."_

## Short Definition

A **Kukai** is a human–Reiva bond that has failed and become corrupted — a warped, predatory being that retains complete consciousness and memory, but exists inside a collapsed emotional state they cannot exit. They are not mindless. They are trapped.

---

## Appearance

- **Silhouette:** Almost humanoid. Mostly dark — the corruption spreads across their entire form as a physical expression of the collapsed Rei state.
- **Signature White Part:** One body part remains unnaturally white. This is the only part that resists corruption and cannot be changed.
- After death, the white part is surrounded by Kyumin energy over decades and crystallises into an indestructible Relic.
- **Shapeshifting:** Kukai can assume any form — any person, any creature, any shape that serves the current objective. The shapeshifting is total and accurate. The white part cannot change. It is the only tell.

---

## The Consciousness Problem

Kukai are fully aware. This is the specific horror the story does not let the audience look away from.

They remember everything. They know who they were. They know who you are. They know exactly what they are doing to you and they cannot stop themselves from doing it — not because they lack will, but because the Pressure that was never resolved has become the entire architecture of their existence. The self is still present. It is not in control.

A Kukai wearing the face of someone you knew is not an imitation. It is that person, behind a wall of collapsed Pressure, unable to stop the version of themselves that is going to hurt you.

A recently turned Kukai — days, weeks, perhaps a few months after transformation — can articulate what happened to them with complete accuracy. They can explain the Code. They can describe the exact moment the Pressure passed the point of no return. They can ask you for help in language that is coherent and genuine and still hurt you before the conversation ends because the hunger returned.

Old Kukai have compressed down further. The coherent windows are shorter. What surfaces in them is not explanation anymore. Just grief. A look in the eyes that is not hunger, lasting a moment, and then gone.

---

## Coherent Window Stages

*(Canon added 2026-08-24.)* Window decay is not random — it progresses through recognisable stages. Writers should place any given Kukai at a known stage; the stage governs what scenes are possible.

| Stage | Windows | What surfaces | Story use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lucid** | Frequent, minutes long | Full memory, accurate self-explanation, coherent requests for help — and genuine danger when hunger returns | Recently-turned characters; the horror of being asked for help by someone who means it |
| **Flickering** | Regular but shortening | Alternation: explanation fragments mid-sentence; identity holds, continuity does not | Rescue-attempt windows; communication that almost lands |
| **Grief-only** | Rare, seconds long | No explanation left — only affect: grief, recognition, a look that is not hunger | Tragedy beats; Hitori's windows live here |
| **Hollow** | Effectively none | Nothing surfaces; the shape is pure collapsed Pressure with no one visible inside | End-state; the reason loss is accepted as permanent |

Stage regression is not possible by natural means. See [[Warden]] for the single contested exception — which stabilises a window; it does not rewind a stage.

---

## The Shapeshifting

The shapeshifting is not a separate ability. It is the natural expression of a being whose Rei is no longer structured around a stable identity. A Kukai does not hold one shape because there is no single authentic shape left to hold. They become whatever the situation requires with the same ease a person assumes posture — instinctive, below conscious thought.

They can hold a human form indefinitely. They can hold a specific person's form — voice, mannerisms, remembered details — as long as maintaining it does not interfere with the hunger. When the hunger peaks, maintenance becomes expensive and the form slips. This is the moment most reveals happen — not through clever detection but through a moment where the hunger was too strong to keep the face on.

The white part is always present. A Kukai that has studied its own tell learns to keep it concealed — sleeves over a white hand, hair over a white patch of scalp. The concealment is imperfect under sustained observation. It is perfect under casual attention, which is what most people give.

## Classes — Locked to Kuro

Kukai retain their original Class permanently expressed through Kuro. The Class does not change. The expression does.

| Original Class | Kukai Expression |
|---|---|
| **Vigor** | Reverberating force that carries Kuro through the target — Rage, Fear, or Sorrow installed at the point of impact and through every subsequent shockwave. |
| **Pulse** | Kuro discharged as immediate precise strikes. No buildup. No saturation. Arrives exactly where it is aimed and installs the corresponding negative emotion in the target. |
| **Forge** | Constructs of solidified Kuro — structures that carry the emotional weight of whatever collapsed. A Forge Kukai's constructions are not neutral objects. They feel like the thing that broke the person. |
| **Veil** | The condition still applies, but what happens when it is met is not adjustment — it is breakage. A Veil Kukai finds the thing a person is most desperately not feeling and makes them feel all of it at once, at full accumulated intensity. |

## Ori — The Companion Case

Ori demonstrates that transformation does not necessarily eliminate the person. It redirects them. What remains can be compressed and hungry and still distinctly itself — with opinions, preferences, the capacity to form genuine bonds, and an interior life that the world largely refuses to acknowledge exists.

The world treating Kukai as pure predators is an institutional position that benefits from not being complicated. Ori complicates it. The story can decide how far to take that complication.

---

## Formation

1. **Catastrophic Bond Breakage** — a severe rupture between host and Reiva that neither survives intact.
2. **Refusal to Resolve Pressure** — chronic suppression past a critical threshold, leading to complete psychological collapse.

Transformation is never instant. It is the slow result of a direction maintained for long enough that there is no other direction left.

---

## Death and Pressure Transfer

*(Canon added 2026-08-24 — user-directed.)*

When a Kukai dies, everything it was does not simply end. If a human who is **not yet Kyumin** touches the body at or near the moment of death, the Kukai's accumulated Pressure transfers into them at **amplified** strength — years of unresolved feeling, compressed and collapsing, arriving all at once in a person whose own Reiva has not yet opened.

The consequences:

- **Awakening becomes harder, not easier.** Awakening requires resolving one's own Pressure; the recipient now carries their own unresolved feeling *plus* an inherited archive that was never theirs. The debt compounds before the account is even opened.
- The transfer needs touch at death. Distance, barrier, or Reiva already bonded (awakened hosts) blunts or blocks it.
- The inherited Pressure decays only through genuine processing — honest resolution of feelings the recipient never lived. It cannot be suppressed away; suppression feeds it.
- This is why people do not touch the dead. It is why cradling a transformed loved one as they die is an act with a cost the world never explains.

Relics are the residue of the same principle: after death, the white part crystallises over decades, still humming faintly with the person's unresolved emotion. An economy exists around objects like this — which means grief has a market somewhere.

## Witnessing

*(Canon added 2026-08-24.)* There is no contract system with Kukai. No pacts, no bargaining, no summoning — treating trapped people as counter-parties commodifies them, and the story refuses that move.

What exists instead is narrower and harder: **Witnessing**. A conscious person who voluntarily opens themselves to a Kukai's coherent window — holding contact through it without flinching, without trying to fix or flee — can briefly **stabilise** the window. The mechanism runs on the same rule as death-touch: contact moves Pressure both ways. In a witnessed window, part of the collapsed Pressure transmits back *out* into the witness.

The costs are real and symmetrical:

- The witness temporarily carries what they received; it decays only through genuine processing, like any Pressure.
- Stabilisation lasts moments to minutes. It does not rewind stages, cure anything, or create control.
- A failed Witnessing — flinching, forcing, pitying — can accelerate collapse instead.

Witnessing is communion, not contract: mutual risk taken voluntarily, with no power gained over the Kukai. It is rare, dangerous, and the only mechanism by which the story lets someone reach toward the transformed without a weapon. Ori is the case that proves personhood persists long enough for it to matter.
