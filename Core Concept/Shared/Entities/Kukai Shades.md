## Short Definition

**Kukai Shades** are corrupted forms that exist within the [[Oscuro]] — they are not full [[Kukai]] in the human world. They are the twisted residue of [[Reiva]] and bonds that failed, or entities produced by prolonged exposure to Oscuro corruption.

---

## Key Facts

- Occupy the **lower, toxic floors** of the Oscuro, far from the [[Luz Tree]].
- Their presence **accelerates the decay** of nearby, newly-born Reivas — creating a dangerous clock for every Reiva that lingers.
- They are not rational partners; they are **pure corruption**, pushing their environment toward predation and apathy.

---

## Impact on the Oscuro

- Reivas that linger near Kukai Shades become **tainted**: they either wither or awaken into a host already unstable and corrupted.
- Kukai Shades are the primary reason Reivas **must leave the Oscuro quickly** after birth.
- Their presence creates a hard survival pressure: _find a vessel or be consumed_.

---

## Role in the Human World

- **Watchers:** Kukai Shades observe the physical plane and wait for opportunities — specifically targeting unawakened [[Kyumin]] whose Luz has not yet been stabilised by a bond.
- **Strike vector:** When the conditions are right, they attempt to corrupt a Kyumin before awakening can occur, dragging them toward a [[Kukai]] transformation.
- **Environmental leak:** Their corruption bleeds outward from the Oscuro into areas of high emotional density — places where [[Pressure]] has gone unresolved for generations.

---

## Story Use

- The **"poisoned floor"** of the Oscuro — effective atmospheric foreshadowing of what lies beneath.
- They explain the urgency of Reiva bonding and why delay between birth and vessel is dangerous.
- Can be referenced as the unsettling "dark pull" some [[Kyumin]] sense without knowing what it is.
- They are the hidden explanation behind the legendary night when [[Kukai]] attacked across all plates simultaneously: a mass Kukai Shade event pushed to the surface.
