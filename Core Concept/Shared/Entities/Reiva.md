## Short Definition

A **Reiva** is a sentient, metaphysical fragment of spiritual energy born from a falling leaf of the [[Luz Tree]] in the [[Oscuro]]. Reiva are the spiritual companions that bond to humans, enabling awakening and access to [[Rei]] — and through it, [[Luz]] and [[Kuro]].

---

## Birth & Origin

- Born the moment a **leaf falls from the Luz Tree**.
- Once born, a Reiva searches for a compatible human vessel — remaining in the Oscuro corrodes them. (See [[Kukai Shades]].)

---

## Why They Inhabit Humans

- The Oscuro gradually **drains a Reiva's Rei**. Inhabiting a human anchors them to the physical plane and preserves their essence.
- A Reiva's **survival depends on a vessel**. Without one, it withers and risks becoming a [[Kukai Shades]].

---

## The Sixth Sense

- Reiva can **sense developing humans**: they detect a forming heart in an embryo and later detect when a brain has developed enough structure to host consciousness.
- When the threshold is reached, the Reiva can **finalise the bond** and enter the vessel.

---

## Dormancy & Observation

After entering a living host, the Reiva is **mostly dormant**:

- It observes the human's life and learns the host's psychological patterns.
- It waits for the right trigger — a major emotional event, contact with a [[Relic]], or the resolution of [[Pressure]] — to awaken actively.
- Some Reiva deliberately delay awakening until the host has sufficient emotional clarity.

> _Kaito Amori's Reiva is an example of a long-dormant observer — it waited until Kaito had accumulated enough experience to make awakening meaningful._

---

## Rei Relationship

- A Reiva's lifeforce **is** [[Rei]].
- When bonded, Rei becomes **shared**: the Reiva's lifeforce and the host's vitality intertwine.
- Rei expresses as [[Luz]] through positive emotional states and as [[Kuro]] through negative ones.
- If the Reiva's Rei is completely drained (through overuse or corruption), the host dies.
- Destroying the host can equally doom the Reiva.

---

## Death & Consequence

> A Reiva is an extension of the soul in this system.

|Event|Consequence|
|---|---|
|Complete Rei drain|Host dies; Reiva dissipates|
|Host death|Reiva is likely doomed|
|Bond failure / corruption|Risk of [[Kukai]] transformation|

---

## Story Hooks

- Reiva sometimes delay awakening until the host has emotional clarity — this explains characters who exhibit sensitivity but have not yet awakened (e.g., Kaito Amori's chronic low growls around him).
- A Reiva's personality and accumulated observation shapes the bond's character and the host's [[Manifestation]] or [[Weaver|Thread]].
