# Locations

Index of all locations in Code: Kukai. Read in this order for world context: World History → Tidal Plate → then the rest.

## Files

- [[Tidal Plate]] — home of Kaito's group; Tenebri descendants' exile island; high Kyumin concentration; Drift: grief and quiet endurance.
- [[Sovereign's Crown]] — wealthiest plate; controls Extractor access and institutional knowledge; the authority behind the K.E.F.
- [[Savor Central]] — food and medicine production; warm culture; largely outside the conflict.
- [[Gear District]] — industrial plate; manufactures Extractors; politically neutral.
- [[The Calamity]] — perpetual no man's land; Tenebri faction base of operations; extreme Pressure saturation.
- [[Crash Region]] — historical name for The Calamity before the original Tenebri's rampage; origin of Jakar Offei's family culture.
- [[Oscuro]] — spirit plane where Reiva are born; layered abyss around the Luz Tree.
- [[Luz Tree]] — centre of the Oscuro; converts human Pressure into new Reiva as falling leaves.
- [[The Black Box]] — Ku Mochizuki's father's covert facility on the Tidal Plate; forced Awakening experiments on children; origin of Ku, Kashi, and the Black Box Test name.
