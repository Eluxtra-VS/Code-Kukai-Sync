# Sovereign's Crown

## Overview

The **Sovereign's Crown** is the wealthiest and most powerful plate: the centre of trade, scholarship, and institutional knowledge in the rebuilt world. If the [[Tidal Plate]] is where the unwanted were sent, the Crown is where the people who decided that live.

---

## Identity and Culture

**Rich and knowledgeable.** The Crown controls major trade networks and has accumulated the most surviving pre-war knowledge. Institutional confidence easily becomes condescension toward plates like the Tidal Plate.

**Extractor access.** The Crown is the primary distribution point for Extractor technology, manufactured in the [[Gear District]]. All five generations are accessible to the Crown through various channels.

**5th Generation exclusivity.** The 5th Gen Extractor is only distributed in the Sovereign's Crown. It is not available through black market, factory raid, or special arrangement elsewhere.

---

## Role in the Story

- The Crown represents access and gatekeeping.
- Their Extractor monopoly, especially the 5th Gen, is a direct structural disadvantage for the Tidal Plate.
- Their knowledge of the original Seven and the banishment may be more complete than anyone in the Tidal Plate suspects.
- The [[K.E.F.]] is sanctioned by the Crown, which means the institution supposedly protecting people from Kukai is answerable to the institution with the most to lose from people knowing the truth.
