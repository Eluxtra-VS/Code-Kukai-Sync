# The Calamity

> _"Nobody calls it the Crash Region anymore. The people who remember what it was are either dead or they don't talk about it."_

## What It Was

Before the Tenebri went rogue, this region was called the **Crash Region**.

It was not a place of violence. It was a place of sound — music that came from everywhere, fighting traditions that were passed down as carefully as language, a culture built on the idea that the body and the rhythm it moved to were the same thing. The Crash Region produced fighters who were also performers and performers who were also fighters, and the distinction between the two was never considered meaningful. You learned to move. You learned to make something beautiful while you moved. These were not separate lessons.

The fighting styles that originated here were not aggressive in the way the outside world later came to associate with the region. They were expressive. They read like conversation — two people in dialogue through motion, each responding to what the other said, the fight a kind of music in itself. People came from other plates to learn. Teachers from the Crash Region were sought specifically for what they could not teach anywhere else.

The music was inextricable from the fighting. The rhythms were the same. The way a body moved in combat and the way it moved in celebration drew from identical sources. Outsiders found this confusing. People from the Crash Region found the confusion confusing.

---

## What Happened

When the original Seven — who would become known as the Tenebri — lost control and turned on the world, the Crash Region was where much of the resulting violence concentrated. Whether because of the region's fighting culture making it a focal point, or because of deliberate choice, or because of geographical factors that no surviving record clearly explains — the area became a site of sustained catastrophe.

The evacuation was not orderly. People left with what they could carry. The music was not something you could carry. The fighting traditions went with the people who knew them, and those people scattered across plates that were not prepared to receive them and did not particularly want to.

The region was renamed. **The Calamity.** Not by the people who had lived there — they were gone. By the people who looked at what remained and needed a word for it.

The name erased the history as efficiently as anything else had. The Crash Region does not appear on current maps. The plate designations used by the Sovereign's Crown and the Gear District do not acknowledge what was there before. The Calamity is described in official documentation as a region of ongoing conflict with no stable governance and no prior cultural significance. The prior cultural significance was simply not recorded.

---

## What It Is Now

**The Calamity** is a no man's land. Factions, mercenaries, warlords, and the Tenebri organisation fight for territory, resources, and ideology in a cycle that has not stopped since the original evacuation. The infrastructure that exists is ruins. The borders shift depending on who is winning and where.

The **Tenebri organisation** operates within it — not out of desperation but by choice. The Calamity's constant conflict and extreme ambient Pressure serves them in ways that have not been fully explained. The region's Drift signature is volatile and dense — Rage, Desperation, the specific grief of cultural erasure. The Tenebri find this environment useful.

What the Tenebri find useful and what was destroyed to create it are not unconnected.

## The People Who Remember

The descendants of the Crash Region's population live primarily in the Northern Gear District — the region that absorbed the largest wave of evacuees. They brought the music. They brought the fighting styles. They kept them alive in households, in informal communities, in the specific stubbornness of people who know that what they carry exists nowhere else.

The Northern Gear District equivalent of Jamaican cultural texture carries the Crash Region's heritage in forms the Sovereign's Crown's official record does not acknowledge exist. This is not accidental preservation. It is intentional refusal to let the renaming be complete.

Offei Oshi's family is among these. His parents fled the Crash Region during the collapse. Everything he knows of it came through them — through music played in a household on a plate that was not home, through fighting forms taught in back rooms because the original context for them was gone.

## Drift Signature

Rage. Desperation. The specific weight of something that existed and was erased.

Relics found in the Calamity almost always reflect aggression-type Pressure — but also, occasionally, something else. Objects from before the evacuation that were left behind, seeded with decades of the culture that produced them, carrying a Rei signature that does not match the violence that came after. These objects are rare. Finding one feels wrong in a way that takes a moment to identify — the wrong kind of wrong. Not dangerous. Mournful.
