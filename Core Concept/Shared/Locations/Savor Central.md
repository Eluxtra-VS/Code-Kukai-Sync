## Overview

**Savor Central** is the plate that feeds the world — specialising in food production and medicine, with food being the dominant identity. It is the most immediately necessary plate in practical terms: every other plate depends on what comes out of here in some form, even if they won't always admit it.

---

## Identity & Culture

- **Food first.** Savor Central's culture is built around cultivation, preparation, and distribution. The best growers, brewers, cooks, and preservers in the rebuilt world are from here or trained here.
- **Medicine as extension.** The medicinal knowledge of Savor Central developed alongside the food culture — understanding what grows, what heals, what harms, and how to extract what's useful from the natural world. It is practical medicine rooted in botany and chemistry rather than technology.
- **Less militarised than most.** Savor Central has no reason to be a plate defined by conflict. What it has is too valuable to destroy, which gives it a kind of passive protection — and a kind of naivety about how other plates operate.
- **Warm and transactional.** Savor Central people tend to be hospitable by culture but sharp by experience. They know their product is needed. They negotiate accordingly.

---

## Role in the Story

- Savor Central is likely the source of medical supplies relevant to the cast — recovery from Overdrive, treatment of Thread debuffs, anything requiring sustained care rather than combat medicine.
- Its trade relationships with [[Sovereign's Crown]] and the [[Gear District]] give it a more connected position in the wider world than the [[Tidal Plate]], which is largely cut off.
- A character from Savor Central would carry a specific cultural texture: practical, grounded, aware of what keeps people alive in the most literal sense.
