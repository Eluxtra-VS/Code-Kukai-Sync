## Short Definition

The **Oscuro** is the spirit plane where [[Reiva]] are born. It is a layered, luminous abyss centred on the [[Luz Tree]] and populated by both nascent Reiva and corrupted entities called [[Kukai Shades]].

---

## Structure

The Oscuro is **layered** — not a single environment but a depth gradient that grows more hostile the further down you go:

| Layer                            | Character                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Upper levels** (near Luz Tree) | Relatively calm; newly born Reiva drift here; ambient [[Luz]] warmth; the closest the Oscuro gets to safe |
| **Mid levels**                   | Transitional; dimmer; the warmth fades; Reiva sense urgency here and begin searching for a vessel         |
| **Lower levels**                 | Toxic and is the [[Kukai Shades]] territory; any Reiva that lingers here begins to decay or corrupt       |

The upper levels are luminous and still. The lower levels are wrong in a way that is hard to articulate — not dark exactly, but _dim in the wrong places_, as if the light has learned to avoid certain depths.

---

## Energy Flow

The Oscuro is not sealed from the human world. Energy moves both ways:

- **Inward:** Human [[Pressure]] seeps through the boundary between the physical world and the Oscuro, feeding the [[Luz Tree]] and producing new Reiva as leaves fall.
- **Outward:** The corruption of the lower levels can leak outward — bleeding into areas where Pressure has gone unresolved for generations, making those places feel heavier, stranger, more prone to [[Kukai]] activity.

The Oscuro and the human world are in constant quiet exchange. Most humans never notice. [[Kyumin]] sometimes feel it as unease they can't name.

---

## Why It Matters

- Reiva must leave the Oscuro quickly after birth or be corrupted by [[Kukai Shades]]. The plane is not a home — it is a starting point that becomes a threat.
- The lower levels explain why Reiva search rather than wait: staying is not neutral. The alternative to finding a vessel is the floor.
- The urgency built into the Oscuro's structure is the reason bonds form at all — a Reiva that feels no pressure to leave would have no reason to anchor itself to a human.

---

## Imagery

The Oscuro should feel like being **deep underwater** — pressured, beautiful in the upper layers, progressively wrong as you descend. Use:

- Depth and the suggestion of further depth below.
- Dim light and drifting motes of Luz — present in the upper levels, fading in the mid, absent in the lower.
- Sound that carries strangely, or stops carrying at all.
- The sense that something is aware of you from below, even when nothing is visible.
