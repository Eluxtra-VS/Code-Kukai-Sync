> **Name collision note (2026-09-28):** this page is the *region*. For the awakening mechanic, see [[Black Box Test]]; for the facility, see [[The Black Box]]. The region took its name from the facility-era era of the Last War — see [[Crash Region]] for the full naming history.

## Overview

**The Calamity** is a no man's land — a region defined entirely by constant, ongoing conflict. It is not a nation in any functional sense; it has no stable government, no peace, no safe zones. It is the one place in the rebuilt world where the violence of the [[World History|Last War]] never stopped.

---

## Nature & Environment

- **Perpetual warfare.** Factions, mercenaries, warlords, and worse fight for territory, resources, and ideology in an endless cycle.
- **Destroyed infrastructure.** What was built has been unmade. Ruins are more common than standing structures.
- **Shifting borders.** The line of the Calamity expands and contracts depending on who is fighting and where. Neighboring plates maintain uneasy buffer zones.
- **No civilians by choice.** Anyone who ends up in the Calamity is either a fighter, a refugee fleeing something worse elsewhere, or someone who had no other option.

---

## Why It Exists

The Calamity is not simply the result of ordinary human conflict. The region sits on a convergence of old war damage, unresolved geopolitical hatred, and — though few would frame it this way — **extremely high ambient [[Pressure]]**.

Generations of unresolved trauma, loss, and rage have saturated the land. The [[Drift]] signature here is volatile: erratic, violent, and dense. [[Kukai]] are drawn to it. Awakening events here tend to be violent and unstable.

---

## Drift Signature

- **Rage, Desperation, Pride.**
- [[Relic|Relics]] found in the Calamity are almost always linked to aggression-type Pressure — hands built for destruction, eyes that saw too much.
- Awakening events here produce a high rate of **unstable first resolutions**, pushing new [[Akarui]] toward reckless power and new [[Weaver|Weavers]] toward catastrophic self-damage.

---

## The Tenebri Connection

The **[[Tenebri]] villain faction** is *rumoured* to operate within the Calamity — and the rumour is maintained by the Tenebri themselves. In truth they operate there **openly and by choice**: the constant conflict is both cover and power source. The gap between the rumour and the reality is deliberate deniability — inside the Calamity they make no effort to hide; outside it, nothing is confirmed. See [[Crash Region]] for the region's history and naming.

- Their presence is never confirmed by official sources. No government acknowledges them.
- They are referenced only in **shadow — rumour, warning, legend**.
- Those who have encountered them rarely report the meeting clearly.
- The Calamity feeds them. Its Pressure, Kuro saturation, and unending violence are not a problem for the Tenebri — they are the _point_.

> _It is said they could conquer any of the plates single-handedly, with ease. They choose not to. No one knows why yet._

---

## Story Role

- The Calamity functions as the **horizon of dread** in the story — something characters hear about before they ever approach it.
- It is the external manifestation of what happens when [[Pressure]] is never resolved — at civilisational scale.
- Eventual story beats may bring characters there; when they do, it should feel genuinely threatening. Not adventure-threatening. _Wrong_-threatening.
