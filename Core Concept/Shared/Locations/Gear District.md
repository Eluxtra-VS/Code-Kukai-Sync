## Overview

The **Gear District** is the industrial heart of the rebuilt world — the plate specialising in mechanics, engineering, and manufacture. If it exists as a functional piece of technology in this world, it was likely built here or built with parts that came from here.

---

## Identity & Culture

- **Builders and engineers.** The Gear District's identity is defined entirely by what it produces. Practicality is the cultural currency — ideas are only as good as what they can be made into.
- **Politically neutral by necessity.** The District sells to everyone. [[Sovereign's Crown]], [[The Calamity]], plates in between — the District maintains relationships that other plates wouldn't. This neutrality is partly principled and partly economic.
- **Knowledge without ideology.** The District understands _how_ things work. What they are used for is, officially, someone else's problem.

---

## Extractors

The Gear District **manufactures [[Extractors]]** — the firearms capable of forcing [[Awakening]] and triggering [[Extractors|Overdrive]].

Whether the District fully understands what Extractors do to [[Reiva]] and the people shot by Ratio Bullets is a question the story can sit with. They build the mechanism. The metaphysics were presumably handed to them as specifications.

---

## The Factory Raid Arc

Kaito's group targets a Gear District facility to acquire an Extractor. This is likely their first direct engagement with the institutional infrastructure of the wider world — not a Kukai, not a personal enemy, but a building full of something manufactured, guarded, and owned by people with resources they don't have.
