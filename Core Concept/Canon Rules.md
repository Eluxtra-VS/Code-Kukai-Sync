# Canon Rules

This file exists to keep future AI sessions aligned. If another file conflicts with this one, treat this file as the current story canon until the conflict is deliberately resolved.

## Story vs Game

- Story canon and game canon are separate.
- Story canon takes priority for narrative, character, lore, and emotional logic.
- Game systems may abstract or change Awakening, progression, or combat for playability.

## Timeline

- Present day is roughly one century after 2026, not 168 years.
- Use 96 years after 2026 as the working number unless a later file changes it.
- The original Seven ended the last world war and later went rogue.
- The current Tenebri are a successor faction shaped by the legacy of the original Seven. They are not automatically the same people as the original Seven.
- Daiki Amori was part of the current/successor Tenebri and left. He was not one of the original Seven.

## Geography Names

- The Calamity and the Crash Region are the same place.
- Crash Region is the old name.
- The Calamity is the current name, given after the region collapsed and its original culture was erased.

## Main Cast Status

- Kaito Amori starts Arc 1 as Kyumin and awakens during Arc 1.
- Mel Silva awakens during Arc 1 and follows the Reiki path.
- Aiden Takeda awakens during Arc 1 and follows the Akarui Forge path.
- Hitori Tanaka does not awaken and should not take the Black Box Test.
- Ku Mochizuki and Aoki Sora join the group mid-story. They are not part of the original four.

## Awakening And The Black Box Test

- The story version of Awakening is an emotional Pressure event and first resolution.
- The game version of Awakening is a separate gameplay abstraction.
- The Black Box Test is performed after Awakening to identify Type and train early control.
- Ku demonstrates the Black Box Test with Sora, then uses it for awakened members of the group.
- Hitori does not produce a "no result" scene because he is not tested.

## Power System

- There are only two reserves: Reserve and Core Reserve.
- Store is removed canon.
- Null is removed canon.
- Kuro is not universally unknown. It is hidden from most ordinary people and from the early main cast, but some trained awakened groups, elders, Tenebri, K.E.F. specialists, and Extractor engineers know it exists.

## Signature Names

- Kashi Kizu uses Fracture.
- Jakar Offei uses Faultline. Warden is not a separate signature — it is the permanent Kukai bond formed as Faultline's extreme application at a transformation Jakar directly caused.
- Kaito's ability is called Divergence for design purposes, but he has not named it in-story yet. Kaito is Weaver Vigor (was Veil) — Inadequacy is Vigor's desire, and his honest-focus requirement is an amplifier gate, not a Veil gate.
- Sora is Weaver Vigor, signature Mend (was Forge/Mending) — class follows desire, not function; Mend's destructive mode Unmake is gated behind self-blame.
- Mel's signature is Flare (was Hearth/Harbor), Reiki Pulse — a signal of attention that makes her seen; someone genuinely seeing her makes it a beacon.
- Niko cannot take Divergence until Kaito claims/names it in language within Niko's hearing.

## Signature Rules

- No character has more than one signature. A signature's most extreme application (e.g. Warden) is not a second ability.
- Conditions are a universal signature layer (perks/amplifiers/stabilizers). Veil is the only class where the condition is the entire mechanism: no effect until the condition is met, then absolute within scope.
- Veils should be rare; the cast should stay diverse across Vigor/Pulse/Forge/Veil.

## Dante And Judgement

- Judgement requires intentional harm and present guilt.
- The target must have knowingly harmed someone and still carry guilt, whether acknowledged or suppressed.
- Accidental harm alone is not enough.
- Remorseless harm alone is not enough unless guilt exists underneath denial.

## Kaito's Sin Mirror

- Kaito is not a Wrath mirror.
- Kaito's best sin mirror is inverted Pride / shame-pride. Reader-facing abstract noun: Inadequacy — the refusal to be seen incomplete.
- This is different from Aiden's Vainglory. Aiden performs pride because he lacks self-belief; Kaito wants to erase the version of himself he thinks is unacceptable.

## Ku-Clowns

- "Ku" in Ku-Clowns shortens Kukai, not Ku Mochizuki.
- The full meaning is Kukai-Clowns.
- Their purpose is to capture, study, and exploit Kukai. Their connection to the K.E.F. comes from that practical use.


## Alice Amori

- Alice Amori is Kaito's mother; previous name Sena is deprecated as an alias only.
- Alice has Brazilian texture: Portuguese appears in intimate/tired/angry moments, but avoid caricature.
- She withholds Daiki's history because she fears Kaito chasing his father and originally wanted him to stay below the Awakening threshold long enough to leave the Tidal Plate.
- Recommended reveal timing: early Arc 4 after Hitori's loss, producing temporary resentment before Kaito later understands the danger she was delaying.

## Kukai Progression (2026-08-24)

- Coherent windows decay through four stages: **Lucid → Flickering → Grief-only → Hollow**. Stage regression is not naturally possible; see `Shared/Entities/Kukai.md` for the stage table.
- **Death-touch transfer:** a non-Kyumin human who touches a dying/dead Kukai absorbs its accumulated Pressure at amplified strength. This makes Awakening harder, not easier. Decays only through genuine processing.
- **No contracts with Kukai.** The narrow permitted mechanic is **Witnessing**: voluntary contact holding a coherent window without flinching can briefly stabilise it, transmitting part of the collapsed Pressure back into the witness at mutual risk. Communion, never bargaining; stabilisation only, never cure or control.


## Terminology Standard (locked 2026-09-28)

One word per concept. All files must comply:

| Concept | Canonical word | Deprecated synonyms |
|---|---|---|
| Akarui / Weaver / Reiki | **Path** | Type, "awakened Types" |
| Vigor / Pulse / Forge / Veil | **Class** | Affinity, "Class Types" |
| Kyumin (pre-awakening) | **pre-awakening state** | unawakened Type |
| Reiki-as-Buki-variant | **Sub-Path** | Sub-Type |

- "Type" appears only in "Buki" contexts or quotes of old documents.
- "Affinity" never appears as a mechanical term; flavor use allowed in dialogue only.
- Awakened paths: **three** (Akarui, Weaver, Reiki-as-Sub-Path). Classes: **four** (Vigor, Pulse, Forge, Veil).


## Naming System (locked 2026-09-28)

Two registers, both canon:

| Register | Where it's used | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Technical** | Documents, Black Box system, clinical contexts, Sora's classifications | Externalizer, Internalizer, Displacer, **Ember** |
| **Folk** | Dialogue, regional speech, friend groups, culture | Reika (Tidal Plate), Luz, Kuro, and regional variants |

Rules:
- Technical names are canonical in lore/docs. Folk names are canonical in dialogue.
- Folk names need no etymology rules — they need a **namer and a moment** (who said it first, why it stuck). Regional variation is a feature: different plates may use different folk names for the same thing, and characters still understand each other.
- Old single-register collisions (Rei/Reiva/Reiki sharing a root) are resolved by register separation: folk names may sound alike; technical names share no roots.
