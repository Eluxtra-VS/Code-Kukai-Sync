# Character Pressure Matrix

**Scope:** Every currently documented named Code:Kukai character and major institution. Add a row before introducing a new named character into a scene, faction, power, or arc.

This is the **editable control sheet** for Code:Kukai characters. It translates the power system's central rule into story practice:

> **Pressure → coping behavior → first resolution → power expression → consequence → arc movement.**

Use two separate status fields consistently:

- **Canon status: LOCKED** — established in `Canon Rules.md` or confirmed in `memory.md`; do not change without a deliberate retcon.
- **Canon status: WORKING** — strong current direction; safe to revise during development.
- **Canon status: OPEN** — intentionally undecided; do not let a scene accidentally canonize it.
- **Production status: SCENE PROOF NEEDED** — the idea exists in a profile but needs a concrete scene that makes the reader feel it.

## How to Use This Sheet

Before writing any scene, answer five questions:

1. What Pressure is active before the scene?
2. What coping behavior does the character use instead of naming it?
3. What choice does that coping behavior force?
4. How does the power system reveal or punish the choice?
5. What is different afterward — even if the character refuses to admit it?

If the answer to question five is “nothing,” the scene probably needs an emotional turn or does not need to exist.

## Scene Proof Rule

A profile claim is not fully real until the reader sees it in at least three forms:

1. **Ordinary behavior** — how the character acts when no fight is happening.
2. **Pressure choice** — what they choose when the emotional cost becomes unavoidable.
3. **Power consequence** — how Rei, ability, bond, or transformation responds.

Characters who currently need the most scene proof:

- Mel's care landing inward.
- Ku's management failing in front of Kaito or Sora.
- Hitori choosing freedom once before becoming unable to choose anything.
- Alice's reveal and its aftermath.
- Seong's escalation problem.
- Jakar encountering genuine connection without manufactured suffering.
- Hyoga's fascination as morally consequential rather than merely eccentric.

## Edit Controls

When changing a character, update these fields together:

- Pressure / core word
- Default coping behavior
- Path / Class / signature
- Visual motif
- Arc movement
- Scene proof
- Related open decisions

Do not change a power in isolation. In Code:Kukai, a power change is also a psychology change, a scene change, and an arc consequence.
