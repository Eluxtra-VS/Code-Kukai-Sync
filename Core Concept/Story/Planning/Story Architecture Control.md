# Story Architecture Control

This is the **change-friendly master plan** for the Code:Kukai story. It separates what the story must preserve from what can be changed during development.

## Status Vocabulary

- **Canon status: LOCKED:** source-of-truth canon or a confirmed structural pillar.
- **Canon status: WORKING:** current recommended version; revise freely if the replacement is stronger.
- **Canon status: OPEN:** intentionally undecided; do not let a draft decide it accidentally.
- **Production status: SCENE CARD:** approved structure ready to script.
- **Production status: SCENE CARD NEEDED:** one scene concept exists; its card still needs drafting.
- **Production status: SCENE CARDS NEEDED:** multiple scene concepts exist; cards still need drafting.
- **Production status: SCENE CARD CLUSTER:** several irreversible turns are grouped and must be split before scripting.
- **Production status: ARC CLIMAX DESIGN NEEDED:** arc spine exists; climax still needs scene-level design.
- **Production status: SCENE CARDS CREATED:** cards exist; prose/script production has not begun.
- **Production status: MUST NOT BE ACCIDENTALLY RESOLVED:** intentionally protected open material.
- **Production status: DRAFT:** useful treatment material, not yet final.

## The Story Engine

Every major story movement should pass through this chain:

```text
Cultural misread of the Code
→ character-specific Pressure accumulates
→ coping behavior creates a choice
→ power system literalises the choice
→ consequence changes a relationship or objective
→ character is offered a more honest alternative
→ they accept, refuse, or arrive too late
```

The story does not need every character to resolve Pressure positively. It needs every major consequence to be emotionally legible.

## Arc 4 — Skeleton Queue

Arc 4 is not yet fully planned. The safest current sequence is:

1. **Aftermath of Hitori** — the group attempts routine and discovers routine was held together by him.
2. **Alice Kitchen Reveal** — truth arrives through domestic intimacy, not exposition spectacle.
3. **Kaito Pulls Away** — he treats the reveal as another example of being managed/edited.
4. **Daiki Evidence** — a concrete encounter or consequence shows Alice's fear was not abstract.
5. **Mel's Care Lands** — Kaito accepts support without converting it into a task.
6. **Kaito Reframes Alice** — anger remains; the moral picture becomes more complicated.
7. **Inheritance Decision** — Kaito chooses a next action with knowledge, not compulsion.

The exact evidence, Daiki's direct involvement, and whether Kaito approaches or avoids him remain OPEN.

## Endgame — Jakar / Ori Scene-Card Queue

The detailed sequence is in [[Jakar-Ori Endgame — Refusal, Isolation, and Death]]]. Preserve the following locked direction while scripting:

1. **The Refusal** — Ori refuses a Warden-directed action without breaking Warden.
2. **The Defeat That Does Not Free Anyone** — Jakar is defeated or contained; Ori is not used as the final weapon.
3. **The Quiet Release That Is Not a Release** — Jakar stops active pressure and communication; the permanent bond remains.
4. **The Last Coherent Window** — Ori's chosen isolation becomes unsustainable; no cure is discovered.
5. **The Death Coda** — Ori dies quietly; the survivors turn the loss into changed practice rather than a clean moral slogan.

Jakar's exact custody outcome, final observer, record medium, location, and death mechanics remain OPEN implementation details, but they must not contradict Warden's permanence or Ori's confirmed fate.

## Power-System Continuity Rules for Story Scenes

1. **Pressure is not a number in prose.** Show it through behavior, sensory distortion, speech, body language, and power quality.
2. **First resolution reveals tendency.** Do not treat Awakening as a menu choice in story scenes.
3. **Class expresses desire.** Vigor = enough, Pulse = reach, Forge = build, Veil = control conditions.
4. **Path changes direction.** Akarui externalises; Weaver internalises; Reiki displaces into Anchors.
5. **Power cannot erase emotional consequence.** A win does not resolve Pressure unless the character actually processes something.
6. **Kuro is strategically effective but emotionally costly.** Its reveal must change the meaning of earlier fights, not merely add a stronger attack type.
7. **Every Veil scene identifies condition, scope, and cost.** Avoid “it works because the character is powerful.”
8. **Only Reserve and Core Reserve exist.** Do not reintroduce Store or Null.
9. **Hitori is never Black Box tested.** His omission is a deliberate dramatic fact.
10. **Divergence is unnamed in-story until the claiming scene.** Documentation can use the design name; dialogue cannot prematurely canonise it.

## Change Protocol

When you want to change something:

1. Edit the relevant row in `Character Pressure Matrix.md` or this file first.
2. Mark the old item `RETIRED` rather than silently deleting the reasoning.
3. Check which scenes, powers, relationships, and payoffs depend on it.
4. Make the smallest coherent change across those files.
5. Run `/canon-check` before treating it as story canon.

This keeps experimentation safe. You can change the story without losing why the previous version worked.

## Quality Gate

An arc is not ready to script until every scene has:

- an emotional center;
- a before/after Pressure movement;
- a power-system or world consequence where appropriate;
- a visual motif;
- a specific setup or payoff;
- a stronger end image than start image;
- no contradiction with `Canon Rules.md`;
- a clear status: LOCKED, WORKING, or OPEN.
