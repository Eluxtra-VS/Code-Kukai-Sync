# Chapter 1 — "The Plate" (Full Script)

**Format:** Manga script — panel-by-panel with dialogue and visual direction.
**Pages (approx.):** 42
**POV:** Primarily Kaito Amori; rotating through Mel, Aiden, Hitori, Ku, Sora
**Setting:** East Tidal Plate, summer holidays

---

## SEQUENCE 1 — THE OPENING (Pages 1–8)

---

### Page 1

**Panel 1 (Full-page spread)**
Aerial view of a large central road — a working dock town in motion. People crossing between stalls and storefronts. Fog thinning in the morning light. The rhythm of a community that was built to be a punishment and became a home instead. On the right, past a fence, the ocean glints through the haze.

**CAPTION (upper left, small type):**
*Humans are expressive creatures.*

**Panel 2 (Bleed, bottom third of page)**
Two kids shoving each other playfully in the street. One laughing, mouth wide open. The other mid-shove, caught in motion. Unguarded. Unfiltered.

**CAPTION:**
*But in this day and age we are told to bottle all of it up.*

---

### Page 2

**Panel 1 (Wide)**
A crowded street corner. Adults passing. Faces neutral. Hands in pockets. Conversations happening but mouths barely moving. The contrast with the kids from page 1 is deliberate — same street, different species.

**CAPTION:**
*To keep it to ourselves.*

**Panel 2**
A woman glances at her reflection in a shop window. Adjusts her expression before turning away. A micro-correction. She has done this a thousand times.

**CAPTION:**
*That suppressing your emotions is a sign of power.*

**Panel 3 (Narrow, vertical)**
A boy — maybe seven — crying silently. His mouth is closed. His shoulders shake. He is not making a sound. An adult hand rests on his shoulder, not comforting, just... there. The boy wipes his face and stops.

**CAPTION:**
*This is what is called the "Code."*

---

### Page 3

**Panel 1 (Full page)**
**HARD CUT.** 

A Kukai. Close enough to fill the frame. Head slowly tilted to the right — the angle wrong, the motion too smooth. The face is not quite human. The eye closest to the reader is a piercing blue — not glowing, just... present. Unblinking.

**Panel 2 (Below, inset)**
Pull back. First-person perspective from the ground, looking up. Grass in the foreground. The sky dark above. The Kukai's form silhouetted against it. The feet of the person we're viewing through are splayed on the grass — as if they've just tripped. One shoe half off.

---

### Page 4

**Panel 1 (Wide)**
The Kukai rears back. A limb — too long, too jointed — raises against the dark sky. The motion is predatory but not fast. Deliberate. Like it knows the person on the ground cannot move.

**Panel 2 (Close-up)**
The blue eye. Filling the frame. The pupil dilates.

**Panel 3 (Full page)**
**BLACK.**
Complete, silent black.

---

### Page 5

**Panel 1**
VOICE (from darkness, muffled, like sound underwater):
**KAITO.**

**Panel 2**
Still black. The voice is closer now:
**Kaito.**

**Panel 3**
**KAITO GET UP!**

**Panel 4 (Half page)**
A head being lifted from arms — first-person perspective, the camera rising as if someone is physically pulling the viewpoint up. We see: a boy's face, close. Messy dark hair. Steady eyes. To the right of him, a chain-link fence. Past it: the ocean. Fog lifting.

This is HITORI TANAKA.

**HITORI:**
Kaito, come on, I made a bunch of food for the meetup we agreed on. The others are already there. Hurry!

### Page 6

Reverse angle. We see Kaito from Hitori's perspective — still on the bench, blinking awake, disoriented. Sweat on his forehead. His eyes are two different colours: left brown, right blue. The blue one catches the first break of sunlight through the fog. 

**Panel 2 (Detail)**
Hitori's hand closes around Kaito's. 

**Title Card (top left of panel, small):**
**East Tidal Plate**

**Panel 3**
Kaito on his feet now, rubbing his eyes with his free hand. Hitori already half-turned, ready to move.

**KAITO:**
I'm coming.

**Panel 4 (Narrow, bottom of page)**
The bench, now empty. The fog beginning to clear. In the distance, the sound of dock bells.

The Tidal Plate docks at morning. Boats at rest. Crates stacked. Workers moving in the rhythm of a place that runs on muscle and salt. The ocean stretches to the horizon. On the far edge of the frame — barely visible — the silhouette of another plate, distant and clean-lined. The Crown's territory.

## SEQUENCE 2 — THE DOCK MORNING (Pages 9–16)

---

### Page 9

**Panel 1 (Wide)**
The docks. Fog lifting. Alice Amori hauling a net onto the pier. The morning catch glints silver in wooden crates beside her. Kaito approaches at a half-jog.

**KAITO:**
Everything okay? You should of asked me for help. If I didn't oversleep I would of been here before.

**ALICE:**
You didn't oversleep. You don't sleep enough to oversleep. 

She says it while giving him a stern look.

**Panel 2**
Kaito already lifting a crate — too heavy, stacked wrong. He adjusts without pausing.

**KAITO:**
What do you need me to—

**ALICE:**
Kaito. What are you doing. Put that down. I don't need help. 

**Panel 3**
Kaito is already moving. He picks up two crates before Alice can suggest one at a time.

**ALICE:**
You know, if you stack them on top of each other and use both hands it's ea—

**KAITO:**
Got it.

### Page 10

**Panel 1**
Alice's face. She watches him for a bit longer than the conversation requires. Her mouth opens slightly — she almost says something — and closes. The withheld information is visible but unreadable. She does not say what she is thinking.

**Panel 2**
Kaito, walking away with the crates, unaware of the look.

**Panel 3 (Wide)**
The east pier. Kaito sets the crates down outside cold storage. A dock worker — grizzled, mid-forties — approaches to talk to Kaito

**DOCK WORKER:**
You should be worried about yourself. Your mother is fine. Were you not taught better?

He stops. His eyes catch on Kaito's face.

Close-up: Kaito's eyes. Right blue. Left brown. The light catches the blue one differently.

**Panel 2**
The dock worker's face. He *looks*. Then he looks away — a deliberate motion, consciously breaking eye contact. Then he looks *again*. The double-take is slow enough that Kaito cannot possibly miss it.

**Panel 3**
Kaito's face. Completely neutral. He clocks both looks — the first one, the second one, the space between them. His expression does not change.

**Panel 4**
The dock worker coughs as Kaito picks up another crate.

**Panel 5**

**ALICE:**
Kaito. Its fine. Put it down. You already stand out enough.

**KAITO**:
Sorry.

Kaito standing alone on the pier. The ocean behind him. The blue eye catching the light. His face has a frustated look.

**Panel 2 (Small, internal)**
A flash — the Kukai's blue eye from the nightmare. Just one panel. No transition. The association is made without commentary.

**Panel 3**
Kaito blinks. The flash is gone. He turns and walks back toward his mother.

### Page 15

**Panel 1**
AIDEN TAKEDA arrives like a truck — loud, gesturing, already mid-story before he's fully in the frame.

**AIDEN:**
SO BOOM — I gave him a hook to the jaw. BANG. The other 5 were all stunned. One came up and I got hit a *few* times but that's besides the point, I was dodging punches left and right—

**AIDEN:**

**MEL:**

---

### Page 16

**Panel 1**
Hitori walks back toward the table, cradling his hand.

**Panel 2**
He sits down. Mel puts a band-aid on the table in front of him without a word. No eye contact. Just the object, placed exactly where his hand is going to be.

**HITORI:**
Thanks a bunch.

**Panel 3 (Visual counterpoint — split page)**

*Left panel:* The dock worker, walking away. The Code spoken. "You won't be handed anything in life."

*Right panel:* Mel's band-aid, already on Hitori's hand. The Code contradicted. Care provided without being asked.

No commentary. No narration. The page makes the argument.

**Panel 4**
Kaito coughs — the kind of cough that precedes a question.

**KAITO:**
Hey guys — do you wanna go around looking for some abandoned places tomorrow?

Everyone turns toward him.

**KAITO:**
It's the summer holidays. So I think it'll be fun.

**Panel 5**
Mel nods. Aiden grins. Hitori shrugs — which for Hitori means yes. The response is unanimous without being coordinated.

## SEQUENCE 3 — YOSHI INTERLUDE (Pages 17–18)

---

### Page 17

**Panel 1**
The group is still gathered around the table. From the direction of the community centre, a man approaches — YOSHI KUSAKABE. Late twenties, maybe thirty. He carries a cardboard box against his hip — supplies for the docks, maybe, or something for the community centre's next event. He moves with the unhurried ease of someone who knows everyone here and is known in return.

**Panel 2**
Yoshi spots the group. His face shifts — not a performance, just warmth. The specific warmth of someone who genuinely likes people and has never been embarrassed about showing it.

**YOSHI:**
Kaito! Hitori! Good, you're both here — saved me a walk.

**Panel 3**
He sets the box down on the edge of their table. Inside: flyers for a community centre barbecue, a handful of small tools — the kind he's always giving away — and a packet of band-aids that he definitely bought himself and will never mention.

**YOSHI:**
These are the last of the summer flyers. If you see anyone who looks bored, hand one over. Attendance has been down and Mrs. Hirano keeps threatening to cancel the raffle.

**AIDEN:**
The raffle?! She can't cancel the raffle. I've been cultivating my ticket-pulling strategy for *months*.

**YOSHI:**
That's what I told her. The strategy alone deserves recognition.

Aiden beams. 

---

### Page 18

**Panel 1**
Yoshi turns to Mel and his tone shifts, just slightly. Not patronising. Attentive.

**YOSHI:**
Mel, I still have that weaving kit you asked about. The one with the better thread. It came in yesterday — I set it aside for you.

**MEL:**
Why you'd do that? You didn't have to you know.

He grins and then shakes his head and his smile snaps back to a normal 

**YOSHI**:
I just had some extra you know.

**HITORI**:
Uhm.. What the hell

**Panel 2**
Mel's hand moves to the bracelet on her wrist — the same weave pattern. She doesn't say anything. Her thumb traces the worn fibres. 

**Panel 3**
Yoshi fishes out the small steel looking ball with a pattern Kaito noticed earlier — the one that will sit on Kaito's desk, unused but never thrown away.

**YOSHI:**
Almost forgot. Kaito — you left this at the centre last week. I've been carrying it around hoping I'd run into you.

He hands it over. Kaito takes it, turning it over in his palm.

**KAITO:**
thanks man.

**YOSHI:**
Don't mention it. That's what I'm here for.

**Panel 4**
Yoshi picks up his box — lighter now — and turns toward the docks. At the community-centre doorway, his stepson has appeared. Yoshi rushes over to greet his son, He is old enough to understand the care in Yoshi's face and young enough not to know what to do with it.

**YOSHI:**
Barbecue's Saturday. You coming?

**STEPSON:**
Maybe. Don't save me a place.

**YOSHI:**
Okay.

The stepson takes one of the flyers anyway, folds it once without looking at the picture, and walks inside. The gesture is not rejection. It is not acceptance either.

**Panel 5**
Yoshi watches the doorway for one second longer than he should. Then he turns back toward the group

**KAITO**: 
You okay man? What was th-

**YOSHI**:
Don't you ask those sorts of things son.

**YOSHI:**
See you at the barbecue. Bring your appetites because i know you Aiden will. And Aiden — bring your strategy.

**Panel 6 (Bottom of page, wide)**
The group dispersing. Four figures moving in different directions. Yoshi's back retreating toward the dock workers. The folded flyer remains visible in his stepson's hand through the doorway. The fog has fully burned off. The sun is high. The day looks ordinary. It will not be.

## SEQUENCE 4 — THE NIGHT REVISION (Pages 19–22)

---

### Page 19

**Panel 1 (Full page)**
Kaito's bedroom ceiling. The room is dark. The only light is a thin strip of moonlight through the window. Kaito's eyes are open.

No dialogue. Just the ceiling. The silence.

---

### Page 20

**Panel 1**
Kaito's face, half-lit. His eyes — both of them — stare at nothing.

**Panel 2 (Memory fragment — small, bordered differently)**
The dock worker's face from earlier. Looking. Looking away. Looking again. The panel has no background — just the expression, suspended.

**Panel 3 (Memory fragment)**
Alice's mouth opening and closing. The thing she didn't say. The thing Kaito has been trying to fill in since it happened.

**Panel 4 (Memory fragment)**
Yoshi's face as he handed over the tool. Then the community-centre doorway: *Maybe. Don't save me a place.* The folded flyer in his stepson's hand. Yoshi smiling as if the answer is enough.

**Panel 5 (Memory fragment)**
Mel looking away after he questioned whether she'd eaten. The silence between them. The deflection.

**Panel 6**
Back to Kaito's ceiling. But now — overlaid on the dark — faint subtitles appear. Words he's rewriting. Dialogue he's correcting. They scroll across the ceiling like a teleprompter only he can see.

**The subtitles read:**
*"Sorry — I should've been there earlier. I know you went alone."*
*"My eyes? Yeah. Born with them. Don't worry about it."*
*"You haven't eaten. I can tell. Here — take some of mine."*
*"Thanks, Yoshi. For everything. For... noticing."*

**Panel 7**
Kaito closes his eyes. The subtitles fade. Nothing resolved. Nothing slept.

---

### Page 21

**Panel 1**
Kaito's hand, resting on his chest. His fingers twitch — the beginning of a motion he stops before it completes.

**Panel 2**
His face again. Eyes still closed. The revision has not stopped — it has just moved inward. The audience cannot see this version. They can only see the stillness.

---

### Page 22

**Panel 1 (Panel sequence — three narrow vertical panels)**

*Left:* The ceiling. Dark.

*Middle:* The ceiling. The first grey of pre-dawn.

*Right:* The ceiling. Light breaking through the window. The night is over.

**Panel 2**
Kaito's eyes, open. The blue one. The brown one. Both awake. They have been awake.

**Panel 3 (Wide, bottom of page)**
Kaito sits up. The room resolves around him — small, functional, a teenager's space. On his desk: Yoshi's tool. He reaches for it. Pauses. Puts it in his pocket instead.

Food.

### PAGE 34

Yoshi's face.

**Panel 4**

**Panel 2**

**MEL:**  
Yoshi...?

**KAITO (small):**  
No...**

## Chapter 2 Implications

Kaito's awakening is now deferred to Chapter 2. Specifically:
- **Rooftop crisis scene (Chapter 2, Sequence B):** Kaito awakens here — his right eye activates for the first time, the Weaver State fires. This is where he becomes what he is.
- **Black Box Test (Chapter 2, Sequence C):** Aiden, Mel, and Kaito are tested only after Awakening. Hitori is deliberately never tested.
- **Ku's pause:** When Ku sees Kaito hasn't awakened in Chapter 1, his reaction (longer pause, "something else is waiting") is foreshadowing. When Kaito DOES awaken in Chapter 2, Ku's reaction should pay this off — recognition, not surprise.
