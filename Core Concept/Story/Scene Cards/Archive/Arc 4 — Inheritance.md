These cards turn the current Arc 4 working direction into a scriptable sequence without deciding the open Daiki evidence, direct-contact timing, or Kaito's later naming scene.

## 1. The Routine Without Hitori

**Arc:** Arc 4 — Inheritance
**POV / Emotional Center:** Ensemble, with Kaito as the emotional hinge
**Pressure Movement:** Shock held in practical motion → routine exposes the absence → group admits they do not know what to do next
**Power-System Beat:** No new ability; existing bond/Pressure behavior reveals missing support structure
**Visual Motif:** Empty chair / unpassed object / anchor-shaped negative space
**Emotional Thesis:** The person who made everyone else feel supported can disappear before anyone realises support was reciprocal.

### Start Image
The group wakes in a space arranged around Hitori's habits: food portioned, tools aligned, one place already prepared.

### End Image
Someone asks where Hitori is, and nobody answers because the question has finally become real.

### Beat Breakdown
1. Each member performs a task Hitori used to do without noticing.
2. The attempts fail in small, ordinary ways rather than melodramatic collapse.
3. Mel tries to feed everyone; Kaito tries to organise; Aiden fills silence with performance; Ku leaves the room; Sora starts repairing a thing no one asked her to repair.
4. A practical clue confirms this is not ordinary lateness, while the story still withholds the full truth of his transformation.
5. The group realises their routines were not neutral; Hitori was carrying them.
6. No one says “we should have noticed.” The composition says it.

### Character Threads
- **Kaito:** Tries to solve absence as a problem, beginning his next self-correction spiral.
- **Mel:** Over-functions and excludes herself from care.
- **Ku:** Recognises a disappearance pattern and contains his knowledge.
- **Sora:** Repairs the room instead of tolerating the room as damaged.

### Must-Include
- Hitori's absence is felt through structure before it is explained through lore.
- Do not show the full wings yet.

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** Anchor imagery from Arcs 1–3; Hitori's role as group centre.
- **Sets up:** Alice's reveal, the need for truth, later Relic/freedom imagery.

### Notes
Canon status: LOCKED. Production status: NEEDS PROSE.

## 2. The Kitchen Table

**Arc:** Arc 4 — Inheritance
**POV / Emotional Center:** Kaito and Alice
**Pressure Movement:** Kaito arrives carrying grief and urgency → Alice recognises silence has become dangerous → both lose control of the careful domestic pattern
**Power-System Beat:** Kaito's Pressure destabilises attention; no new ability is required
**Visual Motif:** Food cooling untouched / hands busy until they stop / the table becoming distance
**Emotional Thesis:** Protection through silence can become another person deciding what truth you are allowed to survive.

### Start Image
Alice serves food automatically. Kaito sits but does not remove his coat.

### End Image
The table remains between them after the truth is spoken, larger than the kitchen.

### Beat Breakdown
1. Kaito comes home from the aftermath trying to appear functional.
2. Alice notices the performance immediately but does not challenge it with a lecture.
3. Kaito asks a practical question that is secretly about Hitori, the Crown, and whether adults have been hiding the map from him.
4. Alice realises that waiting longer would leave him vulnerable to someone else's version.
5. She tells him Daiki is alive, was Tenebri, left, and remains dangerous.
6. She tells him the blue right eye is connected to Daiki's history without turning the scene into a power-system dump.
7. Kaito hears the protection as management: another person editing what he is allowed to know.
8. Alice loses control briefly; Portuguese appears as intimacy and anger, never decoration.

### Character Threads
- **Kaito:** Feels his deepest wound mirrored by Alice's withholding: someone else decided the incomplete version of the truth was safer.
- **Alice:** Chooses truth while knowing it will damage the relationship.

### Must-Include
- Alice does not ask forgiveness immediately.
- Kaito is allowed to resent her.
- Daiki's danger is concrete enough to be frightening but not fully explained yet.

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** Alice's long silence, Kaito's eyes, Hitori's loss making secrecy unsustainable.
- **Sets up:** Kaito pulling away, Daiki evidence, inheritance choice.

### Notes
Canon status: LOCKED direction. Production status: NEEDS PROSE.

## 3. Kaito Pulls Away

**Arc:** Arc 4 — Inheritance
**POV / Emotional Center:** Kaito
**Pressure Movement:** Hurt seeks a clean verdict → Kaito turns Alice into a problem he can solve → the verdict fails to make him feel safer
**Power-System Beat:** Divergence's right-eye focus becomes unreliable when Kaito tries to revise the emotional meaning of the reveal
**Visual Motif:** Doorframes / edited messages / right eye off-centre in panels
**Emotional Thesis:** Turning betrayal into a simple verdict can feel like control while preserving the wound underneath.

### Start Image
Kaito writes a message to Alice, deletes it, and writes a more accurate one that says less.

### End Image
He stands outside a place where Alice would normally find him, unseen by her and dissatisfied with the distance he chose.

### Beat Breakdown
1. Kaito avoids home without admitting he is avoiding Alice.
2. Mel offers care; Kaito redirects it into a question about what the group should do next.
3. Kaito attempts to use State on a damaged object during training and fails to hold the configuration clearly.
4. He blames fatigue, the object, the timing, or Ku's instruction rather than the refusal to look honestly.
5. Ku sees the pattern but does not force confession.
6. Kaito nearly returns home, then chooses one more night away.

### Character Threads
- **Kaito:** His Inadequacy treats Alice's secrecy as proof that even love edits him.
- **Mel:** Learns that trying harder to care can become another form of pressure.
- **Ku:** Recognises containment and allows Kaito the dignity of choosing when to speak.

### Must-Include
- Kaito's resentment should not be punished by the narrative.
- His ability failure must be specific and visual, not a generic “emotion nerf.”

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** Divergence focus rule; Alice reveal.
- **Sets up:** Mel's care landing inward, concrete Daiki evidence, Kaito's eventual honest perception.

### Notes
Canon status: WORKING. Production status: NEEDS PROSE.

## 4. Evidence of Daiki's Danger

**Arc:** Arc 4 — Inheritance
**POV / Emotional Center:** Kaito
**Pressure Movement:** Kaito wants evidence that Alice was wrong → evidence proves she was afraid for a reason → certainty becomes more complicated, not comforting
**Power-System Beat:** All Seeing Eye is witnessed or its consequences are discovered; the exact method remains open
**Visual Motif:** Reflected eyes / tinted glass / someone observed without consent
**Emotional Thesis:** Understanding why someone protected you does not erase the cost of being kept in the dark.

### Start Image
Kaito finds a record, witness, or scene that appears to support the simplest version of his anger: Alice hid Daiki because she wanted control.

### End Image
Kaito sees the gap between love and control in Daiki and realises the two can coexist.

### Beat Breakdown
1. Kaito pursues a lead without telling the group, believing information will make the choice clean.
2. The lead reveals Daiki's continued proximity to the Tidal Plate or Tenebri gravity.
3. All Seeing Eye's condition and horror are shown through consequence, not a technical lecture.
4. Kaito recognises the family resemblance: Daiki watches and controls from distance; Kaito edits and controls himself from inside.
5. The evidence does not absolve Alice. It removes Kaito's easiest explanation of her motives.
6. Kaito returns with a more difficult question instead of a clean verdict.

### Character Threads
- **Kaito:** Sees inheritance as a pattern of control he can reject rather than a destiny he must reproduce.
- **Daiki:** Remains partially unreadable; love does not make him safe.
- **Alice:** Her fear becomes understandable without becoming automatically correct.

### Must-Include
- The exact evidence is **OPEN**.
- Do not decide whether Kaito directly meets Daiki in this card.
- Do not resolve Daiki's morality through a single heroic act.

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** Alice's reveal; All Seeing Eye; Kaito's eye motif.
- **Sets up:** Kaito's inheritance decision and later naming/claiming arc.

### Notes
Canon status: OPEN for exact evidence and contact timing. Production status: NEEDS PROSE after the evidence decision is chosen.

## 5. Care That Lands

**Arc:** Arc 4 — Inheritance
**POV / Emotional Center:** Kaito and Mel
**Pressure Movement:** Kaito tries to convert care into logistics → Mel refuses to over-function → Kaito accepts a small, imperfect act of care
**Power-System Beat:** Flare protects Kaito only after he willingly accepts her Anchor; exact Anchor object remains WORKING
**Visual Motif:** A care-kit object placed between them, then held by Kaito
**Emotional Thesis:** Care becomes real when it is allowed to be received without becoming a debt or a repair project.

### Start Image
Mel prepares three possible solutions and hides two before Kaito can see them.

### End Image
Kaito holds an Anchor Mel offers without immediately asking what he needs to do in return.

### Beat Breakdown
1. Mel approaches without asking for the entire truth.
2. Kaito offers a practical task in exchange for her presence.
3. Mel names the exchange gently: he is trying to make being cared for useful.
4. Kaito deflects; Flare remains available but does not activate fully.
5. A small danger forces Kaito to choose whether to accept an Anchor.
6. He accepts without promising to be better or thanking her enough to close the moment.
7. Flare stabilises him imperfectly; the imperfection is proof the care is real rather than control.

### Character Threads
- **Kaito:** Accepts care without solving the person offering it.
- **Mel:** Includes herself in the Flare network instead of disappearing into service.

### Must-Include
- The scene is not a confession or romantic payoff yet.
- Flare cannot force acceptance.

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** Arc 2 adjacent warmth; Mel's Anchor logic; Kaito's care deflection.
- **Sets up:** Romance progression and Mel's later self-inclusion breakthrough.

## 6. The Inheritance Decision

**Arc:** Arc 4 — Inheritance
**POV / Emotional Center:** Kaito
**Pressure Movement:** Knowledge creates competing obligations → Kaito names what he wants without pretending fear is gone → action chosen from informed agency
**Power-System Beat:** Kaito uses Divergence without prematurely naming it in-story; focus is steadier because he acknowledges what he sees before correcting it
**Visual Motif:** Two eyes looking at the same object without one correcting the other
**Emotional Thesis:** Growing up is not choosing the cleanest version of your inheritance; it is choosing what you will do with the truth of it.

### Start Image
Kaito stands before two routes: one toward Daiki's trace and one toward the group's immediate safety.

### End Image
He chooses a route and tells the group why, including the part he does not know.

### Beat Breakdown
1. The group presents competing practical concerns.
2. Kaito begins to make the choice as a performance of certainty.
3. He stops and names the uncertainty instead.
4. His right-eye focus holds on the actual object/problem rather than the version he wishes were present.
5. He chooses a next action that is neither blind pursuit nor passive avoidance.
6. Alice's truth remains painful; it is no longer hidden.

### Character Threads
- **Kaito:** First meaningful movement toward the true Code: honest perception before correction.
- **Group:** Begins treating Kaito as someone who can make informed choices, not someone who needs to be protected from every truth.

### Must-Include
- This is not Kaito's Divergence naming scene. That remains OPEN for later.
- The decision must create a new objective for the next arc or movement.

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** Alice reveal, Daiki evidence, Kaito/Mel care, right-eye focus.
- **Sets up:** Inheritance pursuit and eventual naming/consequence arc.

### Notes
Canon status: WORKING. Production status: NEEDS PROSE.
