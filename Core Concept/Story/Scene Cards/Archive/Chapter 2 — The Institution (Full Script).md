# Chapter 2 — "The Institution" (Full Script)

**Format:** Manga script — panel-by-panel with dialogue and visual direction.
**Pages (approx.):** 50
**POV:** Ku, Kaito, Mel, and ensemble
**Setting:** East Tidal Plate — community centre, dock district, Kaito's home, and the lake

**Continuity rule:** Chapter 1 ends with Aiden and Mel awakened, Kaito still Kyumin, and Hitori still unawakened. Chapter 2 begins with the K.E.F. response. The group escapes to temporary shelter, then Kaito and Mel's rooftop/lake sequence occurs. Kaito's first partial, misdirected Awakening happens during the rooftop crisis. Only after the rooftop scene does Ku perform the Black Box Test on Aiden, Mel, and Kaito. Hitori is never tested.

---

## SEQUENCE A — THE K.E.F. RUN (Pages 1–16)

**Sequence purpose:** The institution arrives before the group has language for what happened. Procedure becomes the first antagonist: not because every person present is monstrous, but because the system has learned to turn loss into a manageable form.

**Panel 1 (Full page)**
Before dawn. Headlights cut through hard grey fog on the main road to the community centre. K.E.F. transports arrive in a clean, disciplined formation. Their insignia means nothing to the Tidal Plate and everything to Ku.

The response has arrived before the town has finished waking.

## SEQUENCE B — THE ROOFTOP AND THE LAKE (Pages 17–36)

**Sequence purpose:** This is the emotional centre of the chapter. Kaito's first Awakening is a consequence of his decision to erase visible weakness. The lake scene then gives him something his power cannot provide: care that does not demand a finished version of him.

**Production rule:** The rooftop crisis is not a Black Box Test. Kaito does not yet know the words *Weaver*, *State*, or *Awakening*. The power activates before anyone can classify it.

---

### Page 17

**Panel 1 (Full page)**
Kaito sits on the slanted tiles of his roof, staring into empty darkness. No fog. No stars. Only the faint outline of the ocean beyond the fence.

**Panel 2 (Inset)**
His bedroom window behind him, open. The house below is silent.

**Panel 3**
Kaito's face. Blank on the surface. His thoughts move too quickly to become words.

---

### Page 18

**Panel 1**
Fragments of the day without borders: Alice's mouth opening and closing; the dock worker looking at his eyes; Ku looking at his empty hands; the black residue where Yoshi disappeared.

**Panel 2**
Imagined versions overlap. Kaito supplies better answers for himself, each arriving too late.

**KAITO (thought):**
*I should have said—*

**Panel 3**
His fingernails press into his arms beneath his sleeves. Hard enough to hurt. Hard enough to become a different thought.

---

### Page 19

**Panel 1 (Close)**
Blood begins to stain beneath Kaito's sleeve. Keep the image restrained; the consequence is clear without making the injury graphic.

**Panel 2**
Kaito notices. His hands stop.

**Panel 3**
He looks at the marks as if his own body is another problem in front of him.

**KAITO (thought):**
*No one can see this.*

---

### Page 20

**Panel 1**
His right eye catches the moonlight. The blue is suddenly too deep, almost black at the centre.

**Panel 2**
The eye focuses.

A thin line of blue Rei moves from the iris through the skin of his face and down his arm. It is not a flare. It is a correction trying to happen.

**Panel 3**
The marks on his arms fade toward the uninjured state — not healing in a clean flash, but being pulled backward through the body's remembered configuration.

The pain remains.

**Panel 4**
Kaito stares at his skin.

**KAITO:**
...what?

---

### Page 21

**Panel 1**
The blue line cuts out. Kaito's vision doubles. He grips the roof tiles.

**Panel 2**
A small thread of blood runs from the corner of his right eye. He wipes it away before he can examine it.

**Panel 3**
He lies back against the tiles, exhausted and afraid of the question he cannot ask.

**Production note:** This is Kaito's first Awakening: a partial, misdirected right-eye State expression. It answers his demand to erase visible evidence of weakness. He does not understand that a power activated, and he has not named the ability.

**Panel 2**

### Page 23

**Panel 1 (Full page — dream)**
A living room. Warm light. Daiki Amori in a suit and tie, the tie loosened, his jacket over a chair. He smiles as he takes care of Alice, young Kaito, and his little brother.

Daiki kisses Alice's forehead. Alice laughs genuinely.

This is the family Kaito remembers wanting, whether or not it ever existed exactly this way.

Daiki does not look back.

**Panel 2**
The door closes.

**Panel 3 (Full page)**
Kaito jolts awake. Betrayal hits his chest with the force of something physical. He slides down the roof, lands heavily on the pavement, and starts sprinting.

He does not know where he is going. He only knows he has to get away from the house, the dream, and the version of himself still reaching after someone who has already left.

Mel holds him. She does not turn his grief into a task.

**Panel 3**

## SEQUENCE C — THE BLACK BOX TESTS (Pages 37–48)

**Sequence purpose:** The Black Box is a diagnostic after Awakening, not the source of it. Aiden and Mel receive vocabulary for what they did. Kaito receives a troubling name for what happened on the roof. Hitori is deliberately never tested.

---

### Page 37

**Panel 1 (Wide)**
Inside the maintenance shed. Morning light leaks through the cracks. The group has reconvened. Aiden is awake. Hitori has returned carrying water and supplies.

**Panel 2**
Kaito and Mel enter together. They do not announce where they have been.

**Panel 3**
Aiden looks at them.

**AIDEN:**
You two look terrible.

**MEL:**
Good morning to you too.

**Panel 4**
Ku studies Kaito's sleeve and then his eyes.

**KU:**
You awakened.

Kaito stops.

---

### Page 38

**Panel 1**
Kaito looks at Ku.

**KAITO:**
I don't know what I did.

**KU:**
Then we find out what direction it took.

**Panel 2**
Ku draws a square boundary in the dust.

**KU:**
This is the Black Box Test. It identifies what kind of awakened you are and gives us a baseline for control. It cannot tell you why you woke up. It cannot undo what happened.

**Panel 3**
Kaito watches the empty square.

**Panel 4**
Ku looks at Sora.

**KU:**
Show them.

---

### Page 39

**Panel 1**
Sora kneels and places her hand inside the square. Darkness rises along the lines, forming a stable cube.

**Panel 2**
The cube hums just below hearing. Sora's hand remains visible.

**KU:**
The Black Box reads the direction your Rei took when you first resolved Pressure.

**Panel 3**
Kaito's face appears in broken reflections across the black surface.

**KU:**
It tells us what you are beginning as. Not what you have to remain.

**Panel 4**
Sora withdraws her hand. The cube fades.

---

### Page 40

**Panel 1**
Aiden steps forward. His humor returns, controlled now — a choice, not a reflex.

**AIDEN:**
Let's find out whether the inside of my head is as impressive as the outside.

**Panel 2**
His hand enters the cube. Gold flashes. Shackles appear, interlocking around the space where attention would settle.

**Panel 3**
The result resolves: **AKARUI**.

**KU:**
You externalise. The constructs you made at the centre are Forge. You pulled the Kukai's attention onto yourself and held it there.

**Panel 4**
Aiden looks at the gold around his fingers.

**AIDEN:**
So being impossible to ignore is useful sometimes.

**KU:**
When you choose who you are asking to look at you.

Aiden absorbs it without turning it into a joke.

---

### Page 41

**Panel 1**
Mel kneels at the square. Her bracelet is dark but warm.

**Panel 2**
She places her hand inside the cube. The darkness drifts away from her palm and gathers around the bracelet.

**Panel 3**
The woven pattern appears. The result resolves: **REIKI**.

**KU:**
You displace Pressure into objects. The bracelet is your Anchor. It responded when you reached for protection without stopping to decide whether you were allowed to need it.

**Panel 4**
Mel touches the bracelet.

**MEL:**
I wasn't thinking.

**KU:**
Reiki reaches before it explains.

---

### Page 42

**Panel 1**
Hitori sits at the edge of the room. He watches the cube disappear.

**Panel 2**
Ku does not offer him the square. He turns toward Kaito.

**Panel 3**
Hitori notices. His expression barely changes.

No one calls attention to the omission. The empty space is the point.

**Panel 4**
Kaito looks at Hitori, then back to the square. He understands that something happened, but not what it means.

### Page 43

**Panel 1**
Kaito steps toward the boundary. His right hand remains over his left sleeve.

**KU:**

**KAITO:**

**MEL:**
Kaito—

**KAITO:**

**KU:**

**Panel 4**

### Page 44

**Panel 1**
The Black Box rises. It does not flare like Aiden's or drift toward an object like Mel's. It contracts around Kaito's hand and draws inward toward the blue eye.

**Panel 2**
Thin lines divide the darkness into overlapping configurations: before, during, after. None stay aligned.

**Panel 3**
The cube shows the rooftop: blood beneath Kaito's sleeve, the focused eye, skin returning toward an earlier state. The image is a reading, not a memory.

**Panel 4**

No one says the word immediately.

### Page 45

**Panel 1**
Ku looks at the result, then at Kaito. Recognition reaches his face before he can contain it.

**KU:**
You turned the pressure inward.

**Panel 2**
Kaito pulls his hand back. The cube remains for one extra second, stuttering around the empty space.

**KAITO:**
What did I do?

**Panel 3**

**KU:**
Your right eye changed the state of something it could see. At first, that means yourself. You focused on the injury and forced your Rei toward the version of you before it happened.

**Panel 4**
Kaito looks down at his sleeve.

**KAITO:**
So I fixed it.

**KU:**
No. You corrected the evidence.

---

### Page 46

The line lands harder than an accusation.

**Panel 2**
Mel steps closer without touching him.

**MEL:**
Does it hurt?

**KAITO:**
It did.

**MEL:**
And now?

**KAITO:**
I don't know.

**Panel 3**

**KU:**
That is the danger. A Weaver can make the outside look finished while the Pressure remains.

**Panel 4**
Kaito's right eye twitches. He does not activate it.

### Page 47

**Panel 2**

**Panel 3**

**HITORI:**

### Page 48

**MEL:**
You don't have to fix what happened on the roof tonight.

**KAITO:**
I wasn't trying to.

**MEL:**
I know.

**Panel 2**
Kaito looks at his sleeve.

**KAITO:**
That's the problem.

**Panel 3**

## SEQUENCE D — THE MORNING AFTER (Pages 49–50)

### Page 49

**Panel 1 (Wide)**
The group leaves the shed through a service route beneath the northern docks. K.E.F. vehicles are visible through grates above them.

**Panel 2**
Ku leads. Sora follows. Hitori carries the supplies. Aiden walks with his hands in his pockets. Mel stays near Kaito without crowding him.

**Panel 3**

**Panel 4**

**AIDEN:**

**KAITO:**
You don't know what it means.

**AIDEN:**
Neither do you. That's never stopped me before.

He lets the sentence stand without dressing it up.

### Page 50 — Full-Page Final Image

The six figures on a northern maintenance platform, small against the Tidal Plate and the sea.

Aiden's hands carry the faint geometry of gold.

Mel's bracelet rests against her wrist, warm but dark.

Kaito's blue eye is open, no longer dormant, but not yet understood.

Hitori stands among them without a glow, without a test, without being less present.

Ku and Sora face the road ahead — the direction of the K.E.F., the Crown, and the truth neither of them has finished telling.

**FINAL CAPTION:**
*The test had given them names.*
*It had not told them what the names would cost.*

## Production Notes

- **Total pages:** 50
- **Order:** K.E.F. run → rooftop/lake scene → Kaito's first Awakening → Black Box Tests → morning after.
- **Kaito's first Awakening:** Partial and misdirected right-eye State expression. He returns visible injury on his own arm toward an earlier state, but the pain and Pressure remain. The ability is unnamed in-story.
- **Aiden:** Akarui / Forge confirmed after his first resolution; humor remains available but does not erase seriousness.
- **Mel:** Reiki / Flare Anchor confirmed after her first resolution; her care lands through presence rather than fixing Kaito.
- **Hitori:** Deliberately omitted from the Black Box Test; no "no result" scene.
- **Ku/Sora:** Their former K.E.F. affiliation is revealed through procedure, not an exposition dump.
- **Visual motifs:** roof tiles and state-lines; lake reflection; K.E.F. clipboard; Black Box cube; unfinished square; Hitori crossing the line.
- **Open questions carried forward:** What exactly did Ku and Sora do? What is the K.E.F. order? What does Kaito's right eye become when he stops using it to erase evidence? Why was Hitori never offered the test? What happened to Yoshi?
