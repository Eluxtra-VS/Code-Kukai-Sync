# Arc 1 — The Plate

These cards are the first scripting layer beneath [[Arc Treatment (In-Depth Draft)]]. They are deliberately modular: without rewriting the whole arc. Each card must keep the locked canon while leaving dialogue and exact page count flexible.

## Status Key

- **LOCKED:** canon or structural requirement.
- **WORKING:** Not the final decision.
- **OPEN:** Can be changed.

---

## 1. Opening Dock Morning

**POV / Emotional Center:** Kaito Amori
**Power-System Recap:** Kaito is still Kyumin; sensitivity is shown as perception, not an ability reveal
**Visual Motif:** Two eyes / dock ropes / ceiling lines echoed later by Thread imagery
**Emotional Message:** A person can be genuinely attentive to everyone else while treating their own unfinishedness as a defect.

### Start Image
Fog lifting off the Tidal Plate docks. Kaito's blue right eye catches the first light before his face is fully visible.

### End Image
Kaito lies awake beneath the ceiling, replaying a harmless conversation and replacing his own words with better ones.

### Breakdown
1. Kaito helps Alice willingly with a dock task and solves three practical problems before she asks.
2. A dock worker notices his eyes, looks away, then looks again; Kaito notices both looks.
3. Alice offers an ordinary correction without judgment. Kaito accepts it too quickly and turns the moment into proof that he was careless.
4. Kaito performs ease around the friend group, especially when Mel notices he has not eaten.
5. At night, the room becomes a revision chamber: the day's dialogue returns as fragments, and Kaito silently edits himself.

### Character Threads
- **Kaito:** Establishes the difference between genuine kindness and self-directed contempt.
- **Alice:** Shows practical love and the ordinary stability she built to keep Kaito outside the hidden world.
- **Mel:** Her first offer of care lands near Kaito but not inside him.

### Must-Include
- Kaito's right eye is noticed without melodramatic explanation.
- Alice says something ordinary that Kaito interprets as a personal correction.
- The first insomnia image ends the card.

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** Establishes Kaito's Inadequacy, revision motif, Alice's practical steadiness.
- **Sets up:** Divergence's conscious-focus rule, Alice's later reveal, Kaito/Mel care pattern.

### Notes
Do not explain Reiva, Tenebri, or Divergence here. The audience should feel the hidden pressure before receiving its vocabulary.

## 2. Yoshi Community Centre Introduction

**Arc:** Arc 1 — The Plate
**POV / Emotional Center:** Yoshi Kusakabe
**Pressure Movement:** Warmth through giving → a private sting when a gift is accepted politely but not emotionally → compensatory over-giving
**Power-System Beat:** Yoshi remains Kyumin; Pressure is behavioral and environmental
**Visual Motif:** Hands passing objects from person to person; empty hands at the end
**Emotional Thesis:** Care becomes a test when the giver secretly needs the receiver to prove something about the giver.

### Start Image
Yoshi unlocks the community centre before anyone else arrives and turns on lights in rooms that do not need them yet.

### End Image
After closing, Yoshi stands in the centre holding a gift meant for his stepson, with no one there to receive it.

### Breakdown
1. Yoshi remembers names, preferences, and small needs; the centre feels warmer because he is there.
2. He gives Kaito a useful tool his stepson no longer uses, making the exchange feel casual.
3. Kaito thanks him sincerely. Yoshi accepts the thanks but searches Kaito's face for something larger.
4. The stepson appears at a distance, declines a small invitation without cruelty, and leaves.
5. Yoshi immediately creates another task for himself: more food, more gifts, more reasons to be needed.
6. When the centre closes, the giving stops but the need remains.

### Character Threads
- **Kaito:** Receives kindness and unknowingly carries a piece of Yoshi's unspoken need.
- **Yoshi:** Moves from genuine care into compensatory behavior without becoming manipulative in a simplistic way.
- **Hitori:** Accepts a book later, giving Yoshi a moment of real warmth that still cannot cure the wound.

### Must-Include
- The stepson's distance must read as complicated, not cruel.
- Yoshi's kindness must be real.
- The scene must show the need without naming “Pressure.”

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** Tidal Plate culture and the Code misread through ordinary behavior.
- **Sets up:** Yoshi's quiet transformation, Kaito's wrong lesson, the gifts remaining after the incident.

### Notes
This card is a sympathy investment. Do not rush to the transformation; the audience needs to like the person before grieving what happens to him.

## 3. Yoshi Alone After Closing

**Arc:** Arc 1 — The Plate
**POV / Emotional Center:** Yoshi Kusakabe
**Pressure Movement:** Rising → nearly names his need → suppresses it and turns it into labor
**Power-System Beat:** Reiva presence may be suggested through environmental sensitivity, but no visible Awakening
**Visual Motif:** Lights switched on in empty rooms; a gift placed on a chair
**Emotional Thesis:** Suppression can look like competence right until the moment it has nowhere left to go.

### Start Image
The community centre after closing: chairs stacked, lights humming, Yoshi wiping a clean table.

### End Image
A chair holds the gift for his stepson. Yoshi turns off every light except the one above it.

### Breakdown
1. Yoshi checks the centre twice even though everything is already in order.
2. He records a message to his stepson, deletes it, records a more casual one, deletes that too.
3. He says aloud that it is fine; the empty room gives him no confirmation.
4. He notices a child left a drawing behind and places it carefully in the lost-and-found instead of taking it home.
5. He almost calls someone and asks whether he is doing enough.
6. He turns the question into cleaning, then into preparation, then into silence.

### Character Threads
- **Yoshi:** Establishes the exact thing he cannot say before the threshold.
- **Kaito:** May glimpse the lit centre from outside, planting an image he later remembers.

### Must-Include
- Yoshi must almost speak the need plainly, then stop himself.
- No supernatural spectacle is required.

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** Community-centre warmth and rejected gesture.
- **Sets up:** Quiet transformation and Kaito's misread conclusion.

### Notes
The absence of a trigger is the point. Pressure crosses through accumulation, not theatrical permission.

## 4. Yoshi Incident / Two Awakenings (cluster — Kaito deferred to Chapter 2)

**Arc:** Arc 1 — The Plate
**POV / Emotional Center:** Ensemble, with Kaito as the emotional hinge
**Pressure Movement:** Yoshi critical → group panic → Aiden and Mel find distinct first resolutions; Kaito remains powerless through the incident and carries the unresolved pressure into Chapter 2; Hitori remains unresolved and untested
**Power-System Beat:** Yoshi becomes Kukai; Aiden awakens Akarui through outward attention; Mel awakens Reiki through displacement into the bracelet; Kaito remains Kyumin until the Chapter 2 rooftop crisis, where he awakens Weaver through inward correction
**Visual Motif:** Hands reaching / objects left behind / the centre's open doorway
**Emotional Thesis:** The same crisis reveals different people by the direction their first instinct takes.

### Start Image
The community centre is full of ordinary noise. Yoshi is present but not quite participating.

### End Image
Ku and Sora stand in the wreckage while the original four stare at the place where Yoshi was.

### Beat Breakdown
1. A small community interaction repeats the earlier pattern: Yoshi gives, waits, and receives politeness instead of the proof he needs.
2. His body begins to continue past human limits; the transformation is slow, wrong, and disorienting.
3. The Kukai reaches toward the group's Pressure rather than attacking with clear intent.
4. Aiden makes himself impossible to ignore; his outward pressure pulls the Kukai away from the others.
5. Mel reaches for the care-kit and an object responds; she displaces the need to protect into what she carries.
6. Kaito sees the consequence but cannot resolve his own pressure during the incident; he runs, carries Mel with him, and leaves with empty hands.
7. Ku and Sora intervene and force the Kukai to flee rather than kill it.
8. After the K.E.F. response begins, Kaito's accumulated pressure reaches the rooftop crisis; he forms the wrong lesson and his first State expression is partial or misdirected.

### Character Threads
- **Kaito:** Does not awaken during the Yoshi incident; the incident becomes the catalyst for his later harmful interpretation of the Code on the rooftop.
- **Mel:** Her first power action is care routed into an Anchor.
- **Aiden:** His recognition hunger becomes protective by accident.
- **Hitori:** He remains present and unawakened; do not test him or create a “no result” beat.
- **Ku / Sora:** Their competence arrives before explanation and creates the group's new orbit.

### Must-Include
- No Black Box Test occurs during the incident.
- Hitori does not awaken.
- Kaito's Awakening must feel like a cost, not a clean victory.
- The Kukai is not simply a monster; it is a person whose Pressure has lost its human outlet.

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** Yoshi's private collapse, Aiden's performance, Mel's care-kit, Kaito's self-audit.
- **Sets up:** K.E.F. response, Black Box tests, Ku/Sora relationship to Kukai, the central Code thesis.

### Notes
Aiden and Mel's two incident awakenings should be visually distinct but not explained through a lecture. Kaito's deferred rooftop Awakening is a separate Chapter 2 turn. The audience understands Type through choice before terminology arrives.

**Production rule:** This cluster is not a single script scene. Split it into the following atomic cards before prose:

### 4A. Yoshi Crosses the Threshold

- **Emotional center:** Yoshi
- **Pressure turn:** Unspoken need becomes irreversible collapse.
- **Power beat:** Kyumin → Kukai; transformation is quiet, disorienting, and drawn toward Pressure.
- **End image:** The community centre remains recognizable while the person inside it does not.

### 4B. Aiden Draws the Kukai Away

- **Emotional center:** Aiden
- **Pressure turn:** Performance becomes protective action before he can call it courage.
- **Power beat:** Akarui first resolution; outward attention pulls danger away from the group.
- **End image:** Aiden stands between the Kukai and the others, still speaking loudly because silence would expose the fear.

### 4C. Mel's Displacement Awakening

- **Emotional center:** Mel
- **Pressure turn:** Care cannot reach everyone from her own body, so she routes it into what she carries.
- **Power beat:** Reiki first response through the care-kit/Anchor.
- **End image:** The object keeps protecting after Mel's hands leave it.

### 4D. Ku and Sora Intervene

- **Emotional center:** Ku / Sora
- **Pressure turn:** They choose containment without erasure, revealing their ethics before their history.
- **Power beat:** Experienced awakened intervention; Kukai is driven away, not killed.
- **End image:** Two strangers stand in the wreckage while the original four stare at the space Yoshi occupied.

### 4E. Kaito's Deferred Misdirected Awakening (Chapter 2)

- **Placement:** Chapter 2, after the K.E.F. run begins; before the Black Box Tests.
- **Emotional center:** Kaito
- **Pressure turn:** He sees what Yoshi became, reaches a private crisis, and chooses self-correction instead of honest need.
- **Power beat:** Weaver first response; right-eye State expression is partial or misdirected and remains unnamed in-story.
- **End image:** Kaito's visible injury is returned toward an earlier state, but the pain and Pressure remain.

## 5. K.E.F. Arrival / The Run

**Arc:** Arc 1 — The Plate
**POV / Emotional Center:** Sora / the original four observing institutional response
**Pressure Movement:** Immediate danger → false promise of containment → recognition that the institution is containing knowledge, not only harm
**Power-System Beat:** K.E.F. treats Kukai as a target; Ku and Sora choose non-lethal intervention and become liabilities
**Visual Motif:** Clipboard lines / sealed doors / the group framed behind infrastructure
**Emotional Thesis:** A system can call itself protection while making people disappear from the record.

### Start Image
K.E.F. vehicles arrive before the smoke has cleared; their formation is more orderly than the community centre ever was.

### End Image
The group hides behind dock infrastructure while a K.E.F. operative labels the site as an incident, not a loss.

### Beat Breakdown
1. Ku knows the response time and moves before he explains how.
2. Sora checks every person, then apologises for the ones she cannot stabilize.
3. K.E.F. agents arrive with containment tools and no questions for civilians.
4. The group sees how quickly Yoshi becomes a classification.
5. Ku chooses a route that protects the group but exposes his own former familiarity with K.E.F. procedure.
6. Sora notices he knows too much and chooses not to ask yet.

### Character Threads
- **Kaito:** Begins to treat hidden knowledge as danger rather than mystery.
- **Sora:** Establishes responsibility reflex and practical competence.
- **Ku:** His silence protects the group for one scene and plants future liability.

### Must-Include
- The K.E.F. must not be cartoonishly evil; procedure is the horror.
- No public explanation of Kuro yet.

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** Ku/Sora's intervention and K.E.F. institutional role.
- **Sets up:** Pursuit, Ku's history, Sora's order, Crown neglect.

### Notes
Keep the action short. The emotional impact is the institution's refusal to call the event what it was.

## 6. Black Box Demonstration / Omission (after Kaito's rooftop Awakening)

**Arc:** Arc 1 — The Plate
**POV / Emotional Center:** Ku Mochizuki
**Pressure Movement:** Controlled instruction → memory intrusion → renewed containment
**Power-System Beat:** Ku demonstrates the Black Box Test with Sora; Aiden and Mel are confirmed, Kaito is classified after his rooftop Awakening, and Hitori is deliberately not tested
**Visual Motif:** A cube in darkness / hands held still / an empty place in the sequence
**Emotional Thesis:** Turning trauma into a useful tool is not the same as healing it.

### Start Image
Ku draws a boundary in the dark and tells Sora to put her hand inside it.

### End Image
The test sequence ends with an unoccupied space where Hitori could have stood.

### Beat Breakdown
1. Ku demonstrates the test on Sora; she trusts him without demanding the origin of the name.
2. Aiden is tested; he performs confidence, and Ku watches the performance rather than correcting it.
3. Mel is tested; the cube responds toward her care-kit/Anchor.
4. Kaito is tested; the result confirms Weaver, and Ku's pause betrays recognition.
5. Hitori watches. Ku does not offer the test.
6. Hitori does not ask. The omission becomes the scene's unsaid event.
7. On the walk home, Yoshi's gifts remain with the group; Hitori carries his quietly.

### Character Threads
- **Kaito:** Recognises Ku's pause but cannot interpret it.
- **Ku:** Uses a former instrument of harm as a diagnostic while repeating his old habit of withholding.
- **Hitori:** Is not excluded by incompetence; the omission has a hidden reason that must remain undisclosed.

### Must-Include
- Hitori is never tested.
- No “no result” scene.
- The emotional climax is the omission, not a reveal.

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** Black Box trauma, new Paths, K.E.F. pursuit.
- **Sets up:** Ku/Kaito mirror, Hitori's long Pressure arc, Kashi's opposite conclusion.

### Notes
The scene should be quiet enough that a first-time reader may only register the omission subconsciously. On reread, it should hurt immediately.
