# Arc 2 — The Knot and the Announcement

Two scene cards plus the backstory canon that connects [[Aiden Takeda]] and [[Aoki Sora]]. Backstory direction is user-confirmed (2026-08-29). Cards are in the same format as [[Arcs 2–3 — Pursuit and Tenebri]]

**Order in the arc:** recognition card slots after card 3 ("What Sora Fixes"); the announcement slots after card 5 ("Ku-Clown Observation") and before card 6 ("K.E.F. Contact"). This placement means the unspoken confession is already hanging over the group when Sora is captured in card 8, and nobody has told her.

## Backstory Canon: The Netmenders' Tier (user-confirmed 2026-08-29)

- Roughly nine years before story start, a Kukai rose in a lower dockside residential tier of the [[Tidal Plate]] called the Netmenders' Tier, during a night tide. The Crown's response was slow, then institutional: survivors processed, and unaccompanied minors relocated inland "for care."
- Aiden (6) and Sora (8) were neighbor kids in the tier. They were not close friends in any formal sense. She was the pier kid who fixed things: crab lines, net rigs, kite lines. He was the kid whose kite line kept slipping.
- **Sora's mother** was a netmender. She taught Sora to count under her breath while tying ("one-and, two-and") and to finish every knot with a double loop "so it holds when the tide pulls." The mother is unaccounted for after the incident. The Plate's records were never corrected. This is ordinary Crown neglect, not a mystery to solve.
- **The kite line.** The evening before the evacuation, Sora re-tied Aiden's kite line and told him: *"Hold it like this and it won't slip."* The vans came before morning. She was eight, processed overnight, and gone without a goodbye. Aiden was told she "went inland." To a six-year-old that meant gone.
- **The rhyme.** Plate children in the dock tiers chant: *"Tide don't knock, tide don't call, the sea just takes, and that is all."* Adults add the closing line that does the damage: *"so hold your weeping till it's flat."* It is the misread Code in nursery form: grieve in private, endure in silence. The old netmenders' version carried a verse the institutional textbooks dropped — *"and weep where hands can hold you up"* — the true Code, nearly lost. (Registered plant; see below.)
- **After:** Sora passed through Crown institutional care, was aptitude-screened along with other displaced minors, and entered the K.E.F. pipeline young. The institution that processed her disaster became the institution she needed to believe in. [[Ku Mochizuki]] later recruited her onto operative work. Her childhood silence habit hardened into the adult version: she fixes things before anyone notices they broke.
- **Aiden stayed.** His family kept their home; [[Mai Takeda]] was already moving in older circles. Aiden kept the knot. He still says *"hold it like this and it won't slip"* to people when he fixes things, a phrase he does not know he kept. Sora has heard him say it since joining the group. It is one of the ways she has been sure who he is since the day they met.

### Recognition asymmetry (canon)

- **Sora recognized Aiden** the moment the group met (Chapters 2–3). He grew up loud, but the kite-line kid is in his hands. She said nothing. Her reasoning is her Pressure: the girl who left without saying goodbye has no claim on him, and *I ruin everything I touch* will not let her touch the one clean memory she has of herself.
- **Aiden did not recognize Sora.** She is the one who changed: trained posture, unperforming quiet, an adult's stillness where a pier kid's bounce was. He had no face to match.
- **The recognition beat** (card A below) runs through hands, rhythm, and the rhyme, not faces. Recontextualizes "Watched / Unwatched Training": when Sora observed Aiden's unperformed constructs and left before he could convert the moment into an audience, she was protecting both of their privacies at once.

### Early-reader seeds (scripting requirement, logged 2026-08-29)

Per questionnaire answer 9, the reader knows Sora recognized Aiden from early. When Chapters 2–3 are scripted or revised, these seeds must be present, reader-facing only, never cast-facing:

1. **The lingering repair:** at least one gear-stop beat where Sora's hands stay on Aiden's harness straps a beat too long after the repair is done. No dialogue.
2. **The phrase overheard:** Aiden says "hold it like this and it won't slip" to someone else while fixing gear. One panel on Sora: stillness nobody clocks. Reader-visible, cast-invisible.
3. **The unasked fix:** she repairs something of his before it breaks and takes no credit; the narration does not explain it.

These three carry the dramatic irony that "What the Knot Knew" pays off. They must exist on the page before card A plays.

## Card A — What the Knot Knew

**Arc:** Arc 2 — The Pursuit (after card 3, "What Sora Fixes")
**POV / Emotional Center:** Aiden, with Sora observed
**Pressure Movement:** Ordinary watching → a childhood pattern ambushes him → he tests it and is answered → both retreat into work
**Power-System Beat:** None on purpose. This scene is deliberately power-silent; the tell is manual, not Rei.
**Visual Motif:** Hands / a strap redone properly / a rhythm counted under breath
**Emotional Thesis:** You can know someone by their hands long before you are ready to know it in your chest.

### Start Image
Sora, next morning, quietly unpicks her own over-tight repair from the day before because she noticed it herself.

### End Image
Aiden re-lacing his own strap the way she taught him at six, having said nothing either. Two people keeping the same secret for opposite reasons.

### Beat Breakdown
1. Camp stop, gear check. Sora redoes the too-rigid strap repair she is not satisfied with.
2. She counts under her breath and finishes the knot with a double loop.
3. Aiden's hands stop. The pier memory arrives whole: a girl, a kite line, a tide-pull knot.
4. He tests it the only way a proud person will: he says the rhyme like a joke, first lines only.
5. She completes the closing line before she can stop herself. *"So hold your weeping till it's flat."*
6. Silence does the rest. She deflects into work. He lets her.
7. Neither names it. He knows she knows he knows. She knows the rhyme gave her away.

### Character Threads
- **Aiden:** Recognition arrives through competence, the one channel his Vainglory respects. He does not chase the moment because chasing would make his feeling visible before he can stage it.
- **Sora:** The rhyme escaped her training. One involuntary sentence, and the boy from the pier has the thread.

### Must-Include
No face-recognition beat. No naming of the Netmenders' Tier in dialogue. The audience should assemble the history across scenes, not receive it.

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** "What Sora Fixes" (the strap, the too-tight stitches); Sora's hands-first design language.
- **Sets up:** "The Announcement"; the strap chain completing at dawn; the kite-line phrase payoff.

## Card B — The Announcement

> **SUPERSEDED 2026-08-29 (questionnaire answer 1):** the camp-fire version below is retired as a study draft. The canon Announcement is the rebuilt empty-seat version — prose at [[Arc 2 — The Announcement (Scene Draft v3)]], positioned at the arc close after Card K ([[Arc 2 — The Rescue and the Ringmaster]]). Locks: group fears the worst / reader knows she's alive; core four + Ku; clean register; Ku's one extra sentence; silent Sora page; penultimate + lost-verse sting. The strap-chain beats below remain canon history (the recognition, the rhyme); the dawn strap-return is retired with this version.

**Arc:** Arc 2 — The Pursuit (after card 5, "Ku-Clown Observation"; before card 6, "K.E.F. Contact")
**POV / Emotional Center:** Aiden; the group as chorus; Sora absent by design
**Pressure Movement:** Group suspicion of Ku and Sora's patterns → Aiden defends her with observed specifics → the defense outruns him → he announces the feeling to everyone it is safe to announce it to → the group becomes the keeper of the secret
**Power-System Beat:** Aiden redoes Sora's strap repair by hand while he talks, counting her rhythm without noticing; one thumbnail-sized shackle settles the finished knot, small, clean, unperformed, because for once he is not building for the room. Kaito clocks the unwatched-quality output and files it.
**Visual Motif:** Low fire / a strap and needle / one clean knot / an empty place at the fire
**Emotional Thesis:** His first fully sincere public act is a confession performed without its true audience: he tells everyone except the person it is about, because she would apologize.

### Start Image
The group around a low fire after finding Hyoga's notes, everyone arranging themselves away from the two people nobody is accusing.

### End Image
Dawn. Sora comes off watch, finds her spare strap redone: looser, right, finished with the double loop she taught a boy on a pier nine years ago. She double-taps it once, looks at the sleeping camp, and says the only thing her Pressure allows, to nobody: *"Tide's flat."* She goes to put water on.

### Beat Breakdown
1. Post-Hyoga unease. Kaito, planning-logic, notes that if the Ku-Clowns study patterns, Ku and Sora carry patterns nobody else knows. It lands like an accusation even though it is logistics.
2. Mel starts a care-defense. Aiden cuts across it, and the register drops: he lists what Sora does before anyone asks. Straps, watches, gear, boots, second watch so Mel can sleep.
3. Mid-list the truth outruns the defense: *"because she—"* and he decides. Chin up. He tells the fire, the group, the dark where she is standing watch: he is in love with her, has been since he worked out who she was.
4. He tells them she recognized him from the start and never said, and that he will not make her say anything first. Then he asks them for the one performance his pride cannot survive: silence. "She'd apologize."
5. Ku, without looking up: *"She would."* Permission arriving disguised as a factual correction.
6. Nobody argues. Hitori adjusts the watch rotation so Aiden can take her dawn slot without it looking like following her. The care is logistical, and every person at the fire is now lying by omission.
7. Aiden finishes the strap. The shackle settles the knot, clean and small. He falls asleep mid-slouch, performing unconsciousness for no one.

### Character Threads
- **Aiden:** The Vainglory inversion. The announcement is real specifically because its intended audience is missing; his power, given no one to impress, does the truest thing it has ever done.
- **Kaito:** Recognizes both the unwatched output and the shape of editing yourself out of your own confession. Files it next to his own habits. Says nothing.
- **Mel:** Watches the strap work, recognizes care performed as repair, and guards the silence like she guards everything else.
- **Ku:** Grants the thing he would never say easily: that Aiden has understood Sora correctly.
- **Hitori:** Still the anchor in Arc 2. Fixes the logistics of someone else's feeling, as usual, without comment.
- **Sora:** Knows only the knot. Her Pressure will not let her claim it.

### Must-Include
Sora never hears the announcement, on-page or off. The group's secret and her secret must interlock without touching: everyone but her knows the feeling; only she knows the hands. No response from Sora beyond the double-tap; whether she feels the same stays open.

### Setup / Payoff
- **Pays off:** Card 2 (unwatched quality; card 2 precedes this scene in arc order), card 3 (the strap), card A (the rhyme and the knot).
- **Sets up:** *(restructured 2026-08-29)* Sora's separation at the Arc 2 close (Card K, [[Arc 2 — The Rescue and the Ringmaster]] — Sora's K.E.F. capture is cancelled canon), where the unsaid thing becomes the thing Aiden cannot unsay to himself and the Announcement is delivered to an empty seat; the kite-line phrase payoff when one of them finally says it to the other (phrase appears only as remembered speech, not present-day dialogue); the rhyme's lost verse as a late-arc true-Code payoff.

## Registered Plants (new, pending human confirmation)

1. **The Netmenders' Tier rhyme** — the misread Code in nursery form; recurring dock-tier texture. Its **lost verse** ("and weep where hands can hold you up") is a late-arc true-Code payoff candidate.
2. **The kite-line phrase** — "hold it like this and it won't slip." Sora has already heard Aiden say it to others. The payoff is one of them saying it to the other, and what it costs. NOT yet paid off: reserved, present-day use only.
3. **The double-loop knot** — Sora's mother's knot; the recognition token; now sitting on Aiden's strap where she can find it.
4. **The unaccounted netmender** — Sora's mother, unrecorded by Plate records. Ordinary Crown neglect; candidate for a quiet Arc 4+ record-hunt payoff alongside Sora's kept page, not a rescue plot.
5. **The double-tap** — Sora's two-thumb press on a knot that matters; established at the dawn coda ("the size of a promise nobody had kept and somebody just had"). Recurring tell; future scenes can echo it wordlessly.
6. **"Tide's flat"** — the rhyme's suppression line turned private signal. Sora says it to no one; candidate for a one-time inversion when someone finally says it back to her, or when she says it and means the lost verse instead.
7. **Sora's hip-clip** — Mel's line, "her harness hip-clip sticks." Minor gear token; candidate for a capture-scene detail in card 8 (someone notices the sticking clip at the worst moment).
8. **The torn kite tail** — the unfilled childhood promise ("she'd fix the tail tomorrow"). The vans came before morning. Candidate for a quiet late-arc completion beat: someone finally fixes a kite. Texture unless promoted.
