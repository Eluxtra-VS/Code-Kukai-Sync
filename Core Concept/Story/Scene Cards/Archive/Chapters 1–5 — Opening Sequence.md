# Chapters 1–5 — Opening Sequence

This document plans the **first five chapters** of Code:Kukai as a manga/animated series. It converts the existing Arc 1 scene cards into production-ready chapter breaks.

**Revised 2026-08-10:** Chapter 1 has been restructured to open with Kaito and the main cast — not the Ku/Sora cold open. The Ku/Sora parallel material (K.E.F. mission, child witness, Crown cover-up) has been moved to Chapter 3, where it lands as dramatic irony AFTER the reader has met the group. Chapter 1 now covers daily life → Yoshi discovery → awakenings → Ku/Sora intervention → Ku's truth reveal.

---

## Chapter 1 — "The Plate"

**Chapter thesis:** Before anyone understands what any of this costs, someone they liked disappears into it. The Plate is introduced as a home first, a punishment second. By the end, Aiden and Mel have awakened, Kaito has run with empty hands, and the question that will drive the story has been asked: *why is the truth of the Tidal Plate hidden, and who decided it should stay that way?*

**Pages (approx.):** 55–80 (extended first chapter; can be split into Chapter 1A/1B for weekly release)

**POV:** Primarily Kaito, with sequences from Mel, Aiden, and Hitori

**Setting:** The Tidal Plate, summer holidays, roughly one week's span

---

### Sequence 1 — The Opening (Pages 1–8)

**POV:** Omniscient narration → Kaito

**Start Image:** An aerial view of a large central road — people moving through their day, the rhythm of a working dock town. The narration begins.

**Narration (revised from user draft):**
> *"Humans are expressive creatures."*
>
> *"But in this day and age we are told to bottle all of it up. To keep it to ourselves."*
>
> *"That suppressing your emotions is a sign of power."*

Cut to kids playing in the street — laughter, shoving, the unguarded expression children are still allowed.

Then: a sudden cut. A Kukai. Head slowly tilted to the right. A zoomed-out view from below — first-person, looking up from the grass as if *you* are the one about to be attacked. The feet of the person whose perspective we're in are visible, splayed on the ground — as if they've just tripped.

The Kukai rears back to strike.

**Black.**

> *"Kaito."*
>
> *"Kaito."*
>
> *"KAITO GET UP!"*

A head being lifted from arms — first-person perspective. We see Hitori, hand extended, gesturing for Kaito to grab on.

> *"Kaito, you can't be slacking — your mom needs help at the docks. The others are already there. Hurry!"*

The background is murky — a bench in a secluded corner of the central district. To the right, a fence partially blocks a view of the ocean. Fog is lifting.

Title card, top left:

**East Tidal Plate**

Next panel: Kaito grabs Hitori's hand.

> *"Sorry. I'm coming."*

**Production note:** The Kukai in the dream should have one detail that mirrors Kaito himself — ideally the blue right eye. Not obvious on first read. On reread: the thing attacking in the nightmare was always himself. Do not explain this. Let the image do the work.

---

### Sequence 2 — The Dock Morning (Pages 9–16)

**POV:** Kaito

**Start Image:** Fog lifting off the Tidal Plate docks. A blue right eye catches the first light before the face is fully visible.

**Beat 1 — Kaito and Alice.**
Kaito helps his mother with the morning catch. She went without him this time, but he arrives to help get the boxes of fish sorted. He moves too fast — carrying more than he can, already solving problems before she names them.

Alice tries offering help:
> *"You know, if you stack them on top of each other and use both hands it's eas—"*

Kaito, before she finishes:
> *"Got it."*

Alice watches him for a beat longer than the conversation requires. Her mouth opens slightly — she almost says something — and closes. She does not say what she is thinking. The audience should feel the withheld information without knowing what it is.

**Beat 2 — The Eyes.**
A dock worker comes up to collect the boxes.
> *"Thanks, kiddo. You're strong for your a—"*

He notices Kaito's heterochromia. Pauses. Looks. Looks away. Looks again.

Kaito clocks both looks and files them without comment. His face does not change. Something behind it does.

The man coughs, excuses himself, and walks away.

**Production note:** The blue right eye is not a defect. It is the bond mark — the Reiva's presence visible since before Kaito was born. The audience should register it as unusual without being told why. The explanation comes much later.

---

### Sequence 3 — The Friend Group (Pages 17–28)

**POV:** Rotating — Kaito, Mel, Aiden, Hitori

**Start Image:** Kaito at a bench with a small table. His friends filter in naturally — not a staged gathering, but four people whose orbits overlap by habit.

**Beat 1 — Mel and Kaito.**
Mel notices Kaito has not eaten. She places food beside him without asking.

> *"Thank you. Wait... You're here giving ME food when you haven't even eaten yourself, have you?"*

> *"I actually have,"* Mel responds.

A small silence. Both slowly look away. Neither fully believes the other. Neither calls it out.

**Beat 2 — Aiden.**
Aiden launches into a story — loud, performative, filling the silence:
> *"SO BOOM — I gave him a hook to the jaw. He then got a* few *hits on me but I surely won. Right, guys?"*

Silence. Then they all snicker and giggle.

> *"Of course you did,"* Mel says.

> *"You SO won that,"* Kaito adds.

Hitori gives Aiden a look — not cruel, just knowing. The kind of look that says: *we all know the real story and we love you anyway.* Aiden clocks it and laughs a half-second too loud.

**Beat 3 — Hitori.**
Hitori gets up without announcement and starts carrying equipment toward the dock workers. Someone shouts:
> *"Hey! Kid, get me that box too!"*

Hitori picks it up and continues. No complaint. No pause. He is simply the person who carries things.

**Beat 4 — The Anchor and the Splash.**
A boat anchor drops. Everyone turns at the loud splash.

Hitori hands over equipment. His hand catches on something — a small cut. He groans.

> *"Run it over some water, it'll be fine,"* a worker says.

> *"Do you got any band-aids? It hurts like hell."*

> *"Stick to the Code, boy. You won't be handed anything in life."*

> *"Got it."*

Hitori sighs and walks back to the table. Mel puts a band-aid down without a word.

> *"Thanks a bunch."*

**The Code — visual counterpoint:** The dock worker invokes the Code to justify not helping. Mel immediately contradicts it by providing care without being asked. The panel composition should place the two actions in visual opposition — the Code spoken on the left, the band-aid placed on the right. No commentary needed.

**Beat 5 — The Hook.**
Kaito coughs.

> *"Hey guys — do you wanna go around looking for some abandoned places tomorrow? It's the summer holidays, so I think it'll be fun."*

They all agree.

**End Image (Sequence 3):** The group dispersing. Four people, ordinary, unaware that tomorrow will end the version of their lives they recognise.

---

### Sequence 4 — The Night Revision (Pages 29–32)

**POV:** Kaito

**Start Image:** Kaito's bedroom ceiling. The room is dark. His eyes are open.

He lies awake, replaying the day's conversations. The dock worker's double-take. Alice's unfinished help. The way Mel looked away after he questioned whether she'd eaten. Each memory returns as a subtitle over the ceiling — his own words rewritten into better versions. What he should have said. What he should have done.

The revision does not resolve anything. It just repeats.

**End Image:** The ceiling. The blue eye, open. Still looking at nothing.

**Production note:** This is the first appearance of the insomnia motif. It should be short — four to six pages — and visually distinct from the daylight sequences. The ceiling becomes a recurring image: the place Kaito goes when he cannot stop editing himself.

---

### Sequence 5 — The Exploration (Pages 33–42)

**POV:** Ensemble — Kaito, Mel, Aiden, Hitori

**Start Image:** The four friends walking away from the docks, toward the older, abandoned parts of the Tidal Plate. Summer light. The mood is light.

The exploration sequence is not plotted beat-by-beat here — it is an opportunity for texture and breathing room before the incident. Key requirements:

- Show the group dynamic in motion: Aiden making everything a competition, Hitori carrying things no one asked him to carry, Mel noticing small details the others miss, Kaito leading without realising he's leading.
- One beat of Kaito/Mel proximity — a moment where she almost takes his hand crossing something unstable, or he almost says something real and pulls back. Small. Unnamed. Both of them filing it away.
- The abandoned places should feel like the Plate's forgotten history — structures that suggest the island was not always what it appears to be. No exposition. Just images.
- As they head back, the light begins to change. Golden → amber → the first hint of dusk.

---

### Sequence 6 — The Shortcut (Pages 43–48)

**POV:** Hitori → Kaito

**Start Image:** The group walking back. It's approximately 7–8pm. Turning dark, but not fully dark yet — that specific hour when shadows stretch and colours shift toward grey. The mood is still post-adventure ease.

**Hitori's suggestion:**
> *"Hey. We can take the shortcut near the community centre. Cuts about fifteen minutes off."*

The group agrees. The centre is closed at this hour — Yoshi would have gone home by now.

Except he hasn't.

**The discovery:**
As they approach the community centre, they notice something wrong before they understand what they're seeing.

Yoshi is on the floor in a fetal position. Foam is coming out of his mouth — slowly turning into a dark, viscous goo. From his eyes: what looks like white tears, but too thick, too constant. He is not crying. His body is doing something his mind has no language for.

They rush toward him.

Before they reach him, Yoshi gets up. Not naturally — his body unfolding from the ground with a wrongness that registers before the conscious mind can name it. More black goo begins to drip from him. Not blood. Something else.

**The scramble:**
Aiden's eyes widen. He sees the others running and makes a decision in the split second he has:

> *"COME HERE! WHATEVER YOU ARE!"*

He turns — away from the group — making himself the loudest, most visible target.

Kaito screams:
> *"AIDEN, RUN!"*

Mel trips. Kaito grabs her hand and *sprints* while looking back. Hitori follows.

**End Image (Sequence 6):** Aiden standing his ground. The Kukai turning toward him. The distance between Aiden and the rest of the group widening. The last of the daylight draining from the sky.

---

### Sequence 7 — The Two Awakenings (Pages 49–62)

**POV:** Ensemble — Aiden and Mel resolve in the incident; Kaito remains unawakened

**Production rule:** Aiden and Mel's awakenings overlap in the Yoshi crisis. Kaito does not awaken here: he grabs Mel's hand and runs while Aiden creates the distraction. Kaito's first Awakening is deferred to the Chapter 2 rooftop crisis.

---

**7A. Aiden Draws the Kukai Away**

**POV:** Aiden
**Pressure Turn:** Performance becomes protection before he can call it courage.
**Power Beat:** Akarui first resolution — outward attention pulls danger away from the group. Shackles form around the Kukai's *attention*, not its body. The constructs are not yet clean — they flicker, half-formed — but they hold.
**Visual:** Aiden shouting, gesturing, making himself impossible to ignore. For the first time in his life, his need for attention has a use that is not performance.
**End Image:** Aiden standing between the Kukai and the others, still speaking loudly because silence would expose the fear underneath.

---

**7B. Mel's Displacement Awakening**

**POV:** Mel
**Pressure Turn:** Care cannot reach everyone from her own body. She routes it into what she carries.
**Power Beat:** Reiki first response — her hand reaches for the woven bracelet on her wrist (her Anchor). It responds before she understands why. A small, warm light. Protection she did not consciously summon.
**Visual:** The bracelet — a small, handmade thing, clearly old — activates without her direction. The Anchor keeps holding its shape after her hands leave it. She stares at her own empty palms.
**End Image:** Mel looking at her hands. The bracelet still glowing faintly. She does not understand what just happened. The audience shouldn't fully understand either — only that she reached for something and it answered.

---

**7C. Kaito Remains Unawakened**

**POV:** Kaito
**Pressure Turn:** He sees what Yoshi became but cannot turn the sight into a resolution. He grabs Mel's hand when she falls and runs, carrying the fear forward rather than solving it.
**Power Beat:** No Awakening. Kaito's right eye remains dormant; his hands are empty. The incident becomes the catalyst for his later rooftop crisis.
**Visual:** Kaito pulls Mel through the dusk while looking back at Aiden and the Kukai. He is useful in the human sense — he chooses someone and gets them clear — but he has no supernatural answer.
**End Image:** Kaito safe with Mel and Hitori, watching Aiden hold the line, ashamed that courage did not look like power in his own hands.

---

**Hitori — The Absence of Awakening**

Throughout the chaos, Hitori is present. He does not awaken. He does not produce a dramatic moment. He is simply there — steady, anchoring — while everyone around him transforms. The audience should register his stillness without understanding it yet.

---

### Sequence 8 — Intervention (Pages 63–70)

**POV:** Ku → the group

**Start Image:** Two figures move through the dusk with the specific economy of people who knew this was coming before anyone else did.

Ku and Sora arrive already in motion. They were tracking the signature before the group knew there was one. They do not explain themselves. They drive the Kukai off — not kill it. The mercy is visible even here, unexplained.

The group finds them standing in the wreckage of what used to be a community centre gathering. Two strangers, clearly capable.

Kaito looks at them and recognises something he cannot name.

**The group ends up bound to Ku and Sora less by choice than by physics.** Splitting up is more dangerous than staying together. No one asked for this.

**Small human beat:** Mel tries to take care of Ku or Sora — the way she takes care of everyone. Gets gently, firmly redirected. First hint that Sora deflects care the same way Mel does. Mirrored pattern. Nobody remarks on it yet.

**End Image (Sequence 8):** Two strangers stand in the wreckage. Four kids stare at the place where Yoshi was. The night has fully arrived.

---

### Sequence 9 — The First Explanation (Pages 71–78)

**POV:** Ku, with Kaito as the emotional anchor of the listening group

**Start Image:** Later that night. The group somewhere safe-adjacent — a dock structure, an abandoned building, somewhere Ku led them without explanation. Everyone exhausted. All of them still carrying Yoshi's gifts.

Ku talks — not a lecture, but fragments. The awakened exist. Kukai are what happens when Pressure has nowhere to go. There is an institution that "manages" both. The Tidal Plate is not ordinary.

Then Ku says something that changes everything:

> *"The truth of the Tidal Plate is being hidden. Deliberately. There are records. There are people whose job it is to make sure no one on this island finds out what it actually is."*

Silence.

Kaito is the one who asks:

> *"Why?"*

Ku does not answer immediately. He looks at Sora — a glance that carries something the group cannot interpret. Then back at Kaito.

> *"That's the question. Isn't it."*

He does not say *I don't know.* He says it in a way that means: *I know more than I'm saying, and what I'm not saying is worse.*

The goal crystallizes — not as a mission statement, but as the natural consequence of everything they have just survived:

> **Goal: Find out why the Tidal Plate's truth is hidden. And who decided it should stay that way.**

Kaito looks at the group — at Mel, Aiden, Hitori, Ku, Sora — and arrives at the thing the story has been building toward since the first page.

**End Image (Chapter 1):** Six figures at the edge of the Tidal Plate — the docks, the fog, the open water beyond. They are looking outward for the first time. Hitori is quiet the whole time. Kaito is also quiet, still carrying the shame of having no power while Aiden and Mel glow faintly beside him. No one asks why — Hitori is always quiet, and Kaito is always editing. To the audience, the difference between their silences should matter.

**Final caption — Kaito's internal voice:**
> *"I've been correcting myself my whole life. I never thought to check whether the world around me was the thing that was wrong."*

---

### Optional Last Page — Full Spread

**Full-page image:** The Tidal Plate from above. Six figures small against the dock infrastructure. Fog rolling in from the water. The Crown's towers visible in the far distance — clean, orderly, watching. The blue right eye visible on the smallest figure.

## Chapter 2 — "The Institution"

**Chapter thesis:** The institution that has been hiding the Tidal Plate's truth now has faces and procedures. The group must learn that safety and containment are not the same thing, while Kaito learns that a power capable of correcting damage cannot erase the pain that caused him to use it.

**Pages (approx.):** 50

---

### Sequence A — The K.E.F. Run (Pages 1–16)

**POV:** Ku / the group observing

**Start Image:** K.E.F. vehicles arrive before dawn. Their formation is more orderly than anything on the Tidal Plate has ever been.

1. Ku knows the response time. He moves the group before he explains how. Sora checks every person, then apologises for the ones she cannot stabilize — as if stabilization were something she owed them.
2. K.E.F. agents sweep the centre with containment tools and no questions for civilians. The group watches from a distance. They understand for the first time: whatever this world actually is, it has people whose job is to make sure no one finds out.
3. An operative labels the site as an incident. Not a loss. Not a tragedy. An incident — the kind of word that fits on a form.
4. The group ends up bound to Ku and Sora less by choice than by physics. Splitting up is more dangerous than staying together. No one asked for this.
5. **Small human beat:** Mel tries to take care of Ku or Sora. Gets gently, firmly redirected. First hint that Sora deflects care the same way Mel does. Mirrored pattern. Unremarked on.

**End Image:** The group somewhere safe-adjacent, exhausted. The K.E.F. facility visible in the distance — cold, procedural, still searching.

---

### Sequence B — The Rooftop and the Lake (Pages 17–36)

**Continuity:** Kaito's first partial, misdirected Awakening occurs here, during the rooftop crisis, before the Black Box Tests.

**POV:** Kaito → Mel

**Start Image:** Kaito sits on the slanted tiles of his roof, having slipped out through his bedroom window.

Kaito's spiral, self-harm, dream, flight, Mel's chase, and the lake scene follow. The right eye activates before the dream, returning the visible injury toward an earlier state while leaving the pain and Pressure intact. Kaito does not understand that he has Awakened and has not been classified yet.

**End Image:** Two figures small against the lake and the moonlight. Neither speaking. Both present.

---

### Sequence C — Black Box Tests (Pages 37–48)

**Continuity:** Aiden, Mel, and Kaito are tested only after Awakening. Hitori is deliberately never tested.

**POV:** Ku → Aiden → Mel → Kaito → the omission

**Start Image:** Ku draws a boundary in the dark and tells Sora to put her hand inside it.

Ku demonstrates the Black Box, then tests Aiden and Mel, confirms their paths, deliberately omits Hitori, and finally tests Kaito after the rooftop Awakening. Kaito's result confirms Weaver; Ku's pause carries recognition rather than surprise.

**Emotional end:** Kaito is seated near the door with Mel beside him, her bracelet faintly warm. He has a name for what happened, not an answer for what it will cost. The test gives him vocabulary without making him emotionally healed.

---

### Sequence D — The Morning After (Pages 49–50)

**POV:** Kaito → brief external

**Start Image:** Dawn over the Tidal Plate. Kaito walking back toward the docks. Mel beside him. Neither speaks. Something between them has shifted and neither has the vocabulary for it.

The group reconvenes. Aiden is already talking. Hitori is already carrying something. Ku and Sora are already planning the next move.

No one mentions the night. No one needs to. But the audience should register: Mel and Kaito are standing slightly closer than they were before.

**End Image (Chapter 2):** The group at the edge of the docks, the morning fog rolling in. Kaito's eyes are clear for the first time since Chapter 1. The ceiling, tonight, might not hold him awake.

---

## Chapter 3 — "The Past That Follows"

**Chapter thesis:** The people protecting the group have their own history with the institution hunting them — and that history is not finished with them yet.

**Pages (approx.):** 42–48

---

### Sequence A — Training Begins (Pages 1–12)

**POV:** Ensemble

Training starts here — not as a curriculum, but as survival. Ku teaches reactively, whatever the day's situation demands, which means the group is always slightly behind the lesson they're learning. Sora is the one who makes the training stick — translating Ku's minimalism into something the others can use.

- Aiden's Shackles flare and falter. He performs even in training, and the constructs wobble when he does.
- Mel's Anchor responds when she reaches for it, but only when she's not thinking about it consciously. The bracelet glows warm against her wrist.
- Kaito's right eye catches on things he doesn't mean to look at. The State function fires without his control — correcting things he didn't choose.
- Hitori trains with them physically. No power. Just presence. He is getting stronger in the way ordinary people get stronger, and no one comments on it.

**End Image:** The group exhausted, bruised, still standing. Ku watches Kaito specifically. Kaito notices. Neither speaks.

---

### Sequence B — What Ku and Sora Carry (Pages 13–30)

**POV:** Sora → Ku (FLASHBACK — the K.E.F. mission, revealed to the READER)

**This is the dramatic irony material.** The reader learns what Ku and Sora did before the Tidal Plate. The group does not.

**Start Image:** A K.E.F. incident report. A child's name, reduced to a line that can be crossed out. "EXPOSURE RISK" in institutional red.

**The Briefing.** A K.E.F. superior delivers the order in the language of procedure: a civilian has accessed records of Crown resource allocation to the Tidal Plate. Medical supply rerouting. Evacuation postponements. Infrastructure funding diverted. The civilian is a child. The order is elimination. This is called "containment."

**Ku Refuses.** Flatly. Without theatrics. He says no, turns, and the room temperature changes. He walks out. The door does not close behind him — Sora notices it remain open, which means someone expected her to follow.

**Sora Tries.** She accepts the order in the same procedural language it was given. She tells herself this is what protocol looks like. She locates the child — ordinary, frightened, small, holding a torn folder with names and numbers they shouldn't have seen. The child asks Sora if she's there to help.

**The Halt.** Sora reaches the decisive position. Her hands move toward the child. They stop. Mending activates on a torn sleeve or broken latch — a repair that fixes nothing about the situation and everything about what Sora can no longer pretend. She cannot complete the order.

**The Route.** She creates the appearance of compliance. A report filed. A route left unlatched. The child disappears into a gap between procedures. Sora stays behind long enough to make the disappearance look like a completed operation, then follows Ku through a different door.

**End Image (Flashback):** Ku and Sora, apart but moving in the same direction. Behind them, the K.E.F. facility recedes. Ahead: open water, and the Tidal Plate beyond it.

### Sequence B, Continued — Present Day

**POV:** Sora / Mel

A training exercise goes slightly wrong. Something breaks — physically or socially. Sora is already apologising before anyone's said a word to her.

Mel is the one who notices. Not calling it out directly — just watching Sora's hands, the way she disappears into repair work the moment something is imperfect. First real demonstration of Mending: the object fixes, and Sora's hands tremble afterward, small enough that she hides them.

Mel does not ask. She places a bandage near Sora without a word — the same way she placed one for Hitori. Sora looks at it. Looks at Mel. Neither says what they're both thinking.

**End Image (Chapter 3):** Sora holding the bandage. Mel already turned away. Two people who deflect care in the same direction, now aware of each other's shape.

---

## Chapter 4 — "What Everyone Carries"

**Chapter thesis:** Every person in the group has a Pressure they cannot name. Some of them are getting closer to naming it. Some of them never will.

**Pages (approx.):** 38–44

---

### Sequence A — Aiden, Unwatched (Pages 1–10)

**POV:** Aiden

Aiden trains alone, thinking no one's around. The shackles hold clean when he's not performing for anyone. He clocks the discrepancy and immediately reaches for any explanation that isn't the true one.

Sora catches the tail end of this. She doesn't say anything — but the audience sees her clock it too, the way she clocks everything.

**End Image:** Aiden staring at his own hands. The shackles still faintly visible. He looks almost confused — like something just happened that shouldn't have, and the explanation is too uncomfortable to hold.

---

### Sequence B — Kaito and Mel, Adjacent (Pages 11–20)

**POV:** Kaito / Mel

A quiet scene. Mel offers him something small — rest, food, a moment of sitting still. He tries to redirect it the way he redirects everything about himself. It doesn't fully land. But it doesn't fully bounce off either.

Something has shifted since the lake. They are standing slightly closer. They are not naming it.

Mel's bracelet glows faintly when she reaches toward him without thinking. She pulls her hand back. He notices. Neither of them says anything.

**End Image:** Two people, shoulder to shoulder, both looking forward. Neither looking at the other. Both aware of where the other is standing.

---

### Sequence C — The Code, Personally (Pages 21–30)

**POV:** Kaito

Kaito's Pressure tightens. The insomnia returns — more revision, less sleep. He has a real conversation with Ku — not a lesson, closer to two people circling the same wound without naming it.

Ku doesn't open up. Kaito notices that Ku doesn't open up. Neither of them says so.

The Code stops being background noise here. Someone Kaito respects says something in passing about enduring quietly, and for the first time it doesn't sound like wisdom to him. It sounds like a description of what's wrong with him.

**End Image:** Kaito alone. The ceiling. The blue eye, open. But this time — something different. He is not just revising. He is beginning to question.

---

### Sequence D — Hitori (Pages 31–38)

**POV:** Hitori

Hitori does not have a dramatic moment. He is simply present throughout the chapter — carrying things, fixing things, being the centre of gravity no one names.

The anchor motif returns. Still warm. Still reading as stability.

One small beat: someone asks Hitori if he's okay. He says yes. The audience should not believe him. The audience should not know exactly why.

**End Image (Chapter 4):** Hitori alone, briefly. A quiet moment — the first time we've seen him without the group around him. He looks at something in his hand — a charm, a piece of dock equipment, a small anchor. Puts it away. Moves on.

---

## Chapter 5 — "Contact"

**Chapter thesis:** The world outside the Tidal Plate has noticed the group. Two kinds of attention arrive at once: one that documents, and one that hunts.

**Pages (approx.):** 42–48

---

### Sequence A — The Clown (Pages 1–16)

**POV:** Ensemble → Hyoga Kira

**Start Image:** The group's first encounter with a Ku-Clown — almost certainly Hyoga Kira.

Brief. Unsettling. Theatrical. He isn't hostile — he's *delighted*. He knows details about the Yoshi incident he shouldn't have access to. He's documenting, not attacking. The scene should feel like meeting someone who's already three steps ahead of you in a conversation you didn't know you were having.

He leaves without violence. The group is more unsettled by that than they would be by a fight.

Ku is visibly disturbed in a way the group has not seen before. He does not explain why.

**End Image:** Hyoga's parting smile. The group standing in the space where he was. The air still unsettled.

---

### Sequence B — Direct Contact (Pages 17–30)

**POV:** Ku / the group

The K.E.F. makes direct contact. Not a battle — an encounter that makes the shift explicit: this group is no longer adjacent to the problem. They *are* the problem now. Someone official says enough, coldly, to make clear how much bigger this is than any of them understood.

Ku is pushed toward the moment where he has to explain more — the Tenebri, his own history, what the K.E.F. actually protects — and doesn't. He almost does. He starts a sentence and doesn't finish it.

Sora watches him not finish it and says nothing. She knows exactly what's underneath the unfinished sentence.

**End Image:** Ku's unfinished sentence. Sora's knowing silence. The group understanding, incompletely, that Ku is protecting them from something he hasn't named yet.

---

### Sequence C — Arc Close (Pages 31–42)

**POV:** Kaito

The group walking back toward whatever is left of normal. Yoshi's gifts are still in their pockets, their bags, their rooms. No one throws them away.

The goal established in Chapter 1 — *find out why the Tidal Plate's truth is hidden* — has not been answered. But it has been given urgency. The K.E.F. wants them quiet. The Ku-Clowns want them documented. The Crown wants them forgotten.

Kaito looks at the group — Mel, Aiden, Hitori, Ku, Sora — and arrives at something that is not yet hope but is not despair:

> *"They're hiding something. That means there's something worth finding."*

**End Image (Chapter 5):** The Tidal Plate from above. Six figures small against the dock infrastructure. Fog rolling in from the water. The Crown's towers visible in the far distance — clean, orderly, watching. The blue right eye visible on the smallest figure.

**Arc 1 close.** The group knows they're being hunted. They don't yet know by what, or entirely why. Ku's silence is no longer protective — it's becoming a liability, and everyone in the room can feel it. Hitori is quiet the whole walk home. No one asks why — he is always quiet. It should read as something to the audience.

## Rooftop Scene — Placement Confirmed

**Scene:** Kaito's rooftop spiral → Mel's chase → the lake → "The moon sure does look beautiful, doesn't it"
**Placed in:** **Chapter 2, Sequence B** (pages 17–36)
**Arc:** Arc 1 (after the K.E.F. escape begins, before the Black Box Tests)

**Why this placement works:**
- Kaito has not yet been classified. The rooftop crisis is where his right eye first activates, and the later Black Box Test gives language to what happened without turning the Awakening into a diagnostic reward.
- The insomnia that was seeded in Chapter 1 now escalates into self-harm (the fingernails digging into his arms). This is the worst his Pressure has been — and the story needs to show it before he begins to manage it.
- Mel's chase is the first time we see her home life and her mother. It deepens her character at exactly the moment the story needs the audience to understand why she gives care so freely and receives it so poorly.
- The lake is intimate but not romantic — it is two people being present for each other in a way neither of them knows how to name. This is the foundation the romance will build on.
- "The moon sure does look beautiful, doesn't it" is the first confession that is not a confession. It lands in Chapter 2 so the audience has three arcs to sit with what it means before either character says it plainly.
