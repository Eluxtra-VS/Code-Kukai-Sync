## Short Definition

The **Black Box Test** is a diagnostic and training method used to identify a person's **Type** after [[Awakening]], and to develop their [[Luz]] control over time. It requires no prior knowledge of how Luz works — only that the person has awakened and can attempt to use it.

> _The name refers to both the dark environment required and the object produced — and doubles as a reference to black box testing in software: evaluating functionality based on input and output, without needing to understand the internal structure. A beginner with no knowledge of how Luz works can still perform this test and produce a meaningful result._

---

## Requirements

- The user must have already [[Awakening|awakened]].
- Must be performed in a **dark area** — ambient light interferes with reading the output clearly.

---

## How to Perform It

**Step 1:** Find a sufficiently dark area.

**Step 2:** Envision forming a cube in your hand. Hold the image clearly — the shape, the weight, the edges. This step can take anywhere from seconds to several minutes depending on the person. Struggling here is normal. Clarity comes with practice.

**Step 3:** Observe the result.

---

## Results by Type

### Akarui

> A cube forms just above or directly on the hand, emitting light.

The construct appears externally — distinct from the body, hovering or resting. Size and clarity of shape vary based on the person's current Luz experience. A new Akarui may produce a dim, shapeless glow. A more developed one produces a defined, bright cube.

The result confirms **external expression** — [[Luz]] is leaving the body and taking form in the world.

### Weaver

> No cube forms. The hand itself emits light.

The person's hand (or another body part) glows — but nothing separates from it. No matter how hard they try to form the cube externally, the light stays inside the body. Size and brightness vary with experience.

The result confirms **internal expression** — Luz routes through the body rather than projecting outward.

### Reiki

> A cube forms on a held/touched/worn object, not on the hand. If no object being touched, held or worn, no cube forms at all.

The construct appears on whatever the person is holding — an object in their grip — rather than on or above their hand. If they put the object down and try again, nothing happens. If they pick it up, the cube returns.

The result confirms **displaced expression** — Luz travels into objects rather than projecting outward or routing inward.

## As a Training Tool

The Black Box Test is not just diagnostic — it is the foundation of early Luz training for all three Types.

- **Shape complexity:** Start with a cube. Attempt a sphere, a cylinder, more complex geometry. The harder the shape, the more control is required.
- **Size:** Increase gradually. Larger output requires more stable Luz flow.
- **Brightness/clarity:** A dim or blurry result means Luz is unstable — usually tied to unresolved [[Pressure]] or a weak [[Bond Stages|bond]].
- **Speed:** How quickly can the result be produced? In combat, hesitation is a liability.

Progress in the Black Box Test directly reflects progress in Luz control. Characters who have developed their abilities significantly can produce complex, brilliant results instantly. Characters under emotional stress produce weaker, less stable ones — making it a quiet diagnostic for internal state as well as skill.

---

## Who Teaches It

For the main cast, the Black Box Test is introduced by [[Ku Mochizuki]]. For most awakened people outside the main cast, early Luz output is **uncontrolled and instinctive** — Akarui expel it uncontrollably from their body, Weavers experience sudden physical buffs they can't regulate. The Black Box Test is one of the first steps toward turning that raw output into something intentional.
