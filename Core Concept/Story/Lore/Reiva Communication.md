## Short Definition

Communication between a host and their [[Reiva]] is not automatic. The Reiva is present and aware, but speaking directly — as opposed to receiving impressions and instincts — requires specific conditions. There are three known methods, each with different availability and limitations.

> _The Reiva can always choose not to respond. Every method listed here opens a channel. None of them guarantee an answer._

---

## Method 1 — At Awakening

When a person first [[Awakening|awakens]], a brief window opens where direct communication with the Reiva is possible. The Reiva has been dormant and observing for potentially years — and the moment of Awakening is the first time it steps forward.

During this window:

- The host can speak to the Reiva directly, as if addressing a person in the same room.
- The Reiva **occasionally speaks back** — not always, and not at length.
- This is often the host's first real sense of the Reiva as a distinct entity rather than an ambient presence.

The window does not last indefinitely. As the Awakening stabilises and the immediate intensity fades, direct communication becomes harder again without other methods.

---

## Method 2 — Earphones

Specially manufactured earphones that interface with the [[Luz]] system. When the user **inputs Luz into the earphones**, a communication channel opens between host and Reiva.

**Properties:**

- Allows direct conversation for a **limited period of time** — the channel stays open as long as Luz is being fed into the device and both parties are engaged.
- The Reiva **can choose to end the conversation** at any point — cutting the channel from their side regardless of how much Luz the host is putting in.
- Works at any [[Bond Stages|bond stage]], but communication quality and Reiva willingness to engage tends to reflect the current state of the bond.

**Manufacture:** To be confirmed — likely [[Gear District]] adjacent, possibly older technology.

**Limitations:**

- Costs Luz to maintain.
- The Reiva's willingness is not guaranteed. A Reiva that is frustrated, stressed, or disengaged may simply go quiet.
- Does not work if the [[Bond Stages|bond is severely damaged]].

---

## Method 3 — Anchor (Reiki Only)

[[Reiki]] can communicate with their Reiva through **any seeded Anchor at any time**, without Luz cost and without a time limit.

- The channel through an Anchor is always passively open as long as the Anchor exists.
- The Reiva may or may not respond — the channel being open doesn't compel them to use it.
- This is the most consistent communication access of any method, which is part of why Reiki develop unusually intimate bonds with their Reiva despite the technique being looked down on by others.

---

## Method 4 — Overdrive Recovery

After [[Extractors|Overdrive]], the host is rendered inactive for days to weeks. During this period, the [[Reiva]] recovers faster and is conscious while the host is not yet fully active.

This creates an involuntary communication window — the host is stripped of all distraction and the Reiva has uninterrupted access. See [[Extractors]] for full detail.

This is not a method anyone chooses. It is a consequence. But it is one of the most significant communication events in any bond's history.

---

## General Notes

- The Reiva's **personality affects how it communicates** — some are talkative, some are sparse, some communicate in images and sensations rather than words.
- At early [[Bond Stages|bond stages]], communication tends to be fragmented and one-directional. At Union, the distinction between impression and language often collapses — communication becomes something between thought and speech.
- A Reiva that has gone silent across all methods is a warning sign. It does not mean the bond is broken — but it means something is wrong.
