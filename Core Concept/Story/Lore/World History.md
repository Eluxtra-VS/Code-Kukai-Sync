## Overview

The world of Code: Kukai is set **roughly 96 years after 2026** — a world rebuilt from near-total ruin, carrying the scars of a catastrophic conflict and the legends of those who ended it.

---
## The Last World War

In 2026, tensions between nations escalated into a **third and final world war** — a conflict of unprecedented scale that reshaped the physical surface of the planet itself, erasing borders, coastlines, and entire civilisations.

The war did not end through politics or surrender. It ended because of **seven individuals**.

---

## The Seven

Seven people — later called the **[[Tenebri]]** — emerged during the war's peak with abilities no other human possessed: command of [[Rei]], supernatural physical capacity, and bond with [[Reiva]] at a depth the world had never witnessed.

Together, they stopped the war **single-handedly**.

The effects of the conflict were irreversible:

- **97% of the global population was lost.**
- Almost all prior knowledge — cultural, technological, historical — was destroyed.
- The shape of the land itself was altered permanently.

The Seven were seen as **deities**. They were worshipped, obeyed, and elevated to the status of royalty.

---

## The Fall of the Seven

Power did not last cleanly.

After years of reverence, the Seven **lost control** — whether through [[Pressure]] collapse, corrupted [[Kuro]] exposure, or something more complex remains debated. They became power-hungry and began killing people, dismantling the peace they had built.

The world's view of them inverted entirely.

They went from saviours to destroyers. From gods to monsters.

They were named **Tenebri** — _darkness_ in an old tongue — not as a title of honour, but as a warning. The name stuck.

---

## The Banishment

After the Seven were stopped (the exact method is either unknown, legendary, or deliberately obscured), the world sought to contain what remained: **their bloodlines**.

Relatives of the Tenebri were identified, gathered, and **banished to the Tidal Plate** — formerly known as _Perish Island_, a place already infamous for:

- "Taking people under the tide."
- "Pulling you into the fog, never to return."

The intention was isolation. What the banishers did not anticipate was that civilisation would **flourish there anyway**.

---

## The World After

The rebuilt world reorganised into distinct plates (regions), each developing its own identity and specialisation:

|Plate|Character|
|---|---|
|[[Tidal Plate]]|Outcasts; seen as rebels or criminals|
|[[Sovereign's Crown]]|Wealthy; skilled in trade and knowledge|
|[[Savor Central]]|Food and medicine production|
|[[Gear District]]|Engineering and mechanical industry|
|[[The Calamity]]|No man's land; constant conflict; never safe|

---

## The Night of the Masked Devils

At some point in the decades following the war, **one night went completely dark** — no prediction, no warning. Across every plate, reports emerged of **"mutated animals"** attacking civilians.

These were [[Kukai]].

No official explanation was given. The event has never repeated. Over time it faded into **legend**, whispered about but never verified — which suits those who prefer people remain unaware.

---

## Present Day

Kaito Amori, his friends, and much of the [[Tidal Plate]] population are **descendants of the original Tenebri bloodlines**. They were born into banishment, grew up as outsiders, and carry the inherited stigma of their ancestors — without knowing the full truth of what those ancestors were.

The [[Tenebri]] villain faction still exists in the present, operating from the shadows of [[The Calamity]]. They are a successor faction shaped by the legacy of the original Seven.
