## Pressure

Seong's Pressure is expressive and explosive: he accumulates it fast and vents it fast, refusing to dwell on emotion until it hardens. His answer to any feeling is to make things move: laugh, fight, joke, run, escalate, improvise. Because it rarely sits still, it rarely rots. The failure mode is conversion speed. You can see it working in him constantly: the joke mid-exchange, the fight accepted for its own sake, the stakes raised before anyone asked, the inability to let a quiet moment stay quiet.

## Link to Ability

Under the rule that Class follows deepest desire rather than function, Seong's deepest desire is the feeling of being fully alive in motion. That makes his Class Pulse, the affinity of exchange and rhythm. His Extractor Anchor works as a key, opening more ways to move, more speed, more danger, more play at a higher ceiling. **Pure Extraction** is the desire made weapon: specialised Luz/Kuro bullets fired through the Extractor, their effects tuned by ratio and emotional state. His favourite application is forcing opponents toward Overdrive, because a bigger, faster, more alive fight is the closest thing he knows to freedom.

## Backstory

Seong grew up bored and boxed in, in the [[Gear District]]. His answer to Pressure became movement, jokes, risk, and play. If he can make the moment feel alive, the Pressure does not get to sit still long enough to rot. The strategy has worked since childhood, and it has never been tested against a moment that required stillness instead.

## Design

Design him as perpetual motion: loose, ready posture that never plants, weight rolling forward, hands never still. Bright, clashing accent colours against practical gear, the wardrobe of someone dressing for a good time rather than a war. His visual tell is the ammunition: Luz/Kuro rounds that shift tone with his emotional state, so his mood is legible right there in his loadout. In combat he grins through exchanges, takes angles nobody lined up, and turns the battlefield into an instrument. The Extractor should read as playful hardware, dangerous but worn like a favourite toy.

# Full Detail

## Core Personality

Silly, hyperactive, sporadic. Seong loves having fun, especially during battle: the fight is motion, exchange, improvisation, the feeling of being fully alive in the moment. He grew up bored and boxed in, and his answer to Pressure became movement, jokes, risk, and play.

## Pressure Profile

Type: Expressive and explosive. He accumulates [[Pressure]] quickly but vents it quickly, and does not like dwelling on emotion until it hardens. His Pressure resolution is **fun and freedom**. The Extractor matters because it opens more ways to move and fight: a higher ceiling, stranger angles, combat as the kind of exchange where he feels most free.

## Relationship to the Extractor

The Extractor is his Anchor and his mouthpiece. He uses it as a key: more capability, more speed, more danger, more play, more freedom to fight how he wants. The risk is real. Overdrive is dangerous, Forced Overdrive is ethically ugly. But pain is not what he is chasing; his emotional logic runs on one idea, that movement keeps him alive.

## Signature Ability: Pure Extraction

Affinity: Pulse. Pure Extraction lets Seong fire specialised Luz/Kuro bullets through his Extractor Anchor; the bullets carry different effects depending on the ratio and emotional state. His favourite application is forcing others toward Overdrive because it makes the fight bigger, faster, and more alive. That does not make it harmless: he can be funny and still be dangerous, playful and still be reckless.

## Relationship to Reiva

Extremely high compatibility with his [[Reiva]]. Low Kukai risk because he is expressive and rarely lets Pressure sit unprocessed. His danger is escalation: turning every feeling into motion before asking whether motion is what the situation needs.
