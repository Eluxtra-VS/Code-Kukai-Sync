## Pressure

Sora moves toward damage with the automatic assumption that if she is there, the damage is in some way hers to address. Her first instinct when something goes wrong skips panic and goes straight to causality: where was she standing, what did she miss, what should she have done three seconds earlier, three days earlier, three years earlier? If Sora is in the room and something breaks, some part of her believes the room has already named her.

## Link to Ability

She internalises everything, blame and structure alike; when something breaks, she locates the repair inside herself first. That makes her a Weaver. Her Thread is her **hands** because hands are where responsibility becomes action: hands hold, fix, reach, fail to catch, fail to stop, fail to save. Mend is Weaver Vigor. Forge wants to build something real; Sora wants her hands to *be enough* not to ruin what they touch. Corrupt is the inverted mirror of Beat-Break: it amplifies when her self-belief is dishonest. Her worst belief, *I ruin everything I touch*, is literal fuel.

## Backstory

She was born in a lower dockside tier of the [[Tidal Plate]] called the Netmenders' Tier, where her mother mended nets and taught her to count while tying and to finish every knot with a double loop. At eight she survived the tier's Kukai incident and was evacuated overnight with the other unaccompanied minors; her mother was never accounted for, and the Plate's records were never corrected. She passed through Crown institutional care, was aptitude-screened with the other displaced children, and entered the K.E.F. pipeline young — the institution that processed her disaster became the institution she needed to believe in. Full backstory canon, including the childhood connection to [[Aiden Takeda]] she recognized on sight and hid: [[Arc 2 — The Knot and the Announcement]].

Worked within the K.E.F. alongside Ku; assigned missions together until the pattern was too consistent to be coincidence. She believed in the work longer than he did. More accurately, she *needed* to believe: if the institution was wrong, everything she had done under its authority became harder to survive. Then came the Order: eliminate a civilian who discovered the Crown's deliberate neglect of the Tidal Plate. Ku refused immediately. Sora tried to carry it out. She reached the civilian, held position, and could not do it. The civilian survived and disappeared. She carries the fact that she tried, and it sits apart from failure. She has not told Ku the full truth of that moment.

## Design

She moves slightly behind the action, doing what holds everything together without claiming credit. Her hands are always busy with something: strap, wound, plan, repair. Apology posture arrives before anyone accuses her of anything. In combat her light reads practical, never performed.

# Full Detail

## Core Personality

Quiet competence, constant attention, responsibility claimed before anyone assigns it. She's reliable, which also makes her dangerous to herself.

## Relationship With Ku

They're not blood related, which is irrelevant. He taught her when to move and when to stay still. She is close enough to challenge him, to know when he is lying by omission, to be one of the few people whose disappointment can reach him. He sees her as family and would not say it easily. She knows anyway. Their closeness predates the group; they arrive as a unit with history others can feel before they understand it.

## K.E.F. History

She came to him for advice often enough that neither named the pattern. Pairing them may have made both easier to predict, and the possibility is left sitting there.

## Why Weaver

Her Pressure says: *if I had been better, this would not have happened.* Wanting to improve herself was never the heart of it. The heart of it is believing the dangerous thing is whatever happens if she doesn't hold herself correctly. See [[Weaver]], [[Vigor]].

## Mend: Scope

Can mend: broken tools and structures; torn straps/bindings/weapons/clothing; damaged Forge constructs; at higher mastery, surface wounds and Rei channel disruptions. Cannot easily: reverse death; undo deep psychological Pressure; restore what she does not understand; fix someone refusing to be fixed. The limitation matters: her arc requires learning that not everything broken is asking for her hands. Full reference: [[Mend]].

## Arc Direction

- **Early:** the capable one behind the capable one. Ku moves first; Sora makes the movement hold.
- **Middle:** the group notices she apologises for things no one accused her of. The more useful Mend becomes, the easier it is to hide inside repair work. [[Mel Silva]] notices the self-erasure pattern early; [[Kaito Amori]] recognises the self-correction and does not like how familiar it feels.
- **Pressure point:** the Order's truth surfaces: she tried before she stopped. It should hurt Ku because he loves her, and it should land as love rather than rejection.
- **Breakthrough:** fails to fix something and does not collapse into self-blame; stays present without taking ownership of all damage. Corrupt loses its grip.
- **Resolution:** repair becomes care instead of confession. She can still Corrupt when it is the honest choice rather than the self-hating one.
