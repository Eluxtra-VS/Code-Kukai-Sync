## Pressure

Ren's pressure is conditional belonging: every group he landed in wanted a different version of him, so he stopped offering the real one. He reads a room the way other people breathe, taking in accent, posture, humour, values, and becomes what fits. He experiences this as competence, and it works well enough that nobody, Ren included, has flagged it as erasure.

## Link to Ability

The desire underneath is Forge's desire, to make something real, present, impossible to dismiss, except the thing Ren builds is himself, rebuilt per audience. Weaver because the construction turns inward: passive whole-body malleability as baseline, complete transformation at Thread activation. The automatic danger-adaptation is the coping made autonomous, the body choosing the surviving shape before Ren chooses who to be. The honesty gate inverts his whole pattern: forms reach their ceiling only when chosen for himself rather than assembled for approval. Full mechanics: [[Chimera]].

## Backstory

Raised on the Gear District, where practicality is cultural currency and a person is worth what they produce. His parents' situation collapsed early; he was shuffled between relatives and work crews, learning fast that usefulness bought kindness and uselessness bought nothing. His Reiva answered during a factory-floor collapse: the adaptation triggered before he understood it, and a shape that should not exist pulled him out of wreckage no human spine should have walked away from. Word of an awakened shapeshifter travelled exactly as far as the Ku-Clown pipeline needed it to: prime merchandise for people who capture, study, and exploit the transformed. He was taken mid-Arc 3, in transit through neutral freight channels no plate would question.

## Design

Restless features that never quite settle, the sense that any still image of his face is slightly wrong. Eyes shift a little with forms. Gear District work clothes layered over everything, worn like a uniform for a job he left. In combat, transformation is fluid and beautiful, and that is the horror of his Worse state: the transformations stay beautiful while the person inside gets quieter.

# Full Detail

## Thematic Function

Ren is [[Hitori Tanaka]]'s dark mirror. Hitori never changed himself for anyone and was consumed by what staying cost. Ren always changed himself and is dissolving by instalments. Two failure modes of self, total rigidity and total fluidity, bracket the true Code between them: change honestly or stay honestly, but choose, and remain the chooser.

He also shares a birth plate with [[Mel Silva]], though they never met there: both raised on usefulness-as-worth, one responding by giving everything away, the other by becoming whatever was asked for.

## Story Placement

- **Mid-Arc 3:** encountered via the Ku-Clown capture pipeline: the group intercepts cargo meant for study/dissection. He is cargo that can look like anything, including rescue personnel.
- **Late Arc 3 → Arc 4:** distrust phase (he lies reflexively about who he is); joins the group in the post-Hitori aftermath, when the anchor-shaped hole needs someone and Ren needs somewhere to stop performing.
- **Open decision:** whether Kashi Kizu takes personal interest in him. A boy whose power is institutionalised self-erasure is either Kashi's favourite proof or his hardest case.

## Combat Notes

Automatic adaptation makes him nearly impossible to ambush: the body reacts to threats he hasn't consciously registered. Against Fracture-type suppression attacks he is paradoxically resistant, because you cannot fracture the suppression of someone who has already dissolved the suppressor. Against honest opponents who simply *see* him, he folds faster than any tier of hardness could protect against.
