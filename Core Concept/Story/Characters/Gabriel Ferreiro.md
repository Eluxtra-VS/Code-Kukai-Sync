## Pressure

Gabriel believes, quietly and completely, that only the strong deserve to stay. Weakness in others he forgives; weakness in himself he treats as a countdown to being left. Where Aiden performs belief, Gabriel proves strength, over and over, structurally, the way you reinforce a wall that everyone you love lives behind.

## Link to Ability

Vigor's desire is the self made more: embodied, sufficient. Gabriel answered it with permanence: be hit, remain, be seen remaining. Weaver because nothing leaves him; the amplification lives entirely inside his frame, passive durability always on, reaching its ceiling at Thread activation with total-body hardening under maximum voluntary flex. His resolve gate mirrors Beat-Break's honesty gate: hardness holds its ceiling only while his will holds unbroken. And his scars, every one taken shielding someone else, glow in unison as the hardness climbs: pain history synchronising into visible proof. Full mechanics: [[Flex]].

## Backstory

The earliest scars came from home: a violent father, a younger sibling and mother standing behind whatever part of Gabriel was biggest that night. He learned to read footsteps on stairs before he learned long division. When his mother finally got them out, they carried nothing but each other and his scars. Years later Savor Central, the plate too valuable to attack, too gentle to defend itself, suffered a localised Kukai incursion. Gabriel, sixteen and untrained, stood in doorways he had no business standing in and took what came through. He survived. Some of the people behind him did too. The deep scars date from that night; so did his Awakening, arriving mid-impact as his body refused to break on schedule. Afterward he trained, obsessively, because next time the wall needed to hold longer.

## Design

Big, calm, deliberate: economy of motion that reads as gentleness until it reads as immovability. Wears short sleeves without apology; the scars are on display, mapped across arms and torso like a record he chose to keep readable. Portuguese phrases surface when tired or moved: soft blessings, his grandmother's cadences. In combat the visual grammar is geological: weight settling, ground accepting him, the matte density spreading, and the scars kindling in sync, brighter with every tier, pulsing together with his heartbeat.

# Full Detail

## Personality

Warm and stubbornly gentle outside combat: the man who carries crates for strangers and remembers children's names. Inside combat he goes absolute. Rage plays no part in it; the register is geological. He doesn't fight angry, he fights settled, and his will is the story's benchmark for what "takes a lot to bring down" means.

## Thematic Function

Gabriel is partial proof of the true Code working: his coping is open endurance rather than suppression. He hides nothing, which is why Kashi's Fracture finds so little grip in him. But his flaw runs adjacent to suppression: hardness-as-worth is its own quiet lie, and the Worse state (numbness, the living statue) is what the misread Code would eventually make of him if he stopped choosing softness on purpose. Where [[Hitori Tanaka]] shows what anchoring costs when it is never refused, Gabriel shows what armour costs when it is never taken off.

## Story Placement

- **Arc 4 aftermath entry:** arrives in the wake of Hitori's loss. The group needs someone solid, and he needs somewhere his strength is not the only thing anyone sees. He works as a contrast engine to the group's grief: steady where everything shook.
- **Relationship hooks:** [[Mel Silva]] recognises the Savor Central survivors' guilt in him immediately; [[Aiden Takeda]] mistakes performance for kinship and gets corrected slowly; [[Kaito Amori]] meets someone whose self-audit happens out loud and finds it disorienting.
- **Open decision:** whether his incursion night connects to larger Kukai movement patterns (ecology thread) or stays local tragedy.

## Combat Notes

Functions as area denial and evacuation cover: the walking wall that buys seconds nobody else can buy. Weakest against mobility and patience: opponents who refuse contact starve his relevance, and sustained Tier Three drains Rei in minutes. Strongest precisely where the group is weakest after Arc 3: holding ground while others grieve.
