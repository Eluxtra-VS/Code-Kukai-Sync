## Pressure

Kaito edits his sentences before he says them. Everyone gets the second draft. He is patient with other people's weakness and harsh with his own, and the harshness usually speaks first. It shows up worst at night: he replays conversations, finds what he said wrong, builds better versions, and fixes nothing by morning. Heavy or social days make it worse because more contact means more material to review. When he says he's fine and just thinking, he never is.

## Link to Ability

He turns problems inward by default, so he's a Weaver. His Thread is both eyes because the eyes are where being seen happens, and he has wanted to correct the blue right eye since he was old enough to want things. Divergence runs on honest focus. If he starts editing himself mid-action, what he sees gets less accurate and his State returns come back wrong. Full mechanics live in [[Divergence]].

## Backstory

[[Alice Amori]] raises him alone and works the docks in the fishing trade. School attendance is spotty. People have stared at his eyes his whole life, and he doesn't know two things: that they mark a Reiva bonded to him before birth, and what his mother knows about his father.

## Design

Right eye blue, left eye brown. The one flaw he can't fix sits at the top of a long list of flaws he's always fixing. He moves like he's late even when he isn't. Animate the editing itself: rehearsed posture, pre-filtered expressions, the half-second delay of someone choosing the corrected version. Let the filter slip occasionally around [[Mel Silva]].

# Full Detail

## Appearance

He looks restless even standing still. The heterochromia is the first thing anyone notices, and he has known people were looking since he was small. There's a list of things about himself he keeps trying to correct, and the thing at the top of it refuses to be corrected.

## Core Personality

People find him easy to be around. Attentive, genuine, quick to notice when someone's struggling. They trust him and feel seen around him.

---

## The Core Word: Inadequacy

The pressure under everything is the belief that the version of himself people might see isn't enough to survive being seen.

Structurally his sin mirror is Pride folded into shame, so shame-pride. He doesn't act superior. He holds himself to a standard nobody could meet and treats each weakness as humiliation instead of information. Readers shouldn't think "pride" when they look at him. They should think he's ashamed of being unfinished. His arc lands on this: being seen while incomplete isn't failure.

It separates him from [[Aiden Takeda]], who performs belief he doesn't have, and from [[Mai Takeda]], who stopped needing recognition altogether. Kaito edits himself so there's nothing left to find.

## Why He's a Weaver

A Weaver turns Pressure back on himself because he believes the problem lives inside him and can be corrected if he works hard enough. Filtering a sentence before it leaves his mouth is a Weaver act. The right eye confirms the pattern: a Weaver Threads whatever part of themselves they believe most needs fixing. He spent fifteen years believing the right eye was wrong. It was never wrong. It's where his power lives.

## Awakening: Yoshi as Catalyst

## Mel Silva

Kaito takes the wrong lesson. Under Pressure he decides the unacceptable parts of himself have to go before he ends up like Yoshi. That's a Weaver resolution and it's misdirected. His Awakening gives him power through self-correction, and later the true Code shows him that correction without honesty is suppression wearing a better face.

Sequence: he stays unawakened through the incident itself, grabbing Mel's hand and running while Aiden makes the distraction. The first partial Awakening hits during the Chapter 2 rooftop crisis. The Black Box Test afterwards places him as Weaver, and Ku pauses longer on the result than he lets show.

## Insomnia

It flares after emotionally heavy days and days spent around lots of people. By morning he has rewritten everything and resolved nothing. Use it as the audience's gauge for how close he is: slower reactions, flat answers, eyes that don't quite focus, deflections that come too fast.

When Mel notices, it should feel close. She offers food, rest, quiet, and he converts it into concern for her. When Ku notices, it should feel uncomfortable. He knows the shape from his own life, says something too precise, and Kaito dodges because it landed too well.

## Distractions

Skipping school. Exploring abandoned buildings. Dock work with his mother. Martial arts. The group. None of it fixes anything. These are exits from the revision, and he got good at finding exits quickly, which means he got good at never stopping long enough to see what he's revising.

## Mel Silva

Mel sees the unedited version and doesn't treat it as a problem. Around her the revision quiets. Not stops, quiets, which is more than he's found anywhere else. He calls it something easier than what it is, and he'll probably keep doing that for a while. This is the primary romance and it runs under every arc.

## Thread: Both Eyes

The Reiva bonded to him before birth. The blue right eye is its most visible mark, not a genetic accident. Each eye carries a separate function and they don't swap jobs.

## Ability Summary: Divergence

Full reference: [[Divergence]].

- **Left eye, Rate:** changes how fast Rei flows through whatever he connects to. Targets drag behind themselves or move before they can steer. Not time manipulation.
- **Right eye, State:** moves a target's Rei toward an earlier or later state in its own history. Wounds return to before they happened. Structures age toward decay they would reach anyway. Nothing he never perceived, nothing past the natural endpoint.
- **Stages:** touch required at first, with State limited to himself. Then clear sight replaces touch. Then anything he consciously perceives in his field.
- **The gate:** State fails or comes back wrong without conscious honest focus. Looking at a wound while refusing to see what it means is exactly what Divergence punishes. Ku's power judges whether he feels cleanly. Kaito's judges whether he sees cleanly.

## Why It Has No Name Yet

Naming the ability is the moment he claims it outright. Niko can't take Divergence until Kaito says the name out loud within hearing, so the scene is tactical as well as personal. Who's there, what it costs, what it unlocks: all still open. See the roadmap.
