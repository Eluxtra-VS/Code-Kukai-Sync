# Friend Group

Index of Kaito's original friend group: the four characters present before Ku and Aoki join mid-story.

## Files

- [[Kaito Amori]] - protagonist; 15; Kyumin at story start; awakens into Weaver Vigor during Arc 1; Thread: both eyes; signature Divergence is unnamed in-story until the claiming scene; sin mirror is Shame-Pride / Inadequacy, not Wrath.
- [[Hitori Tanaka]] - Kyumin who does not awaken; does not take the Black Box Test; becomes Kukai; suppressed desire for freedom; Relic: wings; uncle is Yeshi Tanaka.
- [[Aiden Takeda]] - Kyumin at story start; awakens into Akarui Forge during Arc 1; Shackles signature; Pride as need for recognition; older sibling is Mai Takeda.
- [[Mel Silva]] - Kyumin at story start; awakens into Reiki during Arc 1; signature Flare; Pressure = care outward, never inward; her father worked for Sonya Vance's organisation.
