## Main Cast (standalone files)

- [[Ku Mochizuki]] — reluctant mentor; Weaver Vigor; Thread: Heart; Beat-Break signature; Black Box survivor; former K.E.F. operative; shortened his name from Kukai as the first act of self-reclamation. Joins the friend group mid-story.
- [[Aoki Sora]] — Weaver Vigor; Pressure = self-blame as default; Mend signature with Unmake destructive mode (inverted mirror of Beat-Break); left K.E.F. for her own reasons; recruited by Ku. Joins the friend group mid-story.
- [[Kashi Kizu]] — independent antagonist; Akarui Veil; Fracture signature; Black Box survivor; believes Kukai transformation is the superior outcome; multiple personalities; the person who walked out the same door as Ku and drew the opposite conclusion.
- [[Alice Amori]] — Kaito's mother; dock worker; Kyumin (no awakening trajectory); knows everything about the Tenebri, Rei, K.E.F. and the system through Daiki; has not yet told Kaito.
- [[Seong Ki]] — Reiki Pulse; Pure Extraction signature (specialised Luz/Kuro bullets including forced Overdrive); from Gear District; extremely expressive; low Kukai risk; high Reiva compatibility.
- [[Ren Adeyemi]] — Weaver Forge; Chimera signature (transformation into real, extinct, and mythical composite forms; automatic danger-adaptation); Gear District born; Pressure = conditional belonging. Independent wildcard introduced mid-Arc 3 via the Ku-Clown pipeline; group addition in Arc 4.
- [[Gabriel Ferreiro]] — Weaver Vigor; Flex signature (escalating visible stone-hardness; scar-synchrony); Savor Central survivor of a localised Kukai incursion; Portuguese family line; Pressure = provable strength. Joins in the Arc 4 aftermath.
