# Tenebri

## Short Definition

The **Tenebri** are a faction of fully awakened individuals operating from the shadows of the Calamity. There are seven seats, each representing one destructive tendency taken to its endpoint.

They are not the original Seven. They are a successor faction that claims the shape and legacy of the Seven while twisting it into something darker.

The Tenebri are what the story becomes if Pressure is never resolved: power without accountability, pain without understanding, and desire without love.

## Former Gluttony - Daiki Amori

Daiki Amori previously held the Gluttony seat.

He did not leave because he became morally clean. He left because the Tenebri began to feel like a ceiling. Their structure, secrecy, and hierarchy were useful to him until they were not. Daiki wanted more power, more access, and more room to grow than the organisation would allow.

This is why Daiki still represents Gluttony even after leaving: his hunger did not disappear. It redirected.

Ori now holds the current Gluttony seat. Ori is the first Kukai deliberately created by Jakar Offei and is treated as Gluttony in the current Tenebri structure. The fact that a Kukai holds a Tenebri sin seat is deliberately unsettling and should remain a source of unanswered tension.

## Power Level

Each Tenebri member is capable of conquering a full plate single-handedly with ease. This is not exaggeration in-world. It is the understated truth.

---

## Structure and the Seven Sins

Whether the seven-sin structure was self-assigned or imposed by outside observers is deliberately unanswered.

The important point is that the sins are not costumes. Each member genuinely embodies a failure to resolve Pressure:

- Pride becomes superiority as self-protection.
- Wrath becomes punishment disguised as justice.
- Envy becomes identity theft through desire.
- Sloth becomes refusal to act because action would require responsibility.
- Gluttony becomes hunger that cannot be satisfied.
- Greed becomes extraction from anything offered freely.
- Lust becomes the need to control contact, attention, and desire.

Together, they are the dark mirror of the Code.
