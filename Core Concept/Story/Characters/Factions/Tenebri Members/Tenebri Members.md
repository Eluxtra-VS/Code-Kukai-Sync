## Files

- [[Mai Takeda]] - Sin of Pride; Akarui Forge; Monarch signature; Aiden's older sibling; God complex; does not need recognition because she has moved past needing it.
- [[Dante Vargas]] - Sin of Wrath; Akarui Veil; Judgement condition = intentional harm plus present guilt; former detective; K.E.F. adjacent.
- [[Niko Juul]] - Sin of Envy; Weaver Veil; Thread: ears; Rapt signature; from East Sovereign's Crown.
- [[Yeshi Tanaka]] - Sin of Sloth; Reiki Veil; Calming Fog ability; Hitori Tanaka's uncle; worked in the Gear District until he stopped caring about outcomes.
- [[Ori]] - Sin of Gluttony; Kukai; fully sentient; the first Kukai Jakar deliberately created; connected to Jakar via Warden; coherent windows during downtime; hunger is infinite.
- [[Sonya Vance]] - Sin of Greed; Reiki Veil; condition = someone voluntarily gives something up; hat Anchor with pocket dimension; runs an acquisition network; Mel Silva's father was in her organisation.
- [[Daiki Amori]] - former Sin of Gluttony; left the current/successor Tenebri; now lives on Tidal Plate; Akarui Veil; All Seeing Eye signature; Kaito's estranged father. Daiki was replaced by Ori in the current roster.
- [[Jakar Offei]] - Sin of Lust; Akarui Veil; Faultline (Warden is its extreme application — the permanent bond at a directly-caused transformation); deliberately manufactured Ori's transformation; exclusively Kuro; deeply tragic backstory centred on Ama.
