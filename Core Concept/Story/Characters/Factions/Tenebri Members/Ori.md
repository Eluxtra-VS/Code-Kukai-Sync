## Pressure

Ori's Pressure completed its crossing; what remains of it is appetite. The hunger is infinite, structural, insatiable: consumption of nothing specific, for no purpose, because consuming is what there is now. It behaves like weather inside a person: longer periods of hunger and direction, punctuated by coherent windows that are cleaner when Ori has been recently fed. That does not mean satisfaction, only that the hunger has been managed low enough for other things to be briefly present alongside it. What shows in those windows: intact memories, full knowledge of the timeline, method, and person responsible, plus a silence about what Jakar's consistency might mean that is the loudest thing Ori ever communicates.

## Link to Ability

Ori no longer holds a Type, Class, or signature. Ori is what happens when manufactured desire crosses every threshold: the specific desire of someone obsessed with Jakar Offei, accelerated over three months by the one person who knew exactly where it went, converted at transformation into **Gluttony**, an appetite for Luz that cannot be satisfied because it was never meant to be an appetite. It was desire with nowhere left to go. The Warden link exists because Jakar made himself the direct cause at maximum realness: permanent awareness, communication, and directional influence that cannot be fully refused. The will remains. That is the entire question.

## Backstory

Ori arrived in Jakar's social environment through northern-district geography (adjacent buildings, overlapping schedules, nearly a year of ordinary proximity) and developed an obsession already moving toward something catastrophic on its own. Jakar watched the Pressure building from the outside, calculated the difference between a natural threshold (complete transformation, connection lost forever) and caused transformation (Warden link, coherent windows, some version of Ori still present), and chose to be the cause rather than the bystander. Three months, methodical, with full awareness of endpoint and cost. Ori did not transform accidentally. Ori was constructed, the desired outcome of a deliberate process by a man who needed confirmation the process was reliable.

## Design

Design personhood flickering through predation: a silhouette that should read wrong in stillness, too quiet, oriented toward sources of Luz the way a compass orients north, broken at intervals by the coherent windows, when posture remembers itself, eyes focus, and someone is briefly home. Motifs: hunger without mouth or jaw (consumption rendered as absence, things thinning near them); the chain and schedule as visual ritual (feeding times kept with ceremonial precision). Recently-fed clarity versus unfed fog should be legible at a glance: sharper lines and deliberate movement after feeding; drift, fragmentation, reach when not. Whatever survived of who they were should live entirely in the windows, a half-smile at Jakar's expense, an argument started and abandoned mid-sentence.

# Full Detail

## What Ori Is

Ori is the first Kukai [[Jakar Offei]] deliberately created. The hunger that defines Ori now (infinite, structural, insatiable consumption of Luz) was originally desire: the specific desire of someone in proximity to Jakar for nearly a year, whose obsession was already moving toward something catastrophic on its own, and whose Pressure was then methodically accelerated over three months by someone who knew exactly where it was going. Ori did not transform accidentally. Ori was constructed. The transformation was the desired outcome of a deliberate process by a person who needed confirmation that the process was reliable.

## Sentience

Fully aware, which is the detail that matters. The memories are intact; knowledge of what happened (timeline, method, person responsible) is accessible in the coherent windows between longer periods of hunger and direction. During these windows Ori does not describe the transformation as something done to them. When Ori speaks, it is the language of completion, as if what happened was the correct expression of what was already there. Whether that is accurate assessment or the most disturbing possible expression of how completely Jakar's manipulation reached into Ori's sense of self is a question the story can hold without resolving quickly.

## The Gluttony

Infinite consumption of nothing in particular, for no purpose, as the structural condition of existence now. The desire that was manufactured and amplified past every threshold became, in transformation, an appetite that cannot be satisfied. Ori consumes Luz without taste or fulfilment involved, because consuming is what there is now. The coherent windows are cleaner when Ori has been recently fed; that does not mean satisfaction, only that the hunger has been managed to a level where other things can briefly be present alongside it.

## The Connection to Jakar

Ori sits under Jakar's Warden application, the causal connection letting him direct every Kukai whose transformation he caused. Ori was the first, so the connection has had longest to settle. Jakar maintains Ori's management, the Luz chain and the feeding schedule, with more care and consistency than anything else he does. He has not examined this closely, applying his usual clinical efficiency and noticing only that the efficiency does not feel the same. Ori notices this too and says nothing about it during the coherent windows, discussing other things instead. The silence about what Jakar's consistency might mean is the loudest thing Ori ever communicates.
