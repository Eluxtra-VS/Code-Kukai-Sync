## Pressure

Niko's Pressure is a gap that moves. Being good at most things never produced satisfaction in a ranking culture, only continuous awareness of whoever stood slightly ahead and specific rage that the gap exists at all. The gap travels when he does, which is why attainment never touches it: it was never about the things. It behaves as compulsion, every extraction an act of refusing to sit with his own insufficiency. It shows in him as restlessness toward the next strongest signature, divided focus when he expresses too much of what he carries, and an interior increasingly like a crowded room where the furniture has opinions and the owner cannot remember which pieces were his.

## Link to Ability

Class follows deepest desire, and Niko's deepest desire is the erasure of other people's having, so his Weaver Veil expresses as **Rapt**: hearing someone name their signature within his perceptual range lets him extract a permanent copy of that Rei configuration and integrate its fundamental affinity as his own operating capacity. The Thread being ears is exact: a signature name spoken aloud is the most complete expression of another person's claimed self, and claiming is what he consumes. The sin guarantees the ability fails: each absorbed ceiling opens awareness of the next person ahead, so Rapt keeps the gap open forever, because the gap is the point.

# Full Detail

## Overview

Niko Juul grew up in the eastern quarter of the Sovereign's Crown, close enough to the centre to see exactly what he lacked, far enough to understand the distance was structural rather than circumstantial. The Crown ranks; it values comparative achievement with the enthusiasm of a place that decided ranking equals meaning. The eastern quarter holds people who are technically citizens and experience daily what that means in practice: near the top without being at it, which on a ranking plate is its own particular form of never being enough. He was good at most things, and that was never the problem. The problem is that comparative environments convert competence into continuous awareness of whoever is slightly better, plus specific rage that the gap exists. He left. The Tenebri found him, or he found them; neither party discusses the direction.

## The Envy

His want aims past objects. He wanted things his entire life and attainment never resolved into contentment, which eventually told him something useful: the wanting was never about the things. It was about the existence of someone else having them. He wants the fact of their having it to stop. No capability closes this, because the gap sits between his capability and the specific person standing slightly ahead at any given moment. The gap moves when he does. That is the structure of the sin: unsatisfiable through attainment, since attainment is not what it is about.

## Ability: Rapt

Thread: Ears. The Weaver Veil condition is always bodily; for Niko it is the act of hearing, specifically hearing someone name or speak of their signature within his perceptual range. A signature is the most personally owned thing in the awakened world: the Class affinity expressed at its deepest level, named and claimed in language. The moment someone speaks their signature's name, their Rei briefly configures in the precise shape of what they have fully claimed, the most complete expression of another person's awakened self. When that configuration passes through his hearing, Rapt fires. Nothing is stolen and the original retains their signature fully. Niko extracts a copy of the Rei configuration, the specific affinity shape, and integrates it permanently. Per person, per signature heard, Niko buffs himself in what that person is distinctly good at: fundamental affinity, not technique. Their ceiling becomes accessible to him as additional operating capacity. A Forge user's signature heard adds Forge capability; a Vigor user's signature heard adds that physical ceiling. Stacking has no confirmed upper limit. He pursues the strongest signatures deliberately. The counter is silence: fighting without naming, never speaking your claim while he is present. Signature names are what people reach for at maximum commitment and presence, and Niko builds his approach around manufacturing exactly that urgency. Tactical problem: his Weaver path already gives whole-body expression of his natural Class, and every absorption compounds it, so a Niko operational for months is not the person who started. Accumulation is the point.

## Multiple Buffs: How They Stack

Each person whose signature he has heard contributes one specific capacity. They do not merge into general power; they stack as a distinct, separately accessible library. Five absorbed signatures means choosing what to express, with focus divided when expressing more than one simultaneously. Low counts are simple and devastating: one or two capabilities atop his baseline. High counts turn selection and focus into the problem rather than availability. Years of operation yield a library.

## The Cost: What Envy Pays

Rapt has no upper limit, but it has a price, and the price is the sin. *(Cost added 2026-08-20.)*

- **The fragments never cohere.** Every absorbed capacity is a foreign configuration, someone else's ceiling, someone else's signature shape. They do not merge; they stack as a library, and a library is not a self. The more he absorbs, the less he can feel his own desire beneath the borrowed ones. At high counts the capacities argue, Forge's construction instinct against Vigor's need to be enough against Veil's need to control conditions, and his interior becomes a crowded room where the furniture has opinions and the owner cannot remember which pieces were his.
- **The gap moves anyway.** Every absorbed capacity closes one gap and opens awareness of the next person who has something he does not. The library never feels complete, because the sin is about the existence of someone else having.
- **Rei cost compounds.** Foreign capacities are inefficient in his body, built for someone else's architecture. Sustained reliance on the library degrades his own Rei coherence and raises his Pressure: unintegrated fragments are unresolved emotion given mechanical form.
- **What suppression deepens:** every extraction refuses sitting with his own insufficiency. Filling himself with others hollows his sense of self, and the hollower he feels, the more urgent the next name becomes. Rapt is an addiction with infinite tolerance and a shrinking user. The ability meant to close the gap permanently is what keeps it open, because the gap is the point.
