## Pressure

Yeshi's Pressure is exhaustion with nowhere to go and no wish to send it anywhere. It began as a lifetime of Gear District noise (machinery, people, obligation, an environment never stopping its demands) and finished as a philosophy: caring about outcomes requires energy, and energy spent on outcomes is energy that could have been spent on nothing, which is the only thing he wants. He doesn't suppress in any dramatic sense; nothing left in him seems worth expressing. The visible indicators are small and constant: stillness, economy of speech, the daily cigarette, which is portable silence rather than self-destruction: the exhale, the pause, the world going slightly soft for a moment.

## Link to Ability

Class follows deepest desire, and Yeshi's deepest desire is rest, a moment of quiet he never once found. His Reiki Veil expresses exactly that: **Calming Fog**, smoke produced through seeded cigar Anchors that condenses into structural forms or diffuses into wide sensory distortion clouds, with skin contact or inhalation as condition. The ability ran his life before it ever fired: smoking kept him functional for decades because it was the closest thing to silence he could carry, and he discovered late that the smoke was never just a habit. The fog is his wanted nothing made weapon, a world softened past the point of demanding anything from him.

## Backstory

He worked the Gear District his entire life and never complained, never expressed; he just wanted a moment of quiet and never found one. His wife died when the district had already worn him down to something thin and functional, and her death finished what years of noise had started. Who she was, how long they had, what he was like before: he does not discuss any of it. The people who knew him before and after noticed the difference, and he let the gap stay open. The withdrawal was quiet: first the outcome of his work stopped mattering, then most outcomes, and then a man with a faction pointed him at something requiring almost nothing, which he found agreeable. His nephew Hitori still checks in across the distance from the Tidal Plate. Yeshi answers, not much, but he answers.

## Design

Design spent energy: slouched permanence, clothes that stopped being chosen and started being worn, ash-grey palette over Gear District workman's drab. The cigarette is the centrepiece prop, lit in tension, exhaled as punctuation, smoke behaviour foreshadowing the ability long before it manifests. Body language is minimal-efficiency: no wasted gesture, eyes half-lidded at everything except silence, which gets his full attention. Motifs: disposable cigars each different (seeded Anchors, distinct configurations), pauses stretching past comfort, structures condensing out of haze. When Calming Fog deploys, the world should visibly lose its urgency (edges softening, sound flattening), his personal philosophy imposed as terrain.

# Full Detail

## Core Personality

Yeshi is still, economical with words, and tired of everything that isn't silence. He worked in the Gear District his entire life, the noise constant from the beginning: machinery, people, obligation, the specific exhaustion of an environment that never stops demanding attention. He did not complain and did not express; he wanted a moment of quiet and never found one. He smokes every day, since he was old enough to. It is the closest thing to portable silence he ever found: the exhale, the pause, the world going slightly soft. The simple reading of Sloth would call him lazy, and the truth is closer to exhaustion that became philosophy. He stopped caring about outcomes long ago because caring costs energy, and energy spent on outcomes is energy that could be spent on nothing, the only thing he actually wants.

## His Wife

His wife died. The Gear District had already worn him down to something thin and functional by then; her death finished what years of noise started. What she was to him specifically (who she was, how long they were together, what he was like before) is not something he discusses. The Tenebri did not ask. People who knew him before and after noticed the difference; he noticed too, and did not try to close the gap. The withdrawal wasn't dramatic, just quiet: he stopped caring about the outcome of his work, then most things, then a man with a faction found him in that state and pointed him at something requiring almost nothing, which he found agreeable. Her death is not the only reason he is who he is, but it was the last reason he had to try, and he let it be.

## His Nephew

Hitori Tanaka lives on the Tidal Plate, Yeshi's sister's son. Yeshi knows the boy is steady the way the Tidal Plate produces steady people: someone others lean on, someone who stays. Hitori has known his uncle is struggling since the death and checks in when he can across the distance; Yeshi answers, not much, but he answers. Hitori does not know his uncle is Tenebri. He will find out. By then, Yeshi will already have been part of things Hitori cannot reconcile with the man who answered his messages.

## Ability: Calming Fog

See [[Calming Fog]] for full mechanics. Yeshi produces smoke through seeded cigar Anchors, different cigars each time, disposable, each carrying a distinct smoke configuration. The smoke condenses into structural forms or diffuses into wide sensory distortion clouds; the Veil condition is skin contact or inhalation. The thing that kept him functional for decades is the same thing the ability runs on. He found out late that the smoke was never just a habit.

## The Sloth Reading

The sin is a decision, made after long effort producing nothing worth its cost, that nothing is worth the cost. He is the endpoint of a person who needed rest, was never given it, eventually stopped asking, and lost the last thing that made asking feel worth trying. What looks like apathy is structural withdrawal from a world that used him up and called it purpose. The Tenebri did not recruit him. They found someone who had already decided and pointed him somewhere. He found that agreeable.
