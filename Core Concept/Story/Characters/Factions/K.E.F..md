# K.E.F. — Kukai Extermination Force

## Short Definition

The **Kukai Extermination Force** is a government-sanctioned organisation operating under the authority of the [[Sovereign's Crown]]. Its official purpose is the identification, containment, and elimination of Kukai threats. Its actual purpose is the maintenance of public ignorance — ensuring that Kukai, Reiva, the full scope of the awakened world, and the Crown's deliberate policy decisions about all of it remain unknown to the general population.

---

## Structure and Function

Recruits Kyumin and awakened individuals with combat capability and institutional loyalty. Operates in small cells; members rarely know the full scope of operations outside their unit. Answers to the Sovereign's Crown.

The secrecy mandate is the organisation's most important function. A contained Kukai incident can be explained away. One that becomes public produces questions the Crown does not want answered — specifically, why the Tidal Plate has been left consistently underprotected despite its disproportionately high Kyumin population, and whether that underprotection is deliberate.

It is deliberate. The K.E.F. knows this. Most of its members do not know it consciously.

---

## The Order — How Ku and Sora Left

[[Dante Vargas]] was a K.E.F. consultant during a period when his investigative skills were useful to the organisation. In the course of standard consultancy work, he found documentation — records proving the Crown had made a deliberate policy decision to leave the Tidal Plate underprotected. Not negligence. A decision. In writing.

He filed his finding with the K.E.F. as part of his professional report. He finds things. That is his job.

The K.E.F. classified the finding immediately. They also identified that a civilian on the Tidal Plate — an ordinary person, no classified access, no awakened ability — had independently reached the same conclusion through careful observation of publicly visible patterns. The disproportionate Kyumin population. The inadequate protective response. The gap between what the Crown provided to other plates and what it provided to the Tidal Plate.

The K.E.F. issued an elimination order. Not for a Kukai. Not for a supernatural threat. For a civilian. For someone who knew a political truth the Crown did not want known.

**Ku refused immediately.** Before the reasoning was finished. This was not the organisation he had signed up for. A Kukai Extermination Force ordering the death of a civilian for knowing an inconvenient political fact was not a category of action he had any framework to accommodate. He said no. He understood what that meant and said no anyway.

**Sora tried to follow through.** Not because she believed the order was right — she knew it wasn't. But refusing meant deciding the institution she had worked within was irredeemably wrong, which meant owning everything she had done while she was part of it. The guilt she had been managing carefully would have nowhere to go except directly toward herself. So she tried.

She reached the civilian. She was in the position to carry it out. She couldn't do it. The civilian survived, was terrified, and disappeared. The order went uncompleted. The K.E.F. knows by whom.

What Sora carries from this: the self-blame does not attach to failure. It attaches to having tried. The civilian is alive — but the path to that outcome runs through a moment where she was there, with the order, and she reached. She cannot frame this as a refusal. Ku refused. She got there. She has never told Ku this.

Both departed — Ku loudly and immediately, Sora through a door she had been moving toward for longer than she had named it. Ku's departure gave her the specific moment where the movement became action.

## The Pursuit of Kaito's Group

Both Ku and Sora are institutional liabilities before the Kaito situation begins. The K.E.F. does not pursue immediately — visible pursuit would draw attention to why they left. When Ku makes contact with Kaito's group and triggers a review, the pre-existing calculation updates. The pursuit isn't a new problem. It is the same institutional logic reaching a new conclusion about a new set of names.

What the K.E.F. most wants suppressed: documentation connecting them, the Crown, and a deliberate policy of exposing the Tidal Plate. Kaito's group finding that documentation is the thing that activates full pursuit — not that they know Kukai exist, but that they know the government knew, decided, and kept deciding.

## What They Are Actually Protecting

The specific piece of information the K.E.F. would eliminate people to suppress: the Tidal Plate's population has been left deliberately underprotected. The decision is in writing. At a classification level that Ku's service gave him proximity to. The civilian who independently found it got there through observation alone.
