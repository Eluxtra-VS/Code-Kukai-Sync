# Factions

Index of the two major factions in Code: Kukai.

For individual faction-character Pressure, use [[Character Pressure Matrix]].

## Files

- [[Tenebri]] — the villain faction; seven members each embodying an unresolved sin; operates from The Calamity; could conquer any plate single-handedly; dark mirror of the original Seven.
- [[K.E.F.]] — Kukai Extermination Force; government-sanctioned by the Sovereign's Crown; official mission = contain Kukai; real mission = suppress public knowledge of Rei, Reiva, and the Crown's deliberate policy of leaving the Tidal Plate underprotected. Pursues Kaito's group after Ku makes unsanctioned contact.
