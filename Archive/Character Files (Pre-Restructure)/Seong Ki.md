# Seong Ki

## Basic Information

- **Location:** [[Gear District]]
- **Path:** [[Reiki]]
- **Class:** Pulse
- **Signature:** Pure Extraction
- **Primary Anchor:** Extractor

---

## Core Personality

Silly. Hyperactive. Sporadic.

Seong loves having fun, especially during battle. The fight is not just violence to him; it is motion, exchange, improvisation, and the feeling of being fully alive in the moment.

He grew up bored and boxed in. His answer to Pressure became movement, jokes, risk, and play. If he can make the moment feel alive, the Pressure does not get to sit still long enough to rot.

---

## Pressure Profile

**Type:** Expressive and explosive.

Seong accumulates [[Pressure]] quickly, but he also vents it quickly. He does not like dwelling on emotion until it hardens. He tries to make things move: laugh, fight, joke, run, escalate, improvise.

His Pressure resolution is **fun and freedom**, not self-destruction.

The Extractor matters because it opens more ways to move and fight. It lets him play at a higher ceiling, take stranger angles, and turn combat into the kind of exchange where he feels most free.

---

## Relationship to the Extractor

Seong's Extractor is his Anchor and his mouthpiece.

He is not using it as self-harm. He is using it as a key: a way to unlock more capability, more speed, more danger, more play, more freedom to fight how he wants.

The risk is still real. Overdrive is dangerous. Forced Overdrive is ethically ugly. But Seong's emotional logic is not "pain makes feelings go away." It is "movement keeps me alive."

This distinction matters.

---

## Signature Ability - Pure Extraction

**Affinity:** Pulse.

Pure Extraction lets Seong fire specialised Luz/Kuro bullets through his Extractor Anchor. These bullets can carry different effects depending on the ratio and emotional state.

His favourite application is forcing others toward Overdrive because it makes the fight bigger, faster, and more alive. That does not make it harmless. Seong can be funny and still be dangerous; playful and still reckless.

---

## Relationship to Reiva

- Extremely high compatibility with his [[Reiva]].
- Low Kukai risk because he is expressive and rarely lets Pressure sit unprocessed.
- His danger is not suppression. His danger is escalation: turning every feeling into motion before asking whether motion is what the situation needs.
