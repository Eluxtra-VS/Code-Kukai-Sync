# Dante Vargas

## Basic Information

- **Organisation:** Tenebri
- **Sin:** Wrath
- **Path:** Akarui
- **Class:** Veil
- **Signature:** Judgement
- **Former affiliation:** K.E.F. consultant / adjacent investigator

---

## Core Personality

Precise. Deliberate. Utterly certain.

Dante does not perform certainty. He has it. He spent long enough watching people carry harm they would not acknowledge, dodge accountability they had earned, and reframe damage they caused as something that simply happened.

He is not angry in the explosive way Wrath often presents. He is cold. The anger is there, but it has been refined into something more functional than rage.

That is what makes him dangerous: not the person who loses control, but the person who calmly believes he is the right one to determine what harm deserves.

---

## The Detective

Dante was a detective long enough that the work shaped him. He found things people buried, connected facts people preferred to keep separate, and produced evidence for outcomes institutions preferred to leave unresolved.

He believed in proof and consequence. What the structure delivered over years - people walking free because they had resources, harm unanswered because it was inconvenient - refined his belief into something sharper and more personal.

He did not lose faith in accountability. He lost faith in whether institutions were capable of it.

---

## The K.E.F.

Dante worked alongside the K.E.F., not within it. He was a consultant: useful, close enough to provide value, far enough away to deny responsibility.

During consultancy work, he found documentation proving the Crown's deliberate neglect of the Tidal Plate. He filed it as evidence. The K.E.F. classified it, then issued the order that broke Ku and Sora away from the organisation.

From Dante's perspective, he was doing what evidence required. Whether he understood what the K.E.F. would do with that evidence is the slow-burn question.

---

## Thematic Role

Dante is not Kaito's sin mirror anymore. Kaito's mirror is inverted Pride.

Dante still matters to Kaito because he represents a different danger: the fantasy that if you can name harm precisely enough, you are allowed to become the consequence.

For Kaito, who is learning to stop treating weakness as something that must be corrected, Dante is a warning about another false solution: turning pain into a perfect verdict.

---

## Signature Ability - Judgement

See [[Judgement]] for full mechanics.

Dante can perceive intentional harm a person caused while carrying present guilt, then return that harm in a worse, contextually appropriate form.

The condition is not "has ever hurt someone." It is:

- Intentional harm.
- Present guilt.

This means Dante does not punish everyone. He punishes the guilty who still carry the shape of what they did. His arc is about whether seeing guilt clearly makes him fit to sentence it.
