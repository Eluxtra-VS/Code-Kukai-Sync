# Yeshi Tanaka

## Core Personality

Still. Economical. Perpetually tired of everything that is not silence.

Yeshi worked in the Gear District his entire life. The noise was constant from the beginning — machinery, people, obligation, the specific exhaustion of an environment that never stops demanding your attention. He did not complain. He did not express. He just wanted a moment of quiet and never found one.

He smokes every day. He has since he was old enough to. It is not self-destruction — it is the closest thing to silence he found that was portable. The exhale. The pause. The way the world goes slightly soft for a moment.

He is not lazy in the way Sloth implies as a simple reading. He is exhausted in a way that became a philosophy. He stopped caring about outcomes a long time ago because caring about outcomes requires energy, and energy spent on outcomes is energy that could have been spent on nothing, which is the only thing he actually wants.

---

## His Wife

Yeshi's wife died. The Gear District had already worn him down to something thin and functional by the time it happened. Her death finished what years of noise had started.

What she was to him specifically — who she was, how long they were together, what he was like before — is not something he discusses. The Tenebri did not ask. The people who knew him before and after noticed the difference. He noticed it too. He did not try to close the gap.

The withdrawal that followed was not dramatic. It was very quiet. He stopped caring about the outcome of his work. Then he stopped caring about the outcome of most things. Then a man with a faction found him in that state and pointed him at something that required almost nothing of him, and he found that agreeable.

His wife's death is not the only reason Yeshi is who he is. But it is the last reason he had to try, and he let it be.

---

## His Nephew

Hitori Tanaka lives on the Tidal Plate. Yeshi's sister's son. Yeshi knows the boy is steady in the way the Tidal Plate produces steady people — someone the people around him lean on, someone who stays.

Hitori knows his uncle is struggling. He has known since the death. He checks in when he can across the distance. Yeshi answers. He does not say much, but he answers.

Hitori does not know his uncle is Tenebri. He will find out. By the time he does, Yeshi will already have been part of things Hitori cannot reconcile with the man who answered his messages.

## Ability — Calming Fog

See [[Calming Fog]] for full mechanics.

Yeshi produces smoke through seeded cigar Anchors — different cigars each time, disposable, each carrying a distinct smoke configuration. The smoke can condense into structural forms or diffuse into wide sensory distortion clouds. The Veil condition is skin contact or inhalation.

The thing that kept him functional for decades is the same thing the ability runs on. He found out late that the smoke was never just a habit.

---

## The Sloth Reading

Yeshi's sin is not absence of effort. It is the decision — made after a long time of effort producing nothing worth the cost — that nothing is worth the cost.

He is the endpoint of a person who needed rest and was never given it, who eventually stopped asking, and who lost the last thing that made the asking feel worth trying.

What looks like apathy is a structural withdrawal from a world that used him up and called it purpose. The Tenebri did not recruit him. They found someone who had already decided, and pointed him somewhere.

He found that agreeable.
