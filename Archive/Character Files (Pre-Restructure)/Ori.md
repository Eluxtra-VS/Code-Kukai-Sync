# Ori

## Basic Information

- **Organisation:** Tenebri — the sin of Gluttony
- **Status:** Kukai — sentient, functional, connected to Jakar Offei
- **Origin:** Northern Gear District
- **Formerly:** A person. Name not used.

---

## What Ori Is

Ori is the first Kukai Jakar Offei deliberately created.

The hunger that defines Ori now — the infinite, structural, insatiable consumption of Luz — was originally desire. The specific desire of someone who was in proximity to Jakar Offei for nearly a year, who developed an obsession that was already moving toward something catastrophic on its own, and whose Pressure was then methodically accelerated over three months by someone who knew exactly where it was going.

Ori did not transform accidentally. Ori was constructed. The transformation was the desired outcome of a deliberate process by a person who needed confirmation that the process was reliable.

---

## Sentience

Ori is fully aware. This is the specific detail that matters.

The memories are intact. The knowledge of what happened — the timeline, the method, the person responsible — is accessible in the coherent windows that exist between longer periods of hunger and direction. During these windows, Ori does not describe the transformation as something done to them. The language Ori uses, when Ori speaks, is the language of completion. As if what happened was the correct expression of what was already there.

Whether this is an accurate assessment or the most disturbing possible expression of how completely Jakar's manipulation reached into Ori's sense of self is a question the story can hold without resolving quickly.

---

## The Gluttony

Ori's sin is infinite consumption — not of anything specific, not for any purpose, but as the structural condition of existence now. The desire that was manufactured and amplified past every threshold became, in transformation, an appetite that cannot be satisfied. Ori consumes Luz. Not because it tastes like anything. Not because it fulfils anything. Because consuming is what there is now.

The coherent windows are cleaner when Ori has been recently fed. This does not mean satisfaction. It means the hunger has been managed to a level where other things can briefly be present alongside it.

---

## The Connection to Jakar

Ori is under Jakar's Warden signature — the causal connection that allows Jakar to direct every Kukai whose transformation he caused. Ori was the first, which means the connection has had the longest to settle.

Jakar maintains Ori's management — the Luz chain, the feeding schedule — with more care and consistency than anything else he does. He has not examined this closely. He applies the same clinical efficiency to Ori's maintenance that he applies to everything, and notices that the efficiency does not feel the same.

Ori notices this too. Ori does not say anything about it during the coherent windows. Ori discusses other things. The silence about what Jakar's consistency might mean is the loudest thing Ori ever communicates.
